## Material findings and narrow corrections

**F1 — Energy fraction is not group speed on this lattice.** #7898 T4/E2 and #7905 T1.4, T3 and A4/C1 identify `1-m²/E²` with `(v/c_L)²`, or infer zero hop energy from zero velocity. The exact dispersion gives `v_a/c_L=sin(p_a)/E`; hence actual speed squared is `sum sin²(p_a)/E²`, while the hop fraction is `sum(2-2cos p_a)/E²`. They coincide only to leading Dirac order. Independent exact counterexample: at transverse `p_y=pi/3` and `pi/2`, both with `m=sqrt(2)`, actual `v_y/c_L=1/2` in both states, but the proposed `(2,1)` acceleration is `-16/3` and `-6`. Even equal mass and velocity do not fix the lattice trajectory. Likewise a band-edge state can have zero group velocity and nonzero hop energy. Preserve the exact momentum formula, rename the hop fraction, restrict rest statements to the Dirac rest point/limit, require `E!=0` for divided formulas, and remove exact finite-lattice equivalence/geodesic claims. The asserted longitudinal sign threshold `v/c=1/sqrt(2)` also requires its continuum qualification.

**F2 — An acceleration ratio is not finite-momentum ray curvature or a full quantum/geodesic theorem.** #7898 T3.4 explicitly equates `a_perp/v²` to a momentum-independent Newtonian light curvature, while its packets have different massless speeds. At the three chosen momenta the extra factors are 1.03957, 1.17157, 1.44646. The historical ratios near 1 and 2 are useful **acceleration-coefficient comparisons**. Physical light bending requires the ray-speed normalization and a specified continuum/physical map. The Hamilton equations of the scalar band symbol give the stated momentum formulas; they do not, without a band/adiabatic approximation, prove identical exact dynamics of every finite quantum packet. #7905's continuum `(2,1)` comparison can remain conditional on the stipulated metric and low-momentum approximation.
