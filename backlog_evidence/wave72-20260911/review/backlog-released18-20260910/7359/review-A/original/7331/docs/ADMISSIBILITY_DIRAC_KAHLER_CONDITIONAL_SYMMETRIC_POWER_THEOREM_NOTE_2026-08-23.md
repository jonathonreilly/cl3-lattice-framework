## N1 — THE SYM^N LEMMA, complex-corrected and built rather than cited

### The construction

Let the one-particle form be Hermitian on a two-dimensional span with
`p = <u,u>`, `q = <v,v>` real and `r = <u,v>` complex. For a proper quasi-free
functional the `n`-particle reflected Gram on symmetric monomials is the
PERMANENT of the matrix of one-particle overlaps — every permutation with sign
`+1`.

**THE RUNNER SUMS OVER ALL PERMUTATIONS EXPLICITLY** at `n = 2, 3, 4` on the two
monomials `{u^n, u^(n-1) v}`, and gets

- `A = n! p^n`
- `B = n! p^(n-1) r`
- `D = (n-1)! [ p^(n-1) q + (n-1) p^(n-2) |r|^2 ]`

so the `2x2` minor determinant is exactly

**det = n!(n-1)! p^(2n-2) (pq - |r|^2)**

at every order tested. That is **THE SYM^N LEMMA**, and it is established by
**direct permanent construction**, not by citation of a symmetric-power identity.

### The correction the checker made, and why it is not cosmetic

**THE SOLVE'S r^2 IS CORRECTED TO |r|^2.** The real-symmetric specialization
writes `r^2`; the complex scope requires `|r|^2 = r conj(r)`. The runner measures
that these are DIFFERENT symbolic objects, so this is a correction and not a
notation variant. **The sign conclusion survives it unchanged.**

**WITH p > 0 > q THE DETERMINANT IS STRICTLY NEGATIVE FOR EVERY n >= 2**, because
`pq < 0` and `|r|^2 >= 0` and `n!(n-1)! p^(2n-2) > 0`. The runner asserts this
symbolically, with `p` a positive symbol and `q` strictly negative, rather than by
evaluating at sample points.

**TENSOR RENORMALIZATION CANNOT CHANGE THE SIGN.** Passing from raw monomials to
divided or orthonormal symmetric tensors is **a positive congruence** — a
positive diagonal congruence `D M D` multiplies the `2x2` determinant by
`(d_1 d_2)^2 > 0` — so it **cannot change the sign** or the inherited mixed
inertia. That is measured symbolically on a generic Hermitian `2x2`.
