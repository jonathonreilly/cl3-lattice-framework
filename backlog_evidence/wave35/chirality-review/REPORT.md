**F1 — #7973 T3/T4: support is misstated and conflated with formation (note lines 129–159).** A fully recorded neighbourhood need not have six direction records: five `I` records and one `P(m)` are fully recorded and produce a binary directional menu. The statement must say all six neighbours are direction records. More seriously, `L_HYB` admits both signs, so `P(-m)` is supported against six `P(m)` neighbours although its projection on their sum is negative. `independent_controls.json` reproduces both directly through the original functions. Thus the advertised support iff positive-alignment/XY-fixed-point characterization is false; the abundant 1/3-direction branches also need separate handling. Static compatibility at the final neighbourhood does not follow from Record permanence or characterize sequential formation. Neither the dimer scans nor the Gibbs census establish the claimed densest winding configuration. State the actual static subset and tested templates; specify pins/core and boundary conditions rather than claiming the law selects the vortex.

**F2 — #7973 T5: fair local marginals do not supply independent resampling or destruction of winding (lines 165–177, 263).** The invariant-fibre calculation gives the stated one-site half/half law conditional on its supplied grading. It does not give the joint probability `2^-N^2`, nor a permanent-record re-registration process. Four fair signs have all-positive probability 1/16 under independence and 1/2 under a shared fair coin. The latter preserves the entire winding profile up to a global sign in each realization. A single deterministic half-flip texture with no returned core modes is therefore not a theorem that a coin-signed field has no string mode. Supply an explicit external product resampling model, or restrict to the local odds and declared deterministic textures. Likewise the scalar mean-field iteration is a supplied closure, not the original stochastic nearest-neighbour dynamics.
