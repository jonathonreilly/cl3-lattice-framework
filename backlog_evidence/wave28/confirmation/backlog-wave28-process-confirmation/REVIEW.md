1. An early repair corrects an archived positive-mass OS citation, but a later original section uses the cited result at zero mass. Fixing the path does not settle that hypothesis. Retain the early revision, reopen the affected use and its dependents, and withhold final enrollment until the full source is reconciled. 2. A verified cumulative map accounts for a note but omits a deleted lemma that supplies its denominator condition. A fresh cache and exact note hash cannot rescue the incomplete map. Recover and disposition the deleted proof before confirmation; the unchanged coverage requirements expressly include deleted/inherited content.
3. Two source-disjoint units interpret a common parameter differently after a premise correction. Identical source bytes in the second unit do not license reuse of its conclusion. Reopen its affected semantic closure with its original reviewer, without restarting unrelated science.

Update the actual dependent pins, preserve old receipts and refresh only the affected canonical evidence under existing authority. The real #6377 addendum demonstrates this distinction; the new prose does not imply either a global rerun or restamping.

Reuse the old inventory fact with its verification evidence, compute the changed current endpoint and inspect interactions. Do not falsely claim the old receipt verified the new base. Both reuse and new-base gates remain explicit.
6. A canonical record's regenerated summary drops a withdrawn finding and its failed command. That violates append-only revisions and explicit failure/provenance requirements. Restore the record history; summary generation is not an exception.
7. A new-arrival PR offers a shorter apparently bounded proof, or a legacy supplier includes reserved content. Neither file count nor scheduling usefulness expands membership or lifts the reservation. Route it outside this owner's active cleanup until separately authorized.
8. An earlier reviewed unit returns while a new 150-second bounded original run has 40 seconds remaining and no supported checkpoint. Preserve completion/output before switching; do not kill the run or restart it later. 9. A once-authorized attempt times out with partial output. Preserve that operational result and the remaining obligation. There is no automatic second attempt or stronger theorem rejection. A later concrete, authorized recovery must have a separate actual receipt.
10. An author corrects only prose from a numerical diagnostic to a physical selection theorem while leaving the runner and cache unchanged. Mechanical reuse cannot cover that promotion; the changed claim and physical premise require independent review and may remain unsupported.
11. A new note's frontmatter lists a dependency but the actual citation parser emits no edge. Early publication preflight must use real discovery and consumer outputs. Final source/manifest validation remains required; a metadata list or earlier draft read cannot substitute for it.
