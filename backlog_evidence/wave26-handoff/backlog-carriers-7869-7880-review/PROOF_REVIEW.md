## Aliasing, precision and stochastic scope

When slots are identified, V is the declared diagonal isometry. For a one-site pin `(a,b)`, the off-diagonal coordinates of its repeated product are ab, so it lies in the image of V exactly when ab=0. This elementary identity is valid. The later numerical implementation uses `A=V†Pi_KV` and replaces A by the projector Q onto its eigenvalue-one subspace. Since `0<=A<=I`, `ker(I-A)=ran(Q)`; positive sums have the same common zero space. Their nonzero spectra and residual numbers are not identical. Moreover `I-A <= I-Q`, so a genuinely controlled small H_Q residual bounds H_A from above. The source should explain this actual substitution and numerical spectral threshold instead of presenting it as literal computation of the compressed H_A.

The nine alternating-projection runs and two extracted vectors are retained as fixed-seed finite numerical data.
