## Two specified geometric and count formulas

subgroup H whose common fixed space is a line, let N be its perpendicular plane
and define

    L(H)=(1/|H|) sum_(h in H, h!=I) 1/det((I-h)|N),

only where every denominator is nonzero. If the fixed space is not a line,
the formula is undefined under this convention. For cyclic cubic subgroups of

included. This collision does not identify either formula with a physical scalar.
