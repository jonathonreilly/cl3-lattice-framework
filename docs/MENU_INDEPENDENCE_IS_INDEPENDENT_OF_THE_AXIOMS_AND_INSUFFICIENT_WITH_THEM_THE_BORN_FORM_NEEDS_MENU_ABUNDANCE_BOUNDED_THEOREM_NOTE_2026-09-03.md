## Typed compiler and finite result

L_MENU takes six slots, each blank or one of the six signed unit directions.
Let k be the occupied count and V the value sum. It emits an indexed effect
menu: k=0 gives (I); V=0,k>0 gives (I/3,2I/3); V nonzero and odd k gives
(P(Vhat),P(-Vhat)); V nonzero and even k gives
(2P(Vhat)/3,2P(-Vhat)/3,I/3). The output effects and newly formed directions
are not confined to the seven input symbols. No decoding of all possible
post-record outputs into subsequent conditions has been defined. This is a
conditional compiler, not a model of all four axioms on an iterated domain.

The 7^6=117649 inputs produce 166 menus and 331 distinct effect payloads.
The complete key covariance sweep has 2823576 pairs; the deterministic exact
menu-object sample has 48000 pairs. On the declared 3^3 torus sample there
are 291600 translation and 259200 rotation checks. These are the original
finite domains, not all lattice configurations. Four rules—trace/2, uniform,
trace-power q=2 and q=3—normalize on the indexed menus and are covariant there.

Two aligned input values give the mixed ternary; two opposed values give the
coin menu. The shared I/3 has grades respectively (1/3,1/3), (1/3,1/2),
(1/3,1/5), (1/3,1/9). Thus menu functionality can vary while that **supplied
compiler** remains fixed. Independence from the entire physical axiom system
is unestablished. At one fixed condition a comparison across “whatever else
is offered” is vacuous when the menu is already a function of that condition.
Comparisons across conditions can impose a new mathematical constraint.
