## 1. Exact Semantic And Physical Supports

Each event retains the three support levels from Block89:


operators share an input.

## 3. Why The Block89 Collision Disappears

then into output Record sites. Its net action therefore includes routed
background permutations, so two compact words with disjoint abstract
instruments need not commute.

The pair-aware compiler instead restores every nonsemantic factor after each
logical gate. Its archive paths exchange only their intended semantic
endpoints and restore their interiors. Therefore

```text

supp(U_tilde_f) = L_f.
```

For the hostile pair, `L_e cap L_f` is empty, so


This identity is not limited to the displayed ready state. The runner's
invariant basis input is an adversarial executable witness; operator support
is the proof.

Conditional on the guarded joint corridor being Record-free at transaction
entry, each word avoids the other event's write sites entirely, so either
event may commit first. The second word never transiently alters the Records
formed by the first. Primitive-step Record permanence therefore holds on this
explicit formation domain. It is not an unconditional property of the word
on a lattice carrying arbitrary ambient Records.
