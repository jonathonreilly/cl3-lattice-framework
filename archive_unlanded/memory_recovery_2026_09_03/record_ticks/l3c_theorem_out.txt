STEP 3: which pre-record states DO reproduce their own Born diagonal under a tick rule?
        A tick rule leaves the finished-set odds equal to the Born diagonal of psi0 for every
        schedule iff every gap acts trivially on the conditioned state.  Two exact checks:
  (1) tau = 0 (no dynamics): trivially yes, for any psi0.  This is PR #7858's setting.
  (2) psi0 an eigenstate of H: the FIRST gap acts trivially, but H_R != H, so from the second
      gap on the conditioned state evolves.  Quantified in STEP 1 / STEP 2 above.
  (3) the only states left invariant by every H_R (R nonempty) are the joint eigenvectors of all
      12 single-site-deleted Hamiltonians; the intersection over sites is computed here:
      max ||[H, T_e]|| over the 12 sites = 1.0000 (nonzero: no common eigenbasis)
      the joint kernel of {H_R - H_R'} has dimension 0 -> no nonzero state is left invariant by
      all the post-record Hamiltonians at once.
