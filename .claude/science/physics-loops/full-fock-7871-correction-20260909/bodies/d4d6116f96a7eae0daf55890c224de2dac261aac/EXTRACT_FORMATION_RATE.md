## 1. Exposable landed structure

### Cycle 693: record-cell kinematics and readout, not formation

The executable fixture exposes distinct record identities, content labels, and
finite configurations: two records can share content while remaining distinct
records (`Cycle 693:L56-L60`). It also exposes:

- a local content-count representation (`Cycle 693:L63-L68`);
- the exact empty/content-determinacy/finite-additivity compatibility predicate
  (`Cycle 693:L71-L83`);
- singleton-content factorization of every compatible readout
  (`Cycle 693:L229-L249`) and an independent proof that the fixture solution
  space is precisely the two-dimensional content-count span
  (`Cycle 693:L270-L295`);
- a record cell carrying a lattice site and an \(M_2\)-valued content
  (`Cycle 693:L155-L175`);
- one deliberately constructed covariant local availability rule: noncentral
  contents are available for even six-neighbor occupancy, whereas odd
  occupancy admits only scalar matrices (`Cycle 693:L159-L168`);
- an additive example readout obtained by summing the matrix trace of each
  record content (`Cycle 693:L177-L182`).

This gives a useful *shape* for a future census row:

```text
(event identity, candidate site, local-neighbor pattern, content,
 availability/admissibility predicates, readout)
```

It does not give a formation indicator. In particular, the displayed site
family `(3*index,0,0)`, the empty-neighborhood availability, and the trace
readout are choices in a countermodel fixture, not a derived site distribution
or physical intensity (`Cycle 693:L297-L365`). The runner's conclusion is only
singleton-content factorization for each **supplied** additive scalar codomain;
it does not supply a finite content alphabet, complex operational carrier, or
physical event-algebra product (`Cycle 693:L409-L426`). Thus neither
`matrix_trace(content)` nor `available(...)` may silently be renamed a
formation rate.

### Cycle 719: exact event antecedents and sector tests, not formation

Cycle 719 exposes a much more physical event surface. Matter writes a pointer at
the shared site `(-8,-1,1)`, after which the fixed controller acts without a
host direction insertion (`Cycle 719:L36-L52`). The actual controller word is
executed on all six origin-zero first-event branches, agrees exactly with the
algebraic orbit, returns the declared work registers, and has an exact inverse
(`Cycle 719:L84-L100`). Those six branches are therefore the cleanest bounded
universe on which to ask a first formation question.

The following derived or executable predicates can be recorded as possible
formation-law covariates:

- exact matter-endpoint/pointer production and same-chart controller return
  (`Cycle 719:L84-L110`);
- branch identity and packet-output state on the six compiled first events
  (`Cycle 719:L84-L92`);
- token-sector controls: zero-token, adjacent/distant two-token, and offset
  sectors are distinguished from the lawful result (`Cycle 719:L151-L154`);
- a local refusal predicate
  `syndrome := B OR work; act iff A AND NOT syndrome`, with all truth rows and
  invalid live-token rows checked (`Cycle 719:L159-L176`);
- landed mass/contact diagnostics: the one-particle mass residual is zero and
  free/coin/FSWAP/stream/reverse/seam/contact residuals retain their landed
  values (`Cycle 719:L155-L157`);
- exact local deletions that change source, packet, or finalizer behavior
  (`Cycle 719:L139-L150`).

These distinguish lawful from unlawful controller situations and causally
active from inactive circuit elements. They do **not** distinguish formed from
non-formed Records. The history surface is explicitly a reversible packet
chain, not an inaccessible Record or realized history
(`Cycle 719:L112-L135`). Likewise, the mass/contact quantities are verification
residuals, not a landed energy, action, stress, or hazard scalar. The source
inventory says that the numerical tolerance is a test threshold rather than a
law coefficient (`Cycle 719:L313-L323`), and the residual analysis explicitly
separates a permanent readout from an energy/stress/source identification
(`Cycle 719:L303-L305`).

The named `BINDER/ACTUAL/ADMISS/LAW` inputs are also not derived occurrence
predicates: they are supplied acceptance inputs (`Cycle 719:L190-L200`), while
objective actuality/admissibility is open (`Cycle 719:L214-L224`). The local
`A AND NOT (B OR work)` refusal test is a derived *controller-domain* predicate;
it must not be conflated with any of those four supplied acceptance flags or
with Record formation.

Finally, the 130 applications cannot supply a denominator for a rate. They are
circuit ordinals, explicitly not clock, duration, rate, or physical time
(`Cycle 719:L178-L188`; `Cycle 719:L354-L363`). A first cycle can derive an
event-indexed census, but not a physical rate per unit time.

## 2. First bounded cycle: derive a census before fitting a rate

### Declared inputs

The first cycle should declare only:

1. the exact census universe: the six origin-zero first-event branches on which
   the literal Cycle-719 controller was actually executed
   (`Cycle 719:L84-L92`);
2. the existing landed event word, shared pointer site, declared code, and
   controller-domain fixtures, without changing them;
3. a finite list of candidate Record sites associated with each event, with
   site identity kept separate from content as in the Cycle-693 cell surface
   (`Cycle 693:L171-L175`);
4. the owner-fixed meaning of a positive formation witness: a single
   possibility becomes permanently locked at that site, with no inference that
   any candidate must be positive;
5. all supplied conditions—including `BINDER/ACTUAL/ADMISS/LAW`, clean genesis,
   token sector, boundary/ring, and fresh output cells—as visibly supplied
   conditioning data, not formation labels (`Cycle 719:L190-L224`,
   `Cycle 719:L313-L323`).

The cycle must **not** declare which events form, a numerical rate, a
site-distribution law, a time interval, or a content weight.

### Derived outputs

For each of the six events and each declared candidate site, derive an exact row
of antecedents from the landed fixtures: endpoint/pointer change, branch,
content/site data, local token and clean-syndrome truth, refusal result,
packet/finalizer activity, and the available mass/contact diagnostics. Then
seek a physical permanence/locking witness that is not merely the reversible
packet history.

The primary scientific output is the Boolean census

```text
F(event, site) in {0,1}
```

*only if* `F` follows from a physical locking/Record bridge on the fixture. The
cycle must report which one of the following is actually obtained:

- **empty:** no landed lawful event is shown to form a Record;
- **all-on-declared-sites:** every landed lawful event has a positive formation
  witness at its declared site;
- **structured:** formation is a proper subset correlated with a derived local
  predicate;
- **unidentified:** the landed data distinguish lawful events but no physical
  formation witness has yet been derived.

“Unidentified” is a valid close and is the present evidence-supported baseline.
The existing sources derive neither “all” nor “none.” If a nonempty census is
derived, the same cycle may count formations by site, branch, neighbor class,
and content class. Those are event-indexed counts and conditional frequencies,
not yet a physical-time rate.

The 4,096-column endpoint instrument can remain an independent antecedent
anchor (`Cycle 719:L102-L110`), but the census must not claim full joint-event
coverage there: compiled controller execution is established only for the six
first-event branches, and later recurrence uses the algebraic orbit rather than
re-executing the compiled word (`Cycle 719:L112-L118`).

## 3. Precise no-go boundary

The R-eta no-go states that bare Record formation supplies none of a formation
rule, event rate, site distribution, record-production process, time metric,
weight, or physical-observable bridge (`R-eta no-go:L20-L34`). Its exact witness
assigns direct density, cycle angle, record count, unit rate, and arbitrary
positive rate to the same formed records; all assignments remain compatible
with bare formation while disagreeing numerically
(`R-eta no-go:L36-L52`).

A formation-rate route therefore may not:

- infer a formation event, site distribution, rate, or weight from “Records
  form” alone;
- identify “lawful,” “accepted,” “packet produced,” or “history updated” with
  “Record formed” without deriving the W5 physical Record bridge;
- turn the Cycle-719 circuit ordinal into physical time;
- use Cycle-693 additivity, a trace readout, or freely chosen singleton-content
  weights as a physical event intensity;
- choose a rate package because it reproduces `2/9`, the charged-lepton angle,
  or any desired downstream observable;
- recycle an acceptance bit, numerical residual, deletion response, or test
  tolerance as a law coefficient;
- claim that objective admission, formation, permanence, Born weights, or
  source meaning follows from the reversible controller.

What remains permitted is a separate occurrence-lane event-rate theorem fixed
by physical event structure, a physical action/source bridge, or a narrowly
declared approved primitive (`R-eta no-go:L54-L61`,
`R-eta no-go:L102-L112`). The present extraction pursues the first of those
only as an open derivation program. It does not use a future rate theorem to
retroactively select R-eta.

Cycle 719 sharpens the separation. W3 is objective formation/admission, W5 is
the reversible-packet-to-permanent-Record bridge, and W6 is Born weighting and
realized-history selection (`Cycle 719:L262-L277`). No implication is
established from W3 to W5 or W6, or from W5 to W6
(`Cycle 719:L296-L305`). A rate route must preserve those interfaces rather
than collapsing them.

## 5. Stakes for W6 and the weights program

A derived formation census would define the support of the event ensemble:
which physical events contribute Records, at which sites, with which contents
and multiplicities. A later site-distribution and physical-time law could then
turn repeated censuses into a formation point process or intensity. That is the
upstream population on which the weights program can ask for normalized outcome
weights and realized-history selection.

The direction is one-way at this stage:

```text
landed lawful events
  -> objective occurrence/admission (W3)
  -> physical Record formation census/bridge (W5)
  -> event ensemble and site distribution
  -> Born weighting / realized-history selection (W6)
```

This ordering is not an implication theorem between the walls; it is the
dependency discipline for the proposed work. Cycle 719 explicitly says that
occurrence/admission does not set weights and weights do not select the realized
admitted member (`Cycle 719:L296-L299`), and that the minimal axioms separate
permanent Records from weighting/selection (`Cycle 719:L303-L304`). Formation
counts may therefore supply W6 with an ensemble, but neither counts nor rates
derive Born weights. Conversely, W6 must not be used to choose which events
“must have” formed.

## Extraction verdict

The exposable structure is a local `(site, content, neighborhood, additive
readout)` record-cell surface plus an exact six-branch matter-to-pointer-to-
packet event surface with lawful-sector and refusal predicates. There is no
landed formation bit, physical-time metric, or energy/action intensity.
The top route is the compiled first-event formation census, not a fitted rate.
Its bounded scope is the six actually compiled first-event branches, with the
4,096 endpoint columns retained only as an independent antecedent anchor.
The first cycle must derive `empty`, `all`, `structured`, or honestly
`unidentified`; it may not declare the answer from acceptance or reversibility.
A successful census would supply event-ensemble support upstream of W6 while
leaving Born weights and realized-history selection separate.
