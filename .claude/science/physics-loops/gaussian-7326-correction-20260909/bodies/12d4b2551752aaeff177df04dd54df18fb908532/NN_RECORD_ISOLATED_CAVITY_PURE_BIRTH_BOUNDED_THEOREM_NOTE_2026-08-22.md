## 1. State, Content Kernel, And Proposal Process

Let

```text
Omega = ({blank} disjoint_union M_2(C))^(Z^3).          (1)
```

An occupied coordinate is a permanent Record. `blank` is occupancy state, not
a readable scalar value. The content distribution at a forming site is exactly
Block 33's Borel nearest-neighbor kernel `K_eta`: Gaussian seed on an empty or
nongeneric condition, symmetric atomic copying on an incomplete generic
condition, and the two-projector trace read on a complete six-equal generic
shell.

At each site place an independent rate-one Poisson proposal process, continuing
after rejected proposals and after occupation. Give every proposal independent
marks sufficient to sample `K_eta`. Proposals at occupied sites are ignored.
The unit rate fixes only the scale of an **abstract formation parameter**; no
physical duration, clock observable, time metric, or conversion to framework
units is claimed.

For a blank site `x`, define the base predicate `b_x(omega)=1` exactly when
there is a generic `A in M_2(C)` such that:

1. one of the three unoriented pairs `x+e_i,x-e_i` both carries literal content
   `A`;
2. every occupied nearest neighbor of `x` carries literal content `A`; and
3. fewer than six nearest neighbors are occupied.

Define

```text
h_x = b_x 1{no blank y adjacent to x has b_y=1},
c_x = 1{x is blank} (1-h_x).                            (2)
```

At a proposal of `x`, do nothing if `c_x=0`; if `c_x=1`, draw from `K_eta` and
append the realized Record. Equation (2) is the entire site/rate rule.

The equality in `b_x` is literal Record-content equality, not equality of the
decoded `(C,P)` quotient. In particular, `A` and `A+i alpha I` decode to the
same pair but do not form a protected opposite pair unless `alpha=0`. Exact
copy branches create literal equality with conditional probability one, so the
predicate is reachable even though it is topologically brittle.

The full-shell priority is explicit. Six equal generic neighbors violate item
3, so `b_x=0`, `c_x=1`, and Block 33 Rule R writes a projector. A mixed or
nongeneric arrival violates item 2 and likewise releases the site.

The content Law remains nearest-neighbor as required by Admissibility. The

## 2. Borel Graphical Process And Covariance

The base predicate uses occupancy, genericity, literal matrix equality, and a
finite list of neighbor slots. Those are Borel conditions. Equation (2) is
therefore a bounded finite-range Borel rate. Block 33 proves that `K_eta` is a
Borel probability kernel. Standard Borel randomization supplies a measurable
sampler from the proposal marks.

A direct Harris construction is finite in every backward causal cone. A
range-two update sees at most the 25 sites in the `L1` ball of radius two. Fix
a site and parameter horizon `tau`, and let `N_n` count its ordered potential
ancestor paths of length `n`. Then

```text
(25 tau)^n / n!.                                        (3)
```

In particular,
`P(causal depth >= n) <= E[N_n] <= (25 tau)^n/n!`, which tends to zero.
The ancestor graph is locally finite because each bounded time interval has
finitely many proposals at each site and every update has finite range. Thus
König's lemma turns the absence of an infinite path into finiteness of the
whole backward cone. Intersecting over the countable sites and positive
rational horizons gives one probability-one event on which all finite-horizon
cones are finite. Resolving each finite ancestor graph in proposal order gives
a unique measurable pathwise solution. For a stopping time in the graphical
filtration, the countable marked Poisson field after that time is independent
of the past and has its original law; composing it with this measurable
solution map gives the strong Markov property.

Every coordinate changes state at most once. Consequently a finite region
contains at most its number of sites in accepted births: the process is locally
nonexplosive. This is not a globally finite-jump continuous-time Markov chain;
section 4 gives the exact infinite-volume distinction.

Translations preserve relative slots. Proper cubic rotations permute the
three opposite pairs and all six blank-neighbor candidate tests. The iid clock
and mark field is invariant, so the process Law is translation and
proper-cubic covariant. Internal unitary conjugation preserves literal equality
and genericity, and Block 33's Gaussian/content kernel is equivariant, giving
the optional internal-representation covariance as well.

The rate is discontinuous in the natural matrix topology: replacing one member
of an exact pair `A,A` by `A+epsilon I` releases `c_x` for every nonzero
`epsilon`, however small. No Feller property or noise-robust equality theorem is
claimed. A discrete copied-lineage tag is a live successor, not an imported
premise here.

## 3. Finite-Cavity Exit And Sitewise Saturation

The anti-cluster clause removes the coherent-pair rule's finite all-held trap.
If `h_x=1`, item 3 says at least one neighbor `y` is blank. The definition of
`h_x` says every such blank neighbor has `b_y=0`; hence `h_y=0` and `c_y=1`.
Therefore every held blank has an adjacent eligible blank.

In any nonempty finite blank component, at least one site consequently has
rate one. The next accepted birth occurs almost surely and strictly decreases
the blank count. Induction gives finite-component occupancy in finite abstract
formation time almost surely. Both runners exhaust all 127 nonempty blank
patterns of a seven-site cross in a fixed payload sea as a finite hostile
fixture; the proof is the preceding general implication, not that enumeration.

Recurring clocks sharpen the statement on the infinite lattice to **sitewise
saturation**. Each `c_x` depends on a finite radius-two set, and every coordinate
in that set changes at most once. Thus the local condition at `x` eventually
stabilizes. Suppose `x` stayed blank forever.

- If its stabilized rate were one, a later recurring proposal would fill it
  almost surely.
- Its stabilized rate must therefore be zero. Then `x` is held and has a blank
  neighbor `y` with `b_y=0`. Every nearest neighbor of `y` lies in `B_2(x)`, so
  after `x`'s radius-two stabilization the predicate `b_y=0` persists. Hence
  `h_y=0` and `c_y=1` regardless of the farther candidate tests that could
  otherwise enter `h_y`; moreover `y` remains blank on this event. A later
  proposal therefore fills `y` almost surely, again a contradiction.

For probability bookkeeping, the random finite stabilization time is bounded
by some deterministic integer `m` on one member of a countable union. Each
corresponding forever-blank event is contained either in no proposal at `x`
after `m`, or, for one of six choices of `y`, no proposal at `y` after `m`.
Every such event has probability zero. Hence each fixed site has a finite
formation time almost surely. Countability of `Z^3` makes this simultaneous for
all sites with probability one. From the globally blank initial state, there
is nevertheless almost surely no finite parameter time at which the whole
infinite lattice is complete. For each fixed positive rational `q`, the
independent events that a site's proposal clock has not rung by `q` have
probability `exp(-q)>0`, so Borel--Cantelli leaves infinitely many such blank
sites. Intersect over `q`; for any finite `tau`, choose rational `q>tau`.

Sitewise saturation is occupancy liveness, not compiler correctness. It is
also a resource warning: every finite region eventually has no blank site left.
The process supplies neither erasure nor Record reuse, both forbidden by
permanence, nor an expanding apparatus protocol that transports fresh capacity.
