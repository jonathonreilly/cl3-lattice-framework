## Theorems (all exact; every number in the control's output)
**G1 (the formation law is Gaussian with precision `L_σ† D L_σ`; proved).** The product of the conditional densities `Π_k (P_kk/π) exp(−P_kk |z_k + Σ_{y∈A_k} (P_ky/P_kk) z_y|^2) = π^{−N} (Π_k P_kk) exp(−z† L_σ† D L_σ z)`, so `μ_σ` is the complex Gaussian with precision `P_σ = L_σ† D L_σ` and normalizer `Π_k P_kk` (order-independent), while the static normalizer is `det P`. Executed: `P_σ` from the definition on every order of the plaquette, the path and the `2×3` rectangle.

**G2 (the recorded-set class; proved).** `P_σ` depends on `σ` only through the recorded sets `{A_k}`: two orders with the same recorded sets give the same Gaussian law. Hence (block 05 P1) every monotone order of a rectangle gives one law. Executed: the 24 orders of the plaquette fall into 14 recorded-set classes with `P_σ` constant on each; the 5 monotone orders of `2×3` give one `P_σ`; the snake and the mirror give different ones.

**G3 (the separation, explicit; proved).** `P_σ = P + diag(c_x) + F_σ` with `c_x = Σ_{k: x ∈ A_k} |P_kx|^2/P_kk ≥ 0` and `(F_σ)_{xy} = Σ_{k: x, y ∈ A_k} P_xk P_ky/P_kk` for `x ≠ y` (the fill-in between pairs recorded together by a later site). Consequences: (a) `P_σ = P` iff every `A_k = ∅`, which on a connected graph with at least two sites never happens: the formation law is never the static law; (b) `support(P_σ) = support(P)` iff every `|A_k| ≤ 1` (block 01's Theorem B condition reappears as the sparsity condition), and then `P_σ = P + diag(c)`; (c) with some `|A_k| ≥ 2` the fill-in couples the non-adjacent pairs of `A_k` — on the `2×3` monotone class the pairs `((0,1),(1,0))` and `((0,2),(1,1))`, the anti-diagonal pairs of the two plaquettes (the pairs block 05's corner law puts its `K^2` denominator on). Executed: `c = 5/48` per later recording neighbor at the declared instance (`|P_kx|^2/P_kk = (5/16)/3`), the corrections `[5/24, 5/24, 5/48, 5/48, 5/48, 0]` on the `2×3` monotone class, 0 orders of the plaquette with `P_σ = P` or with `P`'s support, the path's end-to-end order with `P`'s support and diagonal corrections `[5/48, 5/48, 0]`, the middle-out order with fill-in.
