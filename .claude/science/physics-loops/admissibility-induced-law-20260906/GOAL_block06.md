## Theorems (every number is in the controls' `.out.txt`; every decimal below is an exact integer-arithmetic expansion of a rational, rounded outward)

**S2 (self-adjointness and the real spectrum; proved).** `w_O Q_{OO'} = w_{O'} Q_{O'O}` for all orbit pairs. Proof: `G`-invariance of `V` and the transitivity of `G` on each orbit make `Σ_{ρ'∈O'} V(ρ, ρ')` independent of `ρ ∈ O`, so `Σ_{ρ∈O, ρ'∈O'} V(ρ, ρ') = |O| Σ_{ρ'∈O'} V(ρ_O, ρ') = |O'| Σ_{ρ∈O} V(ρ, ρ_{O'})`; multiply by `A_O A_{O'}` (orbit-constant by `G`-invariance of `A`) and use the symmetry `V(ρ, ρ_{O'}) = V(ρ_{O'}, ρ)` to reach the identity. The three hypotheses — `G`-invariance of `V`, `G`-invariance of `A`, symmetry of `V` — are all used and all supplied by F1 and the product rule; the lens showed by counterexample at `W = 2` that dropping any one breaks self-adjointness. Hence `Q` is self-adjoint for `⟨·,·⟩_w`: every eigenvalue is real, `Q` has a `w`-orthonormal eigenbasis, and `Σ_i λ_i^2 = tr(Q^2)` (a theorem from the spectral decomposition, not executed). Executed: the identity on all `38^2` and `178^2` orbit pairs at both triples; `tr(Q^2) = 28006524928, 250087391159985, 16238809878528, 1948759036672266913` in the order of the table.

**S3 (rigorous enclosures without a field; proved and executed).**
(a) The two-sided ratio bounds: for `y > 0`, `lo ≤ λ_1 ≤ hi`. Lower bound: F2's `r(y) = lo ≤ λ_1`. Upper bound: with the left Perron vector `u > 0` (`u^T Q = λ_1 u^T`; by S2 `u_O = w_O x_O`, positive since `w > 0` and `x > 0`), `λ_1 u^T y = u^T Q y ≤ hi · u^T y` and `u^T y > 0`. Also `lo > 0`.
(b) The trace bound: with `λ_2` the largest modulus among the non-Perron eigenvalues (all real by S2), `λ_2^2 ≤ Σ_{i≥2} λ_i^2 = tr(Q^2) − λ_1^2 ≤ tr(Q^2) − lo^2`, using `λ_1 ≥ lo > 0`; `λ_2 ≤ λ_2bound`, a rational upper bound of the square root (integer square root at scale `10^{80}`).
(c) The residual–gap bound: in a `w`-orthonormal eigenbasis with `v_1 = x` (the `w`-unit Perron vector), `ŷ = Σ_i a_i v_i` gives `r^2 = Σ_i a_i^2 (λ_i − μ)^2 ≥ δ^2 Σ_{i≥2} a_i^2 = δ^2 sin^2 θ` whenever `|λ_i − μ| ≥ δ` for all `i ≥ 2`; with `δ = μ − λ_2bound > 0` (executed) every non-Perron `λ_i ∈ [−λ_2bound, λ_2bound]` satisfies `μ − λ_i ≥ δ`, negative eigenvalues giving a larger gap, and `μ ≤ λ_1` is not used. Then `‖ŷ − x‖_w^2 = 2(1 − cos θ) ≤ 2 sin^2 θ` because `cos θ = ⟨ŷ, x⟩_w > 0` (all of `ŷ`, `x`, `w` are positive), so `‖ŷ − x‖_w ≤ √2 · r/δ =: ε`.
(d) The statistic: from F3 the deep-row law is `w(ρ) ∝ A(ρ) ρ_1(ρ)^2` with `ρ_1(ρ) = x_{O(ρ)}` and `A(ρ) = A_{O(ρ)}`, so the pair-parallel probability is `Σ_O A_O x_O^2 n_O / Σ_O A_O x_O^2 |O| = Σ_O w_O x_O^2 c_O / ‖x‖_w^2 = s(x)` for the `w`-unit `x`; and `|s(x) − s(ŷ)| = |Σ_O w_O (x_O − ŷ_O)(x_O + ŷ_O) c_O| ≤ Σ_O w_O |x_O − ŷ_O| |x_O + ŷ_O| ≤ ‖x − ŷ‖_w ‖x + ŷ‖_w ≤ 2ε` (drop `c_O ∈ [0, 1]` with `w > 0`; Cauchy–Schwarz in `⟨·,·⟩_w`; `‖x + ŷ‖_w ≤ 2`).
