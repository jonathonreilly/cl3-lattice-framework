PR #7951 left the shear sector of linearised gravity from records read by nothing, and named one supplied period-2 coupling that would read it. The obvious next question is whether the framework's own vacuum generates that coupling once the interaction is switched on, since the interaction generates a staggered mass and the mass depends on the bond geometry. The answer is no, twice over, and both reasons are exact. First, the geometry a length rule feeds to a nearest-neighbour law is the set of axis edge lengths, and an axis edge length is a function of the diagonal metric alone; the shear is never fed in, so there is no cancellation to locate and nothing at any order in the interaction to cancel. Second, and independently, the massive pi-flux vacuum's translations are the even-sum coarse sublattice -- index 2, not index 8 -- and under that group no covariant nearest-neighbour bond or site pattern carries the shear representation at all. The interaction-generated mass does respond, strongly, to the diagonal metric, and the graviton reading of PR #7951 survives the vacuum's own self-consistency; but the period-2 intertwiner turns out to be a heavier supply than that note priced, because it couples to a labelling of the sites that the vacuum's records do not register.
