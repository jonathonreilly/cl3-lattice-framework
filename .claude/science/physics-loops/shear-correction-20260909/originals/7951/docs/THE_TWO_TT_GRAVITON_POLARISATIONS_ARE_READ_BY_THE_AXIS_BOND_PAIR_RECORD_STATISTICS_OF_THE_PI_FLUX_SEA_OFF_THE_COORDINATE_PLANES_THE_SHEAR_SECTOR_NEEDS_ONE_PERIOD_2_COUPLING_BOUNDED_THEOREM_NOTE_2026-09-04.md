## Corollary -- what is readable from records, and what is supplied

"Readable" here means one thing: the linear map from the six metric components to record data is injective.

1. **With records only** -- the site statistic and the axis-bond pair statistics -- **the two propagating polarisations are read at every momentum with `k1 k2 k3 != 0`, with `O(1)` conditioning.** The cross polarisation is lost exactly on the three coordinate planes, where it is pure shear; the loss is a symmetry zero, exact at every torus size, not a small number.
2. **The three shear components are lost everywhere**, because a shear changes only diagonal edge lengths and the sea hops on no such edge. This holds for statistics of every order, not only pairs.
3. **The sublattice cancellation that forced the rate ruler for the scalar does not touch the pair statistics.** It is confined to the uniform part of the site statistic; the pair statistics respond at `2e-02` where the site statistic responds at `3e-18`.
4. **The pi-flux sea's face-diagonal pair statistics vanish identically**, so on this vacuum no record statistic of any order reads a shear under a length-dressed hop -- two independent zeros, the coupling's and the vacuum's.
5. **The full six-component metric is readable only with one supplied period-2 shear coupling**, unique by symmetry, which is not an edge-length rule: the `z`-bond's own length is untouched by `h_xy`, and the coupling reads the shear through the parity pattern of the endpoint sites, at superlattice-shifted momenta.
6. **Combined with G1:** the static sector is read by the site rate, the propagating sector by the pair statistics, and what remains supplied for linearised gravity from records is the shear coupling, the action `S_R` with its orientation and `G`, the dressing exponent, and the reference sea.

**Reading, not theorem.** In plain words: the sea is a pattern of records, and the only things anyone can count in it are how often a site carries a record and how often two neighbouring sites carry records together. Stretch the lattice by a gravitational wave and those coincidence counts change, by about two parts in a hundred per unit of metric perturbation, in a way that says which of the two wave polarisations passed -- unless the wave travels along a coordinate axis or in a coordinate plane, where one of the two makes no difference to any count at all. Squash the lattice by a shear instead and nothing changes, because a shear lengthens only the diagonals of the cubic cells and the sea has no diagonal hops to lengthen. The record-ontology guard: `s_v` and `C_a(v)` are frequencies of records -- one single-record frequency and one two-record coincidence frequency -- and the Record axiom says "Only records are readable. A readout value is determined by record content alone. A site with no record cannot be read." These statistics are functions of records alone, of the same kind as the site rate the ruler chain reads; the projector `P` and the perturbation theory are reconstructions that compute what would be registered, and nothing pre-record is read. Record licenses the reading; it does not supply the statistic, the rate, the dressing or the coupling, and nothing here is derived from the axioms.
