# Block-38 transaction grants: reachable collision theorem and arbitrary-map boundary

Date: 2026-09-01
Status: author-side bounded theorem plus measured counterexample; `BACKLOG`
Authority: none; no audit verdict and no obligation retirement

## Question and answer

Can the literal Block-38 Record kernel be extended from one isolated front to
arbitrary finite interacting fronts merely by treating its first ordinary
`H -> T` Record as an exclusive transaction grant?

The answer separates cleanly.

- **Positive:** yes on the reachable, clean-footprint, grant-consistent sector.
  Equal rate-one `H -> T` clocks select a compatible winner set; every winner
  then executes the unchanged Block-38 carrier measures inside its own complete
  18-write footprint. The resulting finite-support CTMC is normalized,
  covariant, append-only, owner-filtered, and nonexplosive.
- **Negative at the registered gate:** no on all arbitrary preloaded finite
  well-typed maps with a productive winner required in every overlap
  component. Ordinary `H/T` Records erase their write order, and there are
  permanent-head configurations in which no unchanged Block-38 transaction
  can complete.

Therefore this block does **not** retire the registered Block-37 `W3` wall.
It identifies the missing law data more sharply: a collision-specific
successor together with a writable status resource, or an explicit causal
state-domain restriction.

## Construction on the positive sector

For a source head `h`, frame `f`, and decoded protocol `pi`, let `F(h,f,pi)`
be the 18 sites written by the literal Block-38 trial:

```text
T, G, R, P, A, F, M, B2, C, Q1,...,Q8, H_next.
```

Two transactions conflict when their footprints intersect. A clean head is
one for which every site in its complete footprint is blank. Each clean,
ungranted head contributes the unchanged rate-one Block-38 `H -> T` row.
The first such append is an in-band permanent lease: its ordinary `T`, source
`H`, frame, and protocol reconstruct the owner and footprint from Records.

A literal grant is active only when:

1. the `H/T` pair agrees exactly in frame and protocol;
2. its footprint is disjoint from every other active grant footprint; and
3. every occupied site in its owner view is a causally closed prefix of the
   literal Block-38 transaction DAG.

Continuation proposals are evaluated only on `{source H} union footprint`.
This owner restriction prevents parents from distinct fronts from forming a
hybrid row. A clean conflicting head loses as soon as the winner's `T` is
written; the losing head remains a permanent Record and its intersection with
the recorded grant makes absorption state-visible. Disjoint components simply
contribute the union of their generator terms.

This is an **absorptive/exclusion** law. It does not establish elastic
scattering or survival of every incoming lineage. In particular, losing heads
remain permanent Records but receive no continuation under this construction.

## Decisive boundary 1: Record-state aliasing

Consider the visible map `C={H,T}` with a matching ordinary source/trigger
pair. It can arise from either history:

```text
history A: H is present, then the literal H -> T jump occurs;
history B: T is preloaded, then a matching H is written later.
```

Both histories have exactly the same current Carrier map. Any extensional
Markov generator `L(C)` must therefore assign them the same continuation law.
It cannot recognize A as a historical grant while rejecting B as an orphan.
The implemented reachable-sector convention accepts the aliased `H/T` map as
one syntactic grant and exposes its literal `T -> G` continuation.

This is not a universal no-go for Record collision laws. It is an exact
representation boundary for order-sensitive provenance when write order is
not itself recorded.

## Decisive boundary 2: mutually blocking permanent heads

Use frame 23 with `H1=(0,0,0)` and frame 3 with `H2=(8,0,0)`. Their complete
footprints intersect, and each source head occupies the other's required
future `H_next` site. Records cannot be overwritten. Therefore neither
unchanged Block-38 transaction can complete.

The exact runner confirms:

```text
mutual-head continuation terms       0
mutual-head absorbed transactions    2
preloaded matching T grants          2 literal / 0 valid
pregranted continuation terms        0
```

The 70,200-pair positive census excludes this geometry by its honest
simultaneously-clean predicate. It cannot be used as evidence for the stronger
all-map/productive-winner quantifier.

More generally, a finite configuration can occupy every site of any fixed
finite report neighborhood. Append-only total collision reporting on every
finite map therefore needs at least one of:

- an explicit absorbing outcome that writes nothing;
- a reserved writable collision/status lane with a genesis invariant;
- co-located extra Record capacity or allowed overwrite; or
- a covariant, state-dependent search for a free report site.

## What would close the wall

Two coherent routes remain.

1. **Adopt a causal state domain.** Declare the law domain to be the least
   causal-prefix-closed sector generated from clean heads by the stated
   transitions. This validates the positive theorem but intentionally weakens
   the registered arbitrary-map reading.
2. **Add collision law data.** Record `claim/grant/absorbed` status, causal
   parent or epoch provenance, a normalized collision successor, and a
   guaranteed writable status resource. A nearest-free-site construction can
   be total and covariant for finite maps, but is not uniformly finite-range;
   a strict nearest-neighbor construction needs a reserved lane or enlarged
   local storage.

Neither choice follows from the current four axioms. The equal clocks,
footprint definition, lease convention, owner filter, and any future
collision-successor row are downstream candidate-law data. This block does
not select `lambda`, a physical clock unit, a genesis distribution, or one
actual infinite history.

## No-go discipline

The negative conclusion is deliberately narrow. The following distinct
positive escapes were examined: history-blind syntactic grants with terminal
absorption; a reachable causal-prefix domain; a collision tombstone; random
mark/claim/grant Records; reserved status sites; covariant nearest-blank
search; and widened/co-located carrier status. Several remain viable.

Accordingly, this note does not claim that a Record-only collision process is
impossible. It establishes only:

- an extensional state map cannot distinguish two histories that project to
  the same `H/T` configuration; and
- fixed-range append-only productive liveness fails on arbitrary maps that
  finitely saturate the required output neighborhood.

The two walls are independent of symmetry, rate normalization, literal
singleton preservation, and law selection. The reachable-sector theorem is
retained as positive partial closure.
