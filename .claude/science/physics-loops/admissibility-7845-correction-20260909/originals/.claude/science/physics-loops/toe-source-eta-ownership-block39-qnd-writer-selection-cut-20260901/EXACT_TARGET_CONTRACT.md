
```text

```

covariance, atomless-axis measurability, and the Block-38 causal/local process
shape leave `lambda` free.  Then prove:


- faithful range reduction `Q_b V = V P_b(a)`, together with system QND;

  environment.

Each must imply `|lambda|=1`; the matching Record orientation must exclude
`lambda=-1` and yield the Block-38 `lambda=1`, `kappa=1`, repeat-certain row.
