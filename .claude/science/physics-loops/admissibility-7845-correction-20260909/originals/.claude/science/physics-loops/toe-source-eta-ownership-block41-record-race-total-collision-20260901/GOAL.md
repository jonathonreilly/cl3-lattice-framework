## Proposed transaction-level construction

Every well-typed ungranted `H` Record determines its complete finite Block-38
trial write footprint from its site, frame, and protocol. Two heads conflict
exactly when those write footprints intersect. The first ordinary Block-38
`H -> T` append is re-used as a permanent transaction grant:

1. every ready ungranted head whose full footprint is blank and not excluded
   by an existing conflicting grant owns a rate-one grant clock;
2. when that clock rings, it appends the *unchanged* `T` carrier at the literal
   Block-38 trigger site;
3. that permanent `T`, together with its source `H`, identifies the granted
   head and its complete owned footprint;
4. every conflicting ungranted `H` is thereafter absorbed because its conflict
   with the recorded grant is reconstructible from the Record map; and
5. only proposals whose parents and target belong to one granted footprint are
   admitted, so the winner executes the unchanged
   `T->G->R/P/Q->A->F->M->B2->C->H_next` rows and cross-front hybrids are
   forbidden.

Different granted footprints are disjoint. Their inherited local generator
terms therefore superpose. Equal local grant clocks define an online random
sequential independent-set law: no coordinate priority or host enumeration is
visible, the normal `T` outputs store the winner set, and a losing `H` plus the
conflicting `T` reconstructs the loss. The construction consumes no auxiliary
lease layer: the first ordinary transaction Record is the grant and the normal
18-write footprint is the finite Record debit.

## Required boundary

ownership filter, and absorption semantics are explicit downstream
