# Mutation plan

Each mutation must fail its own named semantic gate.

1. compute conflicts only at the immediate target rather than on complete
   transaction footprints;
2. give heads coordinate-dependent priority weights;
3. accept a supplied claimant subset or lease family rather than derive heads
   and footprints from Records;
4. replace every overlap component by STOP;
5. admit both grants on intersecting footprints;
6. let an ungranted/absorbed head continue;
7. allow a hybrid row whose parents belong to two owners;
8. allow a losing transaction to overwrite a winner;
9. delete or mutate one parent Record after a grant;
10. insert a global sorted-site scheduler;
11. collapse source-declared branch atoms to one output;
12. replace the atomless Haar source row by its six-axis controls;
13. change the singleton trigger rate or any continuation row;
14. omit mixed-protocol, turn, or distinct-trigger conflicts;
15. break one proper-cubic frame or translation;
16. couple two separated components through one global tie-break;
17. use a superlinear unbounded proposal multiplier that defeats the stated
    linear Lyapunov bound;
18. claim all incoming lineages survive an absorptive collision;
19. claim collision arbitration selects `lambda=1`;
20. claim W1, W4/W5, matter/source/gravity, audit, or TOE closure.
