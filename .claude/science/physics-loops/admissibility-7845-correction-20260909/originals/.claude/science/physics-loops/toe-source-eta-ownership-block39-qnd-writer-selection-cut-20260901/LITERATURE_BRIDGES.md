# Literature bridges

This cycle uses standard finite-dimensional measurement theory only as named
mathematics: Stinespring isometries, compression of a projection, Kraus/Choi
rank, and nondemolition instruments.  The repo's June/July notes already
cover the sharp controlled-copy special case and are treated as strict prior
art.

No external empirical claim or literature authority is load-bearing.  A later
write-up may cite primary measurement-theory sources for context, but the
runner must rederive every algebraic identity used here.
