corners**—is an exact description of the fixed H1 action at the operator
level.  It is not yet a complete description of the physical Admissibility
law.

The literal H1 source, before numerical momentum evaluation, has the exact
form

\[
 S_F=mH_T+i\left(H_TD_{\rm in}+D_{\rm out}^{T}H_T\right),       \tag{1}
\]

and `H_T` is the H1 tensor-weighted sum of fixed endpoint averages.  The
actual reverse is obtained by the parent's physical reversal
`(p,q) -> (p+q,-q)`, not by replacing (1) with an adjoint surrogate.

atoms gives rank `136/136` and zero residual in both directions.  Stacking

This is meaningful progress: Block 206's effective radius-three source is a
composition of native one-link action factors rather than evidence by itself

\]

and

\[
 C_\mu={z_\mu u_\mu+z_\mu^{-1}\over2}.                         \tag{3}

\[
 B_{ij}=c_i a_j+c_j a_i,qquad

\[
 H_T=C_2\left(C_0B_{02}-{C_1B_{12}\over\sqrt2}\right).         \tag{5}

## 4. Cube And Edge Representation Theory

Let the eight spatial corners be `c=(c_x,c_y,c_z)` with `c_i in {+1,-1}`.
Under the 24 proper cubic rotations, the scalar corner permutation module
decomposes as

\[
 C_8=A_1\oplus T_1\oplus T_2\oplus A_2.                         \tag{6}
\]

The six signed scalar edges decompose as

\[
 E_6=A_1\oplus E\oplus T_1.                                    \tag{7}

Consequently,

\[
 \dim\operatorname{Hom}_O(C_8,T_2)=1,
 \qquad
 \dim\operatorname{Hom}_O(E_6,T_2)=0.                          \tag{8}
\]

The normalized unique scalar-corner map is the mixed moment

\[
 M r={1\over8}\sum_c(c_xc_y,c_yc_z,c_xc_z)r_c.                 \tag{9}

It is even under corner inversion.  Scalar directions alone therefore
cannot supply a `T2` shear, while scalar corners can.

For a Hermitian edge content

\[
 X_n=a_n I+v_n\cdot\sigma,
\]

the Bloch domain gives

\[
 \dim\operatorname{Hom}_O(E_6\otimes T_1,T_2)=2.               \tag{10}

There are two normalized local comparisons.  For corner `c`, the radial
(Jordan-type) score is

\[
 r_c=\sum_i \widehat c\cdot v_{c_i e_i},                        \tag{11}

equivalently a sum of `Tr[X_{c_i e_i}(widehat c.sigma)]/2`.  The
handed (commutator-type) score is

\[
 h_c=\sum_i(c_i e_i\times\widehat c)\cdot v_{c_i e_i},          \tag{12}

equivalently a sum of
`Tr[X_{c_i e_i}[c_i e_i.sigma,widehat c.sigma]]/(4i)`.

Let `D_odd` and `D_even` be the two Block-206 edge-to-`T2` maps.  Exact
composition with (9) gives

\[
 M A_{\rm radial}=-{D_{\rm odd}\over2\sqrt3},\qquad
 M A_{\rm handed}= {D_{\rm even}\over2\sqrt3}.                 \tag{13}

Both identities pass every proper-cubic frame.  After shell parity is fixed,
the relevant Hom dimension is one in each class.  Thus radial/Jordan content
selects the odd decoder uniquely, while handed/commutator content selects the
even decoder uniquely.  Covariance alone permits a linear combination and
does not decide which physical comparison the action or Records perform.
