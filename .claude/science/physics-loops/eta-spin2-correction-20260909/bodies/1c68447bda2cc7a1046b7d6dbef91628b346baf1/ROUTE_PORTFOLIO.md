# Route Portfolio

## Post-result ranking

1. **Common H1+H2 source/action module:** highest information gain and shortest
   route to a law that survives more than one fixture.
2. **Distributed M4 carrier:** execute immediately after item 1 survives;
   otherwise carrier work cannot repair the source-law failure.
3. **Generated arbitrary history:** valuable only for a source law worth
   extending beyond H1.
4. **Rate and clock:** decisive for end-to-end physics, but downstream of a
   common event law.
5. **q=4 gravity hedge:** keep read-only until it connects representation
   response to selected action/source, Records, or time.

Recheck this ranking after the common-module representation census and again
after any exact H1+H2 action fit.  Do not count extra H1 events, more q=3
channels, documentation, or blocked pipeline work as TOE lane progress.
