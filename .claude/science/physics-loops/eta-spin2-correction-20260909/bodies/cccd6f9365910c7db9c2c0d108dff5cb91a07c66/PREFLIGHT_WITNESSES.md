# Source/Eta Block 09 Preflight Witnesses

1. Block 08 proves that the exact H1/H2 source coefficients occupy the
   five-dimensional trace-free spatial module `E direct-sum T2`, and that the
   native forward and literal actual-reverse source maps are injective on it.
2. Candidate Block 207 proves the H1 off-diagonal `T2` tensor can be represented
   by directional Bloch comparisons and the unique mixed corner moment.  Its
   positive witness has `b_c=-Q c/4`, but it does not derive the inputs or
   clock/Record attachment.
3. Candidate Block 208 supplies a positive endpoint comparison and two-time
   temporal compiler for H1, while explicitly leaving action-state solder,
   cell-leg typing, outcome readability, sharpness, and causal Record
   attachment open.
4. The minimal axioms permit one fixed nearest-neighbor distribution depending
   on neighboring conditions.  They do not specify the distribution, source
   identification, preparation, formation site/rate, or clock.

## Why this is not the Block-08 six-bit search

The runtime input is six full `M2` Record contents, not the frozen 64-mask
affine G-set.  The proposed law is one fixed affine distribution on 14
one-site qubit possibilities.  It therefore changes the physical input type
and must earn that type by positivity, covariance, causal ownership, and joint
action-state placement; the extra continuous coordinates cannot be counted as
ownership merely because they have enough dimension.

## Immediate falsifiers

The registered law fails if any of the following occurs:

1. a probability is negative or the 14 probabilities do not sum exactly to
   one for the full six-Bloch-ball domain;
2. the second moment is not exactly `tau S`;
3. H1 or H2 neighbor/composite-corner states lose positivity;
4. any H1/H2 coefficient, cubic frame, native forward source, or literal
   actual-reverse source has nonzero residual;
5. the symbolic open-family identity or injectivity fails;
6. a runtime path reads `Q`, TT/source coefficients, H1/H2, `p`, `q`, a
   fixture/orbit label, a diagonal site, same-event post-state, or downstream
   M4 state;
7. action/clock data require an untyped extra on-site factor while the result
   is labeled `OWNED-COMMON`;
8. a distribution moment is described as the single realized Record rather
   than a law statistic inferable across Records;
9. formation, history, rate, clock, gravity, retained status, axiom change, or
   TOE percentage movement is claimed without executing its separate gate.

## Required independent reconstruction

The independent runner must rebuild the proper-cubic action, STF map,
probability normalization/positivity bounds, moment inversion, normalized
symmetric-tensor basis, H1/H2 coefficient matrices, symbolic-family identity,
and native forward/reverse rank checks without importing the primary runner.
It must use nonidentical mutations, including target-fed weights, diagonal
corner sites, dropped `E`, dropped `T2`, changed gain, adjoint reversal, and a
false `OWNED-COMMON` promotion.
