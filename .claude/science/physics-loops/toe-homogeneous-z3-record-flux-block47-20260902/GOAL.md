# Goal

Remove the largest artificial premise in Block 46's positive Record
discriminator: isolation of one eight-site cube from the rest of the cubic
lattice.

On the homogeneous infinite lattice, compare one uniform nearest-neighbor
signed adjacency `H0` with the canonical all-plaquette-pi-flux signed
adjacency `H1`. Use one common localized, unrecorded one-particle preparation,
one common cadence, the same local target occupation PVM, and the same
post-formation permanence mechanism. Prove or refute that a target whose
coordinate-parity difference has Hamming weight at least two is exactly dark
for `H1` while a body-diagonal target is reached with positive probability by
`H0`.

This is an assumption-removal block, not a TOE-closure claim. Passing removes
finite-cube boundary isolation from the conditional experiment. It does not
select either action, derive a clock, state preparation, Born functional,
Record formation, or formation-triggered gate, and therefore earns zero
obligation retirement and zero TOE-percentage movement by itself. The
parallel high-leverage target remains the physical matter-functional clause
`I-4` and the operational Record-process ownership map.
