- one bounded theorem note under `docs/`;
- one exact primary runner under `scripts/`;
- one cached stdout under `logs/runner-cache/`;
- source-binding, mutation, panel, novelty, no-go, trace, and handoff

publication tables, global status boards, minimal axioms, or primitive
registries. Do not open a standalone PR unless the postexecution impact gate
changes from zero obligation retirement.
