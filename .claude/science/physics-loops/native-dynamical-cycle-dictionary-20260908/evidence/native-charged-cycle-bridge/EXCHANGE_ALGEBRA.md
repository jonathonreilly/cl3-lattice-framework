# Separate directed three-hop identity

This extension was checked after root supplied the candidate; it is not a blind discovery or a statistical-particle theorem. The earlier DERIVATION hash remains unchanged.

Fix charge s=+1 or −1 and distinct neighbors j,k,l of i. Let t^s_ab have source b and target a: restrict P T_ab P on the right to Q_b=s,Q_a=0. Its image automatically has Q_a=s,Q_b=0. The source hole has B_b=+1 and blank target B_a=−1, so on its permitted domain each directed hop equals −i A_ab with the stated computational-basis domain restriction. This factor is independent of signed species.

Consider the two products (rightmost hop acts first)

 t^s_il t^s_ki t^s_ij and t^s_ij t^s_ki t^s_il.

Both are supported on exactly the same basis inputs: Q_j=Q_l=s, Q_i=Q_k=0, with all other charges initially low, and bit toggles delta_ij=delta_il=epsilon_i s, delta_ik=−epsilon_i s. The three edges are distinct, so each such bit condition is unaffected by the other toggles. In either history i successively has charges 0,s,0,s; one outer source empties, k fills, then the other source empties. Thus neither ordering has a hidden forbidden intermediate state when the other survives. Conversely the endpoint projectors in either ordering require precisely these initial charges and bits.

On this common input domain, the first operator product is (−i)^3 A_il A_ki A_ij and the second is (−i)^3 A_ij A_ki A_il. All three A's meet i, hence pairwise anticommute by the actual native algebra, including their ordered Pauli dressings and orientation conventions. Reversing three factors requires three transpositions and contributes −1. Off the common domain both products vanish. Therefore the two full restricted operators obey

 t^s_il t^s_ki t^s_ij = − t^s_ij t^s_ki t^s_il.

This is an exact microscopic hopping-algebra identity, not merely a probability comparison. It adds a nontrivial phase statement absent from the diagonal support table. It still does not construct a separated matter/link tensor product, an independent gauge redundancy, mobile deconfined excitations or an infrared particle interpretation. No external exchange-statistics theorem is invoked here. The identity does not justify renaming the derived Q=div E relation an independent matter Gauss constraint.
