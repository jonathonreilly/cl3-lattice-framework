1. **The sharing rule is not a menu.** Under the sum rule the endpoint mean is not one option among several but an identity (`A2a`): the exact edge rule the static-Regge note derived *is* the star tick's sharing.

**Reading, not theorem.** In plain words: the axioms say records form, one to a site, permanent and readable, and that a readout is fixed by record content alone. The gravity lane needed a rate at a corner and got one by stipulation; the tick lane needed a unit that leaves the vacuum alone and found that only a whole parity class does. This note only asks whether those two supplied objects can be one, and the honest answer is that they can be one exactly once -- when the unit is a single star, a record site's record forms whenever either of its two stars ticks, and a corner's clock counts its own star.
