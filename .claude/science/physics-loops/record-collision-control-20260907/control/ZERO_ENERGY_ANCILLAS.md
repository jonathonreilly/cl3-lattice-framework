# Arbitrarily many zero-energy ancillary qubits

The supplement's two-site obstruction extends to every finite number m of additional qubits with conserved energy K_total=K tensor I_anc. Allow arbitrary globally placed one-/two-site Hamiltonian controls, including interactions between ancillary qubits and between ancillas and the original qubits, provided EACH complete Hamiltonian sum commutes with K_total. No ancillary independence or product-state premise is required.

In the same product energy basis, an extra ancilla flip has energy gap zero. Thus a battery-bit flip paired with an ancilla flip has the nonzero battery gap alone. The only possibly resonant battery-high-bit transitions remain its pairs with x or f; their two-site coefficients cannot condition on the missing energetic spectator f or x. The corresponding nonresonant spectator sector forces each coefficient to vanish. Single-flip amplitudes with summed diagonal spectator conditioning also vanish because a lone battery flip always has nonzero gap. Consequently every allowed sum obeys [H,n_b0]=[H,n_b1]=[H,Qactive tensor I_anc]=0.

Interactions among the zero-energy ancillas are allowed by this statement, rather than assumed absent. Their concatenation with other allowed controls cannot change the common invariant: commutators, products of admissible propagators, and time-dependent schedules all preserve it. An arbitrary entangled ancillary/reference state supported with the original source in the bright sector stays there. The selected target has zero bright charge, so its probability remains zero even after discarding the ancillas. Postselecting an ancilla outcome also cannot create support in the orthogonal target sector from a globally invariant bright-supported state.

This is not an exclusion of implementations using energetic ancillas, a modified interacting conserved Hamiltonian, nonconserving controls, or support greater than two sites. Those alter premises and require a separate analysis. It establishes only that adding any finite inventory of zero-energy ancillary workspace to the unchanged K and globally energy-commuting two-site control family does not remove this particular obstruction. No claim about a thermodynamic infinite-ancilla limit or unbounded generators is needed.
