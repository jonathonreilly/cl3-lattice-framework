# Limits and corrected routes

Disjoint-pair adjacency is indefinite; positivity of individual inverse operators alone did not prove a positive sum. The new moment bound overcomes that obstacle for the direct sum. Cubic symmetry removes only scalar vacuum projections from the negative incidence channel, not vector-valued excitations. Ward boundary sources cannot be dropped. Matching source sums do not make distinct impurity resolvents commute. The full alpha problem is not declared impossible.
