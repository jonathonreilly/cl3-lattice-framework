## Required successor target

Use the terminal
`NN-TRANSACTIONAL-CAP-PACKET-AND-FIRST-STEP-COMPILER`.  Freeze:

- one selected-center trigger, ordinary one-site `M_2` carriers and Record
  states, and an explicitly bounded product-initialized local environment;
- a finite alphabet of onsite and nearest-neighbor gates/instruments, one
  translation/proper-cubic-covariant rule, finite causal depth, and no absolute
  coordinates, supplied `f`, role labels, scheduler, or global blank projector;
- exact branch-resolved channel equality with Block 16 on the complete 43-site
  blank sector, including arbitrary reference entanglement, rather than sample
  output equality;
- exact identity/STOP on every nonblank Record mask, Record permanence, weights
  `1/6`, CP/TP normalization, and microscopic covariance;
- an algorithmic construction from a six-way covariant coin, one base
  `rho_(143/256)` preparer and cubic rotations, local blank detection,
  orientation propagation, generated roles, prepare/commit, and uncomputation;
- explicit input-dump, output-purification, branch, workspace, and erasure
  accounting rather than an unexplained reset;
- conflict semantics on the complete 73-site transaction footprint: isolated
  triggers commit and factorize, while every trigger of positive conflict
  degree takes a normalized all-or-none STOP with no partial Records, ordering
  dependence, postselection, or deadlock;
- one compiled Block 15 controller step on the 73-site extension.  A writer-only
  compiler receives the partial terminal `NN-WRITER-ONLY`.

Because each branch is a full-rank trace-and-prepare map on a 43-qubit blank
sector, a literal full-channel dilation has Choi rank `2^86` per branch and
`6*2^86` across the six outcomes.  A faithful full-channel construction must
therefore account for at least 89 qubits' worth of environment before reusable
workspace.  This lower bound is a resource warning, not a no-go.

## Falsifiers and kill criteria

Fail the target for any block-sized gate, global blank test, hidden coordinate
table, supplied orientation, target-state lookup, output-programmed ancilla,
unaccounted erasure, postselection, approximate weights/preparations, or
branch-dependent scheduler.  Exact channel equality cannot be replaced by
basis or product-state samples.  Disjoint transactions must commute and
factorize; overlaps may not partially write or depend on execution order.

Stop this route after one bounded scout if it yields only generic “Stinespring
plus SWAPs,” embeds the 43-by-6 table in gates or ancillas, imports Block 16's
target constructor, lacks a frozen local alphabet/environment/depth/conflict
rule, or finds no branch-independent local grammar.  Such a failure is
`NO-MEMBER-IN-FROZEN-COMPILER-CLASS`, never a general locality no-go.  Pivot
flag realization to the pointer route, conflict/liveness to arbitration,
selection/timing to occurrence/time, and gravity only after a timed conserved
source exists.

No axiom update is justified by Block 16 or by this portfolio vote.
