## Claim (bounded)

Under **L2** — each link on a unit-cube face carries its **own independent**
qubit-link `u(2)` connection — the joint connection-generator Lie algebra on
the 4-qubit face is, in the gauge / parallel-transport embedding, exactly

```text
g_face  =  su(2)_0 (+) su(2)_1 (+) su(2)_2 (+) su(2)_3 (+) u(1)_global,
dim g_face = 13,
```

and it contains **NO faithful su(3)**. The two face-diagonals add **0**
dimensions: the nearest-neighbor (NN) 4-cycle already generates the full
13-dimensional algebra (connected-graph collapse). Hence **GATE-COLOR is not
closed by L2** on the face: the dimension coincidence "`4 + 4 = 8 = dim su(3)`"
is **not** realized as a faithful color algebra.

The result is **bounded** because the link-connection convention and the choice
of operator embedding are **inputs** to this audit, not derived from the
Lattice + Quantum + Record axioms (cf.
[`QUBIT_LINK_U2_CONNECTION_ALGEBRA_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-04.md`](QUBIT_LINK_U2_CONNECTION_ALGEBRA_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-04.md),
itself bounded on that convention). This note **does not change axioms**.

## 1. Setup

The face has 4 qubit sites at `(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(0,1)` (labeled `0,1,2,3`
around the square), Hilbert space `H_face = (C^2)^{⊗4}`, `dim = 16`. Links:

```text
NN edges (the 4 sides):  (0,1), (1,2), (2,3), (3,0)
face-diagonals:          (0,2), (1,3)
```

"A link carries `u(2)`" must be turned into operators on `H_face`. Two faithful
**embeddings** are audited; the answer is reported for each.

- **Embedding A — gauge / parallel transport.** The link's structure group acts
  on the matter fibers in the fundamental: an element `X ∈ u(2)` on link `(a,b)`
  acts as `X^(a) + X^(b)` (the same `u(2)` element on both endpoint qubit
  fibers). This is the standard lattice-gauge action of a link variable on
  adjacent matter and matches the note's "unitary map between the two fibers".
- **Embedding B — tight-binding / single particle.** The link induces a hop on
  the "which-site" label space; the `u(2)` phase supplies both the symmetric
  Hermitian hop `E_ab + E_ba` and the antisymmetric Hermitian hop
  `i(E_ab − E_ba)`.

## 2. Embedding A (gauge): dim 13, no su(3), diagonals add 0

Closing the Lie algebra generated by all link generators `i(σ_k^(a)+σ_k^(b))`
plus the central phase `iI_16` gives **dimension 13**, both for NN-only and for
NN+diagonals. Structure (all verified by the runner):

- the central `iI_16` is the `u(1)_global`;
- the derived algebra `[g,g]` has dimension **12** and is spanned by the four
  site-local `su(2)`'s `{iσ_x^(s), iσ_y^(s), iσ_z^(s)}`, `s = 0..3`;
- `su(2)`'s on different sites commute, and each is an **ideal** of `g`.

So the semisimple part is four **commuting** `su(2)` ideals, each of rank 1
and dim 3. A faithful `su(3)` (simple, dim 8, rank 2) would project to each
ideal as a homomorphism `su(3) → su(2)_s` that is `0` or injective; injective
is impossible (`8 > 3`), so all projections vanish and no faithful `su(3)`
exists. The largest simple ideal has dim **3 < 8**.

**Why the diagonals add nothing.** The bracket of two endpoint-summed
generators only ever produces single-site or edge-sum generators — it never
manufactures a two-site operator. The algebra is fixed by *connectedness* of
the link graph, and the NN 4-cycle is already connected. The two diagonals are
redundant: `dim g(NN) = dim g(NN+diag) = 13`. The naive count "`4 NN + 4 diag`"
never adds, because endpoint-summed `u(2)` generators collapse onto site-local
`su(2)` regardless of how many links share a vertex.

## 3. Embedding B (tight-binding) on the face: su(4), diagonals add 0

On the 4-site **label** space, the symmetric+antisymmetric hops on a connected
graph generate `su(n)`. The NN 4-cycle `C_4` is connected, so it already
generates **`su(4)` (dim 15)**; the diagonals complete it to `K_4` but add
nothing. `su(4) ≠ su(3)`. So even the most generous (label-space) reading does
not produce a faithful `su(3)` on the face.

## 4. The one place su(3) appears — and why it is the wrong su(3)

`su(3)` appears in exactly one place: the **hw=1 generation triangle**. The
three generations `{(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(0,0,1)}` are pairwise **face-diagonal**
(Hamming distance 2), forming a triangle `K_3`. In the tight-binding embedding
the three face-diagonal hops generate **`su(3)` (dim 8)** on the 3-dim
generation label space — and here the diagonals **do** matter: the three
generations are **not** nearest neighbors, so NN-only gives **zero** coupling
(0 generators), and the face-diagonals add the entire `su(3)`.

**But this is a `su(3)` on the generation factor, not an independent internal
color.** Its generators **mix generations** (a "color" rotation maps
generation-1 onto generation-2). It is a gauged **horizontal / family**
symmetry. Identifying it with QCD color is physically excluded:

- in the Standard Model each generation is itself a color triplet, so the
  quark count is `3 generations × 3 colors = 9` states per flavor-chirality,
  whereas a generation-`su(3)` has only the **3** generations total: `9 ≠ 3`;
- an unbroken gauged horizontal `su(3)` is not observed and is heavily
  constrained; it is not the QCD color group.

So the dimension coincidence is *explained* — the "missing" `su(3)` is the
family `su(3)` on the generation triangle — but it lands in the **wrong
physical slot**. This matches the parent obstruction: color "requires some
additional non-qubit multiplicity/selector structure" — the diagonal extension
supplies the *generation* multiplicity, not an independent color carrier.

## 5. Verdict on GATE-COLOR

**Not closed by L2.** Concretely:

| embedding | object | algebra | su(3)? | diagonals add |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (gauge) | 4-qubit face | `(+)_4 su(2) (+) u(1)`, dim 13 | no | 0 |
| B (tight-binding) | 4-site face labels | `su(4)`, dim 15 | no | 0 |
| B (tight-binding) | 3-site generation triangle | `su(3)`, dim 8 | **yes — but family, not color** | the whole `su(3)` |

The physical QCD color group (an independent internal `su(3)` under which each
generation is a triplet) does **not** appear. The diagonal extension relocates
the color gap into a generation/horizontal `su(3)` that the SM does not gauge.

## 6. Boundary and named residuals

- **Input, not derived:** the link-connection `u(2)` convention and the choice
  of embedding (A vs B) are inputs. A genuinely different (non-gauge,
  non-tight-binding) realization would be outside the convention and would
  require separate justification (an import).
- **Residual R1 (color carrier):** physical color still needs an independent
  3-dim internal carrier; the diagonal extension does not supply one. Open.
- **Residual R2 (family su(3)):** the generation-triangle `su(3)` is real but
  is a horizontal symmetry; whether any framework structure *gauges* it (and
  whether that is desirable) is a separate, unaddressed question.
- **No axiom change; no status set.** The audit asserts exact algebra facts
  and a bounded negative for color; promotion/standing is the audit lane's.
