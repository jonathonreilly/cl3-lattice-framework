> same-slot point on the supplied carrier.

impossibility claim, not a claim that no future law can select the point, and
not a claim that an axiom amendment is required.

## N2 — Wall-independence audit

The collapsed open-condition set is:

- `W_S`: sharpness, needed only to reduce the invariant effect square to the
  nontrivial `P_3/P_1` PVM;
- `W_U`: an update-law selector conditional on fixed sharp effects;
- `W_N`: an independently fixed one-tick dimensionless fluctuation
  normalization, needed to select a Wilson coefficient;
- `W_A`: physical selection/identification of the Wilson action surface,
  outside the supplied-action theorem.

| Pair | Closing first closes second? | Closing second closes first? | Independent? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `W_S`, `W_U` | no; sharp effects retain the `lambda` family | no; an update rule conditional on an effect need not derive sharpness | yes |
| `W_S`, `W_N` | no; effect sharpness fixes no gauge stiffness | no; a fluctuation coefficient fixes no POVM effect | yes |
| `W_S`, `W_A` | no; a sharp registration does not select an action | no; an action does not force a sharp effect | yes |
| `W_U`, `W_N` | no; repeatable update selection need not fix a tick rate | no; a Hessian law need not choose a measurement update | yes |
| `W_U`, `W_A` | no; a state-update law does not select Wilson dynamics | no; Wilson dynamics does not select the instrument | yes |
| `W_N`, `W_A` | no; the displayed law is conditional on a named kernel and does not physically identify that kernel | no; choosing the Wilson form leaves `beta` free | yes |

No wall is duplicated or downstream of another as defined. The conditional
Wilson coefficient theorem uses `W_A` as a supplied surface and isolates

## N7 — Strongest steelman

A hostile reviewer should object that the coefficient family proves only that
the predicates chosen so far are insufficient. A reflection-positive transfer
construction tied to an independently derived Record clock could produce a
normalised one-tick fluctuation theorem; Theorem W1 would then turn that new
mechanism directly into `beta=2N_c`. The concrete terminal obligation is to
derive both the tick independently of gauge diffusion and
`-Hess_e log K_tick=g_can`, not to declare either. The approved minimal-axiom
boundary leaves downstream dynamics open rather than forbidding it, and the
operator-side leading-point discussion in
`docs/WILSON_TEMPORAL_KERNEL_CASIMIR_GENERATOR_BETA_GBARE_TRANSPORT_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-07-01.md:195-199`
points at the same route, although that row is currently unaudited.

This is a convincing objection to a universal no-go. Therefore the universal
claim is rejected. The shipped claim is demoted to the exact current-premise
counterfamily statement, which the steelman does not contradict.
