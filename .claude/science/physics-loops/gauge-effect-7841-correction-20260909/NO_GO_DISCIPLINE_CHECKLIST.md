# No-Go Discipline Checklist

## Exact bounded sentence

> On the stated nondegenerate full-`L^2` Wilson domain, gauge invariance,
> positivity, spatial isotropy, the coefficient-product condition, and a
> common clock rescaling do not select `g=1` in the displayed family.

This is an exact counterfamily statement about listed properties on a supplied
operator domain. It is not a universal impossibility claim, a full-framework
model-independence claim, a claim that no future law can select the point, or a
claim that an axiom amendment is required.

This table records only how the packet stipulates its mathematical inputs and
which cross-implications it does not establish. It is not a proof that these

possible selector `W_N`; it does not claim a physically independent four-wall
decomposition. The effect and instrument choices belong to their own
finite-carrier statements and are not proved walls to the Wilson target.

The nonempty-edge and nonzero-`H_B` requirements are theorem-domain
hypotheses, not extra physical walls. If either is removed, the corresponding
operator claim is withdrawn rather than promoted to a broader no-go.

## N7 — Strongest steelman

A hostile reviewer should object that the coefficient family proves only that
the predicates chosen so far are insufficient. A reflection-positive transfer
construction tied to an independently derived Record clock could produce a
normalised one-tick fluctuation theorem; Theorem W1 would then turn that new
mechanism directly into `beta=2N_c`. The concrete terminal obligation is to
derive both the tick independently of gauge diffusion and
`-Hess_e log K_tick=g_can`, not to declare either. The approved minimal-axiom
boundary leaves downstream dynamics open rather than forbidding it, and the
operator-side leading-point discussion in
`archive/notes/docs/WILSON_TEMPORAL_KERNEL_CASIMIR_GENERATOR_BETA_GBARE_TRANSPORT_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-07-01.md:195-199`
points at the same route, although that row is currently unaudited.

This is a convincing objection to a universal no-go. Therefore the universal
claim is rejected. The shipped claim is the exact listed-properties
counterfamily statement, which the steelman does not contradict.
