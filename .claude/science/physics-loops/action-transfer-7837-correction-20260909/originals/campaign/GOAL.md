# Goal

Resolve the highest-leverage open Admissibility/Record interface exposed by
Source/Eta Block 20: whether one local action object can supply both Record
content and Record-formation activity without inserting an unrelated hazard,
hidden reset, or external metric clock.

The block compares two explicit microscopic completions of the same positive
action transfer

```text
A_eta = -h m_eta.sigma,
W_eta = exp(-A_eta),
```

where `m_eta` is the covariant mean of neighboring Record directions.

1. A same-qubit Kraus process with action-weighted jumps and the completeness-
   forced no-jump branch.
2. An orthogonal blank/Record-sector process whose jump operators use the same
   `W_eta^(1/2)` and whose formed Records are absorbing.

The campaign must first determine whether the cross-calibration is really an
exponential-family artifact or the more general signature of a positive
constant-determinant transfer. It must then prove or refute the exact normalization, complete-positive
instrument, survival, content, hazard, covariance, minimal-carrier, and
two-site race formulas for both. It must then derive a Record-only discriminator
that distinguishes survival filtering from memoryless formation without
measuring an absolute duration.

The strongest anticipated invariant form is

```text
W_eta = alpha_eta (I + r_eta.sigma),
det W_eta = det W_0
  <=> Gamma_eta/Gamma_0 = 1/sqrt(1-|r_eta|^2),
```

provided the same linear trace/Haar event-flux rule supplies the marked
intensity. The runner must establish the biconditional at its exact scope and
show how a context-dependent scalar action term breaks it.

The positive SuccessSlug is
`ACTION_TRANSFER_RECORD_RACE_CONTENT_CROSSCAL_EXACT` only if the composite is
absent from current main and the open PR/source-eta stack and clears V1--V5.
Otherwise return the narrow duplicate or physical/axiom decision boundary.

percentage moves unless a registered obligation is actually retired. No
