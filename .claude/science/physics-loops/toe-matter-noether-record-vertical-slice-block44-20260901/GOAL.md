# Goal

Determine whether one fixed finite matter law can carry a candidate matter
functional all the way through an action-derived Noether current to a
permanent, even Record that operationally distinguishes CAR from an ordinary
hard-core product, without querying a product label or separately selecting a
different current, writer, or readout for the two theories.

The strongest allowed positive result is a new **conditional connector**:
conditional on the supplied hopping action and the existing physical
matter-functional identification interface, the same law produces a
Record-visible statistics discriminator. It is not an unconditional derivation
of the action, CAR, Born calibration, time cadence, or Record-formation law.

The campaign counts as high-impact only if the conjunction is genuinely
stronger than the existing ring-spectrum, graded-product, and supplied-router
interferometer results. A technically correct recombination of those results is
backlogged with zero TOE movement.
