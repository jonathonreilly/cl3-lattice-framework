# Mutation plan

Required hostile mutations:

1. delete each square edge in turn;
2. reverse the orientation/sign convention for one bond current;
3. remove one current term from a vertex divergence;
4. replace one CAR Jordan-Wigner string by a commuting hard-core operator;
5. replace the graded crossing by ordinary SWAP/FSWAP as applicable;
6. change initial or target occupation while leaving the claim label fixed;
7. introduce a realization-specific writer and require the claim firewall to
   reject it;
8. relabel pointer states and verify invariant branch weights;
9. delete the Born/calibration premise and require probability language to be
   rejected while amplitude/trace algebra remains;
10. set `t=0`, `tau=0`, and a nontrivial exact cadence to distinguish structural
    zeros from trivial no-evolution zeros;
11. test an arbitrary local cubic response `K=aI+bS+cJ` and reject any claim
    that one operational ray fixes the full response family; and
12. swap the permanent pointer update for an overwrite and require permanence
    failure.

Every mutation must either be killed by a named assertion or be recorded as a
surviving limitation before promotion.
