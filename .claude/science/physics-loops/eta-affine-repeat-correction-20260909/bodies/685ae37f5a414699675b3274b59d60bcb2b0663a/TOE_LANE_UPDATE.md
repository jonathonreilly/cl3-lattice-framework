Hammersley--Clifford, triangle-freeness, and current Record force an affine
qubit kernel is decisively triaged: two exact positive nonlinear kernels
survive those named conditions.

affinity plus qubit covariance forces the affine family for a normalized
operational transition density. Orthogonal exclusion then selects the Born
ray; the opposite endpoint selects anti-Born. Ordinary addition of Record-event
probabilities does not provide mixture affinity, and operational affinity does
not automatically constrain an unnormalized six-neighbor pair factor without
a separate probability-to-factor/normalizer theorem.

This is a meaningful improvement in route confidence and axiom-decision
quality, but the percentages measure completed physical bridges and remain
