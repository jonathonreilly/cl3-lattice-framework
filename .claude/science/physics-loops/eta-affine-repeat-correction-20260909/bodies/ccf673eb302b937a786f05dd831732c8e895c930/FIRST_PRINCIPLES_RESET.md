# Block 36 first-principles reset

## Minimal allowed surface

`A_min` is exactly Lattice, Qubit, Admissibility, and Record at canonical
main.  The approved primitives are the scale-reference, kinetic-isotropy, and
realized-state interfaces at their registered scopes.  None supplies the
active probability values or process law.

## Forbidden imports

- Born or trace probabilities as the fair writer;
- an observed/fitted `lambda`, sign, rate, or gravity value;
- a preselected realized branch;
- a hidden state-selection, scheduler, formation, or blank-bank rule;
- Record erasure;
- a loopy-lattice compatibility theorem disguised as tree induction;
- a new axiom or unregistered primitive.

## Stretch target

Use only the displayed Gaussian law plus an explicitly typed downstream
protocol to prove the largest exact positive joint theorem.  Every component
not derived from `A_min` must remain in the import ledger.  The stretch is
successful if it replaces an apparent incompatibility among affinity, factor
typing, and Record permanence by a single executable conditional model, even
though law selection remains open.
