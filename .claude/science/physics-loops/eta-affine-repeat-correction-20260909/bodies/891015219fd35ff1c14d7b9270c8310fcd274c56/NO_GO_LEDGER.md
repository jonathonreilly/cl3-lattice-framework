reversible finite-resource machine with initial random reservoir `E0` cannot

`H(B_1...B_N) <= H(E0)` while the target has entropy `N`.  This does not rule
out an explicit stochastic Markov law, an unbounded reservoir, a dissipative
law, or a different history ontology.  The selected route is the stochastic
escape, so no negative landing is planned.

- finite role/data carriers cannot be encoded without a host role oracle;
- the single-front invariant fails under the displayed table;
