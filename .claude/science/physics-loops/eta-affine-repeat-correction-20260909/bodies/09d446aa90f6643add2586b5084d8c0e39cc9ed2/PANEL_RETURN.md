probability mathematics, lattice/gravity integration, and prior art.  All five

## Consensus

1. Use the center-independent Gaussian content statistic at a fixed half
   threshold as the fair writer.  It supplies a non-Born random bit but not
   occurrence or a controller.
2. The bit Record must form before the branch-dependent preparation/test.  The
   coarse event must be an explicit sum over fine `(bit,outcome)` cylinders.
3. A fair bit is enough: midpoint affinity plus boundedness on the complete
   Bloch domain yields real affinity.  Randomize both operational slots and
   reuse the proper-cubic commutant solve instead of importing a full effect
   algebra.
4. Type the affine pair object as a normalized one-edge density with respect
   to normalized Haar measure.  On trees, leaf integration gives an exact
   product law.  On loops, a global normalizer is nontrivial; do not blur the
   two geometries.
5. Define reset predictively, not as erasure.  Advance to a fresh isomorphic
   port, keep old Records permanent, and prove that the next transcript law is
   independent of the complete archive Record fibre.
6. Keep sign selection, physical invocation/formation, fresh-bank genesis,
   and loopy `Z^3` gluing explicit.  Success is a candidate-law bridge, not an
   axiom derivation or obligation retirement.
