## Foundation boundary

M_2(C) possibility domain, one neighbor-conditioned probability distribution,
and permanent content-readable Records. They do not supply the distribution's
extensional values, a matter state/effect/instrument identification, a
context, an update, a repeatability condition, a formation site/rate, or a
physical clock.

The primitive registry was checked at origin/main commit
2cea9a595ee2f0a6c47096de6f821b905182f48c. The scale-reference,
kinetic-isotropy, and realized-state primitives supply no response parameter,
instrument, repeat condition, or physical attachment. None is load-bearing.

## Counterfactual pass

- If the successor state is mixed, the repeat mass becomes
  (1+lambda kappa)/2; exact closure may be impossible inside the positivity
  square unless both magnitudes reach one.
- If the output Record denotes only a nominal bit rather than the physical
  successor possibility, the sign can be a relabeling and the attachment does
  not select the physical Haar orientation.
- If a fresh independent axis is used, ordinary repeatability is not being
  tested and no endpoint selector follows.
- If the second use reads the first Record, certainty is a classical copy and
  says nothing about the response law.
- If repeatability remains an extensional law requirement rather than a
  consequence of the executed process, the result is conditional support and
  exposes a downstream law decision rather than an axiom theorem.
- If a nonlinear response survives the same randomizer, attachment, repeat,
  and history tests, response-class coverage fails and no affine/Born
  selection may be claimed.

## Forbidden imports

Observed Born weights, empirical frequencies, a fitted parameter, a preferred
axis, imported measurement context, a Lueders rule used as the selector, an
external IID tape, a scheduler, gravity/source arithmetic, and any new axiom
or primitive are forbidden proof inputs. Review-loop is disabled by the user
and will not be used.
