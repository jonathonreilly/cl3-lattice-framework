## 1. Typed Operational Objects

Fix an exact law `L`, a declared family of record-defined preparations `S`,
and a complete family of physically legal finite adaptive protocols `T`.
Assume for this theorem that `L` supplies a normalized conditional transcript
measure

```text
mu_s(tau | J),    s in S, J in T.
```

This line is the numerical input. If only lawful continuation support is
known, replace `mu` by a Boolean support functional; none of the probability
conclusions below then follows.

Define preparation equivalence by

```text
s ~_P s'
iff every finite adaptive future protocol has the same transcript law
    from s and s'.
```

Define effect equivalence by

```text
e ~_E e'
iff every operational preparation and every compatible adaptive surrounding
    context gives e and e' the same occurrence probability and continuation
    statistics.
```

The second definition must include sequential continuations when an event is
being used as an instrument branch rather than only as a one-shot effect. Two
Kraus branches can have the same immediate effect and different later
records.

On these classes, “the same operational effect has the same probability” is
not a further physical postulate. It is the definition of the quotient.
Complete-future equivalence is stable under insertion into every declared
future context, so it is a congruence for adaptive composition.

There are two limits.

First, this quotient is relative to `L` and `T`. Adding a legal tester can
split a class. Second, an operational class does not acquire a qubit operator
label merely because the local algebra is `M_2(C)`. A faithful,
context-compatible identification of the operational effect algebra with

```text
Eff(M_2) = {E in M_2(C) : 0 <= E <= I}
```

must be supplied by, or proved from, the exact physical law and its allowed
test repertoire.

## 2. Record-Fibre Strong Lumpability

Let `h` be a raw history and `R(h)` its complete permanent record
configuration. The record state is operationally sufficient exactly when

```text
R(h)=R(h')
=>
mu_h(tau | J)=mu_h'(tau | J)
for every finite compatible adaptive protocol J and transcript tau.
```

This is record-fibre strong lumpability. If it holds, operational preparation
classes factor through record configurations. The “state is a configuration
of records” sentence and the complete predictive state then agree.

Lumpability does not normalize a measure, choose its values, or provide the
complete tester repertoire. It says that once the law exists, hidden raw
history does not add predictive information beyond the records.

## 3. Physical Randomization Gives Affinity

Suppose a physically recorded randomizer has alternatives `a` and `b` with
law-supplied probabilities `lambda` and `1-lambda`. Conditional on `a`, it
runs preparation `s_1`; conditional on `b`, it runs `s_2`. If the randomizer
record is later forgotten by physical coarse-graining, the law of total
probability gives

```text
p(e | lambda s_1 + (1-lambda) s_2)
 = lambda p(e|s_1) + (1-lambda) p(e|s_2).
```

The same argument for a random choice between two test procedures gives

```text
p(lambda e_1 + (1-lambda)e_2 | s)
 = lambda p(e_1|s) + (1-lambda)p(e_2|s).
```

This is the correct operational origin of affinity. It needs a **physical**
randomizer, its normalized numerical branch law, conditional control, and a
forgetting/coarse-graining operation. A formal convex label alone does not
prove the equation.

The distinction matters. For `x in [0,1]`,

```text
q_2(x) = x^2 / (x^2+(1-x)^2)
```

is positive and normalized as a binary table, but

```text
q_2(1/4)=1/10
```

while physically mixing `x=0` and `x=1/2` equally would give

```text
(q_2(0)+q_2(1/2))/2 = 1/4.
```

Thus affine pairing is derived only when the physical randomization clause is
present.

## 4. Physical Coarse-Graining Gives Effect Additivity

Let `e_1,...,e_k` be mutually exclusive record outcomes in one physical test.
If `e` is the event obtained by forgetting which of these records occurred,
then finite additivity of the normalized transcript measure gives

```text
p(e|s)=sum_i p(e_i|s).
```

For an exhaustive test the sum is one. Under a test-preserving effect map

```text
j : operational effect classes -> Eff(M_2),
```

the corresponding statements are

```text
j(e)=sum_i j(e_i),
sum_i j(e_i)=I  for an exhaustive test.
```

If `j` is faithful and onto the full qubit effect interval, define

```text
m_s(E)=p(e|s) whenever j(e)=E.
```

Faithfulness/context compatibility makes this well-defined. Positivity and
normalization of the transcript law give

```text
m_s(0)=0,
m_s(I)=1,
m_s(E_i)>=0,
sum_i m_s(E_i)=1 whenever sum_i E_i=I.
```

These are exactly the premises needed by the qubit POVM Gleason theorem. In
the form used here: if one context-independent function
`m:Eff(C^2)->[0,1]` is normalized on every finite POVM, then there is a unique
density operator `rho` such that `m(E)=Tr(rho E)` for every qubit effect. Full
effect availability, POVM normalization, and effect noncontextuality are
load-bearing; no independent continuity premise is added.

Content-additive scalar readout is a different map. The Record axiom says,
for pairwise-disjoint records already present in one configuration,

```text
I(R_1 union R_2)=I(R_1)+I(R_2).
```

It does not say how weights assigned to mutually exclusive alternative
histories add. A count map `I(R)=|R|` is compatible with every binary kernel
`q`.

## 5. Strongest Positive Qubit Theorem

### Theorem

For each operational preparation class `s`, assume:

1. `W-NUM`: a normalized conditional transcript measure exists;
2. `W-QUOT`: complete preparation/effect equivalence is used, and it is a
   congruence under the allowed finite adaptive compositions;
3. `W-MIX`: recorded physical randomization and forgetting obey the
   conditional transcript law;
4. `W-COARSE`: mutually exclusive physical record outcomes admit the declared
   coarse-grainings;
5. `W-EFF`: the operational effect algebra is faithfully and
   test-preservingly isomorphic to the full qubit effect interval.

Then `m_s(E)=p(e|s)` is a well-defined normalized nonnegative additive
functional on every qubit POVM. By the POVM Gleason theorem for effects
(Busch 2003; Caves--Fuchs--Manne--Renes 2004), there is a unique density
operator `sigma_s` such that

```text
m_s(E)=Tr(sigma_s E)
```

for every qubit effect `E`.

### Proof map

1. `W-QUOT` makes the value independent of the chosen operational procedure
   representing one effect class.
2. `W-MIX` gives rational and real convex affinity on physically available
   mixtures.
3. `W-COARSE` plus `W-NUM` gives finite additivity and normalization on every
   physical POVM.
4. `W-EFF` transports those properties to all of `Eff(M_2)`.
5. The Busch/CFMR effect-Gleason theorem gives the unique positive trace-one
   representative.

checks an exact three-effect POVM, effect and preparation mixtures,

## 6. Clause-Delete Controls

### 6.1 Delete the numerical law: same structure, different weights

Take one predictive record state and one binary record instrument. For any
`0<q<1`, give a length-`N` word `w` the memoryless weight

```text
P_q(w)=q^(number of 1s) (1-q)^(number of 0s).
```

The `q=1/2` and `q=1/3` models have:

- identical binary support;
- the same one-state strong-lumpability structure;
- the same adaptive protocol tree and branch labels;
- normalized finite composition at every depth;
- the same physical mixture and coarse-graining operations; and
- the same content-additive scalar readout.

They assign different probabilities. An adaptive second operation that uses
kernel `1/4` after first outcome `0` and `3/4` after first outcome `1`
normalizes under either first-stage `q`; adaptive composition preserves both
laws and selects neither.

This is the smallest exact control for the central claim. Randomization and
coarse-graining do not create the numerical law.

### 6.2 Delete full POVM compatibility: cubic binary frame

For every Bloch direction `n`, set

```text
g(n)=(1+n_z^3)/2.
```

Then `g(n)>=0` and `g(n)+g(-n)=1`. Every binary projective context is
normalized.

For the trine directions

```text
n_1=(0,0,1),
n_2=(sqrt(3)/2,0,-1/2),
n_3=(-sqrt(3)/2,0,-1/2),
```

the effects `(2/3)P(n_i)` sum to `I`, but the homogeneous cubic assignment
gives

```text
sum_i (2/3)g(n_i)=5/4.
```

Thus binary complement equations do not replace full effect/POVM
coarse-graining. The full Busch/CFMR domain kills this countermodel.

### 6.3 Delete effect-faithful context compatibility

Give two outcomes in two contexts the same provisional matrix tag `P_0/2`,
but probabilities `1/4` and `1/3`. Each context can be normalized by its
complement. Complete operational equivalence correctly keeps the two
procedures in different classes because their statistics differ.

This does not violate operational noncontextuality. It shows that quotient
noncontextuality alone does not prove that a preassigned matrix tag is a
faithful physical effect identity. `W-EFF` is the bridge from operational
classes to the qubit effect algebra.

### 6.4 Delete effect completeness: a classical `Z` subtheory

Use only diagonal qubit effects. Physical mixtures, normalized
coarse-grainings, adaptive Markov composition, and strong lumpability all
exist. Yet `|+><+|` and `|-><-|` have identical statistics on every diagonal
effect. The effect span has real dimension two, not four.

This theory has a valid affine probability calculus but cannot reconstruct a
general qubit state or invoke the all-effects qubit theorem.

## 7. Prepared-State Identity

The representation theorem returns `sigma_s`, the unique density matrix that
represents one operational preparation class.

If the framework defines

```text
prepared state := complete operational preparation class,
```

and uses `sigma_s` only as that class's unique matrix representative, then
there is no second `PREP-FRAME` identity to assume. The identity is
definitional.

If a separate dynamical or ontic matrix `rho_s` is independently named, the
equation

```text
sigma_s=rho_s
```

does not follow from the representation theorem. The visibility family makes
this exact:

```text
rho_r=(I+r dot sigma)/2,
sigma_(v,r)=(I+v r dot sigma)/2,  0<=v<=1.
```

Every `v` gives a valid normalized noncontextual trace law. For `v=1/2` and
`r=z`, operational tomography gives `p(+z)=3/4`, while the independently
named `rho_z` gives `1`. The theorem represents the table by
`sigma_(1/2,z)`; it cannot rename that matrix `rho_z` without a bridge.

The minimum route is therefore to avoid introducing two preparation-state
objects unless the dynamics genuinely needs both.

## 8. Trial/Reset Corpus and Frequency Theorem

One-shot trace weights and repeated empirical frequencies are different
claims.

Let one binary effect have one-shot weight `q`. Compare two length-`N` process
laws.

### IID reset law

```text
P_IID(r_1,...,r_N)=product_i q^(r_i)(1-q)^(1-r_i).
```

This follows by the chain rule if an exact operational reset theorem proves,
for every prior transcript,

```text
P(r_(n+1)=1 | r_1,...,r_n)=q.
```

Then

```text
E[F_N]=q,
Var(F_N)=q(1-q)/N,
```

so finite Chebyshev concentration and the standard IID strong law apply.

Because records are permanent, “reset” should not mean literal erasure of the
global record configuration. It can mean that the next preparation port is
in the same complete predictive equivalence class while the old records are
archived outside the relevant future interface. That equivalence and archive
rule must be proved by the exact law.

### Frozen-memory law

Sample one bit `B` with weight `q` and set every recorded trial equal to `B`.
Every trial position has the same one-shot marginal `q`, but

```text
F_N is always 0 or 1,
Var(F_N)=q(1-q).
```

For both laws the expected count is `Nq`. Their concentration behavior is
different. The finite paired model proves that a one-shot effect functional,
even together with normalized multi-time composition, is not yet a frequency
theorem.

Thus the trial/reset corpus is separate. The frequency theorem is
conditional on the reset, IID, martingale, ergodic, or other explicitly named
joint-process condition actually proved.

## 9. Actual Member

The same binary measure

```text
{0:1-q, 1:q}
```

is compatible as a mathematical branching object with:

- no named realization;
- actual member `0`; or
- actual member `1`.

Normalization and trace representation do not distinguish these three
semantics.

This does not show that another generic axiom is needed. The live Record
sentence says records form, and the approved realized-state primitive gives a
pointwise reference to the law-admissible state fixed by physical history.
Those may already supply bare one-world ontology. The exact branching-law
document must still say how its measure governs the realized write. A sampled
instrument can bundle the measure and sample semantics; a deterministic or
boundary-conditioned unique extension can derive one history and eliminate
branching altogether.

The safe separation is:

```text

prepared-state identity            -> definition, or explicit rho/sigma bridge
trial/reset corpus                  -> repeated-preparation equivalence
frequency theorem                  -> joint-process theorem
actual member                      -> law-to-realized-history semantics
```
