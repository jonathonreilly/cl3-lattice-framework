# Block 38 goal

Construct and test one source-bound local extension of the Block-37 candidate
Record generator that factors its atomless Haar response through a recorded
Haar axis and a binary qubit instrument, attaches the first branch to a
physical M_2(C) successor state, and executes a second same-axis use before the
value-blind close.

The science target is a bounded conditional selector theorem, not a restatement
of the already-landed static repeatability theorem. The extension must use one
shared response parameter and one shared branch kernel in ordinary and
calibration uses, must forbid causal access to the first outcome Record during
the second use, and must preserve the parent compiler's local confluence,
active quotient M, archive screening S, and arbitrary-finite-history interface.

The strongest allowed positive result is conditional support for selecting the
Block-37 lambda=1 Haar row and attaching it to a declared physical matter
instrument within the executed response class. The block does not claim a
derivation or selection of that law from the four axioms, full multi-front or
loopy Z^3 theory, gravity closure, audit/obligation/TOE-score movement, or an
axiom or primitive update.
