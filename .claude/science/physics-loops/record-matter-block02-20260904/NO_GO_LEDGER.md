# Negative-claim record

This block ships a conditional positive theorem. It does not ship a new no-go.

The earlier source-only fixed-number gap is closed by the polar proof; do not preserve it as a permanent obstruction. Off-half filling, attraction, arbitrary defects, omitted Wilson loops and interacting post-deletion graphs lie outside this proof's hypotheses. Their exclusion from this theorem is not a universal impossibility statement.

Exact boundary controls are used only to show which proof steps need which assumptions. General uniqueness and dynamical sector selection remain different targets.
