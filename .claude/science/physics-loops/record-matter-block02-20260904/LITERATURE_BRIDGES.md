# Literature bridge

- Elliott H. Lieb, The Flux Phase of the Half-Filled Band, Phys. Rev. Lett. 73, 2158 (1994), https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9410025. The abstract and main theorem include cubic geometries and interactions. The ground-state discussion distinguishes a half-filled average from the sector containing an absolute ground state.
- Nicolas Macris and Bruno Nachtergaele, On the flux phase conjecture at half-filling: an improved proof, https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9604043. Theorem 1.4, sections 2(b,c) and 3 provide the reflection construction, spinless repulsive interaction and hypercubic cycle conditions. All three winding directions are explicitly accounted for here on a fully periodic three-torus.

The global flux theorem is established mathematics. The repo-specific contribution is a self-contained fixed-number/domain application and its reproducible checks. A source-only review preceded the independent polar review; both were separate-context same-family work, with no audit authority.

Known literature deserves an applicability check before a finite-size residual is promoted into an open mathematical frontier. The previous notes' planar-only/free-only descriptions are corrected in the science note rather than inherited.
