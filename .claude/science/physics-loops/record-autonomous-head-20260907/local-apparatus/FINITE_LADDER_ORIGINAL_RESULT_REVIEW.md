# Frozen native finite-ladder witness

The preregistered native N=2 square has opposite-dimer hopping coefficients 1 and sqrt(2)/3, four unit fuel latches, initial head 0, sine battery packet [4,5], cap [0,12], and 48 positive half-cell levels at spacing 1/4. The actual original free Hamiltonian is retained. This is a three-event complete Record instrument sequence with all signs and retained battery, not a full finite-time path-summed GKSL simulation or a cube benchmark.

The physical native carrier is imported from the frozen independent repository checker, while custom noncommensurate Hamiltonians, rounded lifts, actual total-energy matrices, cap completion, continuous reference and mutation controls are built here. This is consequently independent of the primary finite-ladder proof implementation, but not an independent rebuild of the baseline native Pauli carrier.

All six mutations fail their target: omitted or doubled fuel gives rounded intertwining defect 0.70710678; separate sign refusal completions give completeness defect 1; asserting original energy invariance fails by 0.02562305; physically resetting the battery after event one changes final energy by 0.26153144; unrestricted distinct-rate sector uniformization gives derivative defect 0.25.

The last control is an explicit two-sector state rho=ones(2,2)/2 with L=diag(0,1), R=L^dagger L and Lambda=1. The correct GKSL derivative has off-diagonal -1/4, whereas adding the proposed null Kraus diag(1,0) to a Poisson channel gives -1/2. It rejects only the unrestricted coherent-sector extension. It does not reject the definite-sector experiment or its invariant classical-sector mixtures.
