# Focused proof review: fuel positivity

Verdict: the proposed statements hold with the distinctions below. This is an algebraic review, not a new numerical run or a retuning of the frozen fixture.

Let the current legal input sector have q live edges, native physical code projector P, full sector energy A_in=H_live+q Delta I, and selected live edge e. Embed the two target code sectors in the common physical carrier, with A_out=H_live-h_e+(q-1)Delta I. Head, history and Record labels are degenerate. Each h_e is self-adjoint with norm at most t.

## Spectral support

For Delta>=t each Delta I+h_e is positive and bounded by (Delta+t)I. Therefore 0<=A_R<=q(Delta+t)I<=L(Delta+t)I, with no commutativity requirement between hopping terms. For battery support [b,b+w], initial total energy support is contained in [b,b+w+L(Delta+t)]. Exact total-energy intertwining then keeps the battery in [b-L(Delta+t), b+w+L(Delta+t)] in every reachable sector. This is a uniform sufficient bound, not an optimal necessary bound. For L=12 and t=Delta=1, [48,49] gives battery containment [24,73], strictly inside the frozen cap [0,97]. Separately, [24,25] gives [0,49] and is sufficient for that alternative cap. The latter is an analytical optional translated preparation, not a changed benchmark or computed improvement.

## Native nonselective identity

Since each surviving hopping term and scalar fuel term commutes with the measured physical Z_e, the two native projectors Q_z=(I+z Z_e)/2 satisfy sum_z Q_z A_out Q_z=A_out. In input/output code dictionaries this reads

sum_z K_z^* A_out,z K_z = P A_out P restricted to the input code.

The notation A_out on the right means its compression to the input code, not identification of the two different target Hilbert spaces. A_in minus this compression is P(Delta I+h_e)P, which is positive. Completeness and this identity apply equally to nonbridges and bridges. No fair-sign normalization is inserted on a bridge. The result is a sign-summed statement. Individual normalized bridge outcomes can have different energy changes; positivity of the average does not establish positivity for each selected sign.

## Lifted battery increment and Fourier sign

Use T_u|E>=|E+u>, Fourier transform fhat(tau)=integral exp(+i tau E)f(E)dE. Then T_u is multiplication by exp(+i tau u), battery energy is -i d/dtau, and the lifted branch is

Y_z(tau)=exp(-i tau A_out,z) K_z exp(+i tau A_in).

Differentiating gives -i Y_z^* Y_z' = -Y_z^* A_out,z Y_z + Y_z^*Y_z A_in. Summing and using completeness yields the multiplication operator

sum_z [-i Y_z^*Y_z'] = exp(-i tau A_in) P(Delta I+h_e)P exp(+i tau A_in) >= 0.

Thus sum_z V_z^* E_B V_z-E_B equals this positive multiplication operator on the full-line lift. Its expectation is nonnegative for arbitrary joint matter-battery states, including correlations, whenever the battery moments exist. One must integrate the displayed fiber expectation over tau for a general state; a single scalar tau is not the general joint-state formula. With the opposite Fourier-transform sign, both the representation of E_B and fiber phases reverse, leaving the physical conclusion unchanged. Degenerate head/history storage introduces no extra energy term. Free evolution generated by A_R+E_B commutes with E_B and leaves its mean unchanged.

## Cap and refusal

On any invariant safe support for which the cap removes no outgoing amplitude, the capped instrument equals the full-line lift on that input support and refusal is zero. The preceding nonnegative sign-summed mean battery increment therefore holds there, including correlated states supported in the safe subspace. A state need not be a product packet if its total-energy support already establishes this safety.

Off that domain, joint completion F=(I-sum_z S_z^*S_z)^(1/2) in an absorbing copy with the same total Hamiltonian preserves total-energy distribution, but the full-line fiber formula cannot simply be reused: compression changes it and F need not commute separately with E_B. Total-energy preservation alone does not establish a nonnegative battery increment for the cap/refusal channel. Accordingly the proposed battery monotonicity theorem should explicitly retain its safe-domain restriction. Absorption means no later jumps in the refusal copy; it does not imply that the refusal map leaves the input state, system energy, or battery energy individually unchanged.

For the fixed generator, on safe sectors the sum over live incident edges has nonnegative battery drift at rate gamma, because each edge contribution above is positive. This is ensemble mean drift for the supplied Markov jump law, not monotonicity of individual battery-energy measurements or of every conditioned trajectory.
