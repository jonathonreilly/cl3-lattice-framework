# Goal

Stress the user's named blocker with the smallest reusable finite witness:
repeated local native-edge Record events, surviving matter transport, and one
consistent energy account on the same carrier.

The target contract is conditional and finite. Starting from the supplied
half-filled fixed-N=4 state on the open 2x2x2 cube, five supplied local
endpoint-star events must:

1. follow only live adjacent edges;
2. leave every prefix connected;
3. preserve the native no-reset Record-history algebra;
4. satisfy the local number-current continuity equation;
5. retain current support on at least four live edges at floor 0.02;
6. keep all site densities in [0.10, 0.90], energy negative, and fixed-N
   excess nonnegative; and
7. close one scalar system-plus-ledger balance without resetting the reserve.

This is a bounded support probe. It deliberately leaves formation occurrence,
the scheduler, renewal, apparatus realization, and continuum behavior as
named obligations. It changes no axiom or primitive, and photon work belongs
to another worker.
