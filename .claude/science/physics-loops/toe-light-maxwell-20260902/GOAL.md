# Goal — compact `U(1)` light sector

## Exact target contract

**Target statement.** On a finite periodic `Z^3 x Z_tau` carrier with compact
`U(1)` link phases and a positive isotropic Wilson plaquette action, derive on
one common surface:

1. exact lattice gauge invariance;
2. the compact cube identity `sum(theta_face) = 2 pi m_cube`;
3. the exact Wilson Euler–Lagrange equation `delta sin(theta) = 0`;
4. source-free continuum Maxwell equations on a quantified smooth
   zero-monopole family, with an explicit truncation bound;
5. the quadratic gauge kernel, its gauge null direction, positive physical
   spectrum, two local transverse modes after Gauss reduction at nonzero
   spatial momentum, and the conditional reflection-positive transfer
   dispersion;
6. the isotropic continuum light cone, together with an anisotropic mutation
   showing which premise fixes the speed ratio.

**Quantifiers/domain.** All finite periodic side lengths at least three; all
positive Wilson stiffnesses; all lattice gauge transformations; all nonzero
spatial Brillouin momenta for the local mode count, with temporal-only and full
zero momentum treated separately; smooth sampled continuum potentials
whose plaquette angles stay on one principal branch for the continuum bound.

**Allowed premises.** Lattice and Qubit axioms as background substrate;
registered kinetic-form isotropy only within its declared scope; an explicitly
supplied compact-`U(1)` Wilson action surface; elementary finite-dimensional
linear algebra, Taylor bounds, and finite Fourier analysis.

**Forbidden weakenings.** No scalar-wave relabeling; no deletion of compact
monopole sectors; no sampled-only mode count; no assumption that a four-vector
automatically has two physical polarizations; no hard-coded `beta=6`; no
continuum Maxwell equation inserted as an input; no Record observable claim
without a separate bridge.

**Required edge cases.** Zero momentum and torus harmonic modes; nonzero cube
monopole charge; arbitrary positive overall stiffness; temporal/spatial
anisotropy; gauge-breaking mass mutation; sign-flipped stiffness mutation.

**Completion witness.** A source note with a self-contained proof, a runner
whose computed checks end with `TOTAL: PASS=N FAIL=0`, and a cached output under
6000 characters. The runner must derive its matrices and spectra rather than
compare embedded expected constants to themselves.

**Outcomes that do not count as closure.** Reproducing textbook Maxwell after
assuming the continuum action; proving only `4-1-1=2`; repeating the scalar
wave runner; or showing only one sampled momentum/volume.

## Honest target state

The block targets a bounded theorem on the supplied Wilson action surface. It
does not derive that action from the four axioms, does not identify a physical
electric charge, and does not claim an effective audit status. Its direct
consumer is Lane 8A's missing source-free Maxwell layer.
