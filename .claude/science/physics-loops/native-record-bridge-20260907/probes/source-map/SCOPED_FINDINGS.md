## Physical placement and the sharper fixed-preparation test

The native instrument places virtual vertices at 2v and edge qubits at 2v+e_a. The old planning brief's three consecutive physical sites are wrong: a four-vertex straight path has edge sites (1,0,0),(3,0,0),(5,0,0). The outer edge qubits are not physical nearest neighbors of the middle one. Its six physical nearest neighbors are other sites (spectators in the supplied carrier realization). Graph adjacency, common-vertex incidence, edge-code correlations and physical nearest-neighbor conditions must be distinguished.

Root's prospective fixed encoded-vacuum square test is therefore potentially more discriminating than the old two-preparation control: after a distant fixed first Record, a second edge probability may change while its six physical spectator neighbors are unchanged. PR6358 already covers current-Record sufficiency failures from different preparations, while PR7931 already exhibits whole-edge-history-conditioned sea states. Neither complete-reviewed note supplies this exact fixed-preparation/two-event/midpoint-neighbor statement. This is only the closest-prior assessment; the matrix assertion is root's pending independent test, not a result established by this source map.

A successful negative test would exclude the declared literal-six-neighbor formation identification on its stated history domain. It would not exclude Admissibility: the actual admissible domain, complete neighbor conditions, preparation mechanism and status of the supplied measurement as primitive formation must first be fixed. A mediator remedy must itself derive those physical conditions, not silently replace geometric nearest neighbors by graph neighbors or arbitrary whole-history data.
