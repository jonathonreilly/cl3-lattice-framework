## Result Up Front

The current Record map is insufficient for the exact Block86 family of
arbitrarily prepared live matter inputs under one current-map Markov kernel.

For `m=0` and `m=1`, the prior Record map is identical. Block86's common
no-event atom has mass `2/3`; the disjoint event packets carry the remaining
mass `1/3`. The two normalized full readable laws therefore have TV `1/3`,
while their formation-conditioned packet laws have TV `1`. Any kernel whose
complete causal condition is the current Record map assigns one future
measure to one such map. No write ordering, adaptive scheduler, extra Markov
depth, or post-event `M_2(C)` label can make that one measure equal two
different measures. A law conditioned on complete Record history or other
supplied law-side data is outside this current-map Markov target and remains
live.

The finite local witness is equally sharp. All three Block86 target sites have
the same blank six-neighbor Record condition. Exhausting all six static write
orders finds a conditional-measure collision in every order. `head-root-meta`
is the unique least-conflicting order: writing the `K_minus` head first lets
the Record map identify the adjacent root site, but the root condition still
cannot see the prepared matter bit and is asked to output both `delta_K0` and
`delta_K1`.

That does not kill a Record-only TOE route. A changed theory can start from a
supplied `K_minus` controller Record, generate `m` at the root with one fixed
prior, and then generate `b` at a transverse metadata site with the Block86
conditional row `p(b|m)`. One total time-homogeneous kernel on finite Record
maps has:

- twelve first-step atoms of total mass one;
- ninety-six two-step packet atoms of total mass one;
- exact translation and all-24-frame covariance;
- zero packet-decoding or probability failures;
- three actual six-neighbor condition signatures with one measure each; and
- ninety-six exact terminal fixed points under a third application;
- identity/refusal on every tested off-domain map; and
- stage inferred from the Record map, with no hidden stage bit.

This is a four-route law/ontology decision, not a binary state-type fork. In
the Record-generated theory, `m` is generated at the root rather than read
from a live input. The prepared family may instead use a persistent
preparation Record, a complete Record-history/law-side condition, or a narrow
readable-state/causal-prestate distinction. The theorem rules out only the
same-current-map Markov realization.

The Block91 34-outcome lift is gated off because the exact one-event prepared
family fails first. The existing one-site algebra can encode 34 labels; label
capacity is not the issue.

No core axiom edit is forced. No TOE percentage moves. There is zero
obligation retirement, and the retained-positive end-to-end theory count
remains zero.

## 4. General Record-Sufficiency Theorem

Let `S_R` be the space of complete Record maps, and let `K` be any Markov
kernel on `S_R` whose condition is the current Record map. For one initial
state `R_0`, every finite-depth future law

```text
K^t(R_0, .)
```

is one uniquely defined probability measure. Internal scheduler randomness
is already integrated into that measure.

The Block86 prepared `m=0` and `m=1` inputs present the same `R_0`. Write
`q=1/3` for its positive formation mass, `delta_empty` for the common no-event
Record atom, and `nu_m` for the normalized formation-conditioned packet law.
Then

```text
mu_m = (1-q) delta_empty + q nu_m,
TV(nu_0,nu_1)=1,
TV(mu_0,mu_1)=q=1/3.
```

Both full laws have total mass one, both conditioning events have positive
mass `q`, and the root contents make the conditional supports disjoint. No
one measure can equal both distinct full laws. This is the
**Record-sufficiency theorem** for the exact prepared-live-input family under
a current-Record-map Markov kernel.

The proof is not limited to three sequential writes. It covers adaptive
schedulers, arbitrary hidden implementation randomness that is not part of
causal state, extra no-event retries, and finite or infinite iteration before
the final packet is read. The same initial Record state has one future
measure. It does not cover complete Record-history conditioning, a supplied
law-domain side condition, or a larger causal prestate.

The theorem does not rule out generating `m` as a Record outcome. That changes
the target from an arbitrary prepared family to one fixed law over `m` and is
constructed next.

The ninety-six two-step packet atoms sum to one. Translating the controller

Only three local signatures occur:

```text
one K_minus neighbor -> (1/2,1/2) on (K_0,K_1),
```

Thus the stage is inferred from the Record map. The runner executes all twelve

Every other off-domain map is an identity/refusal state. The formula is
