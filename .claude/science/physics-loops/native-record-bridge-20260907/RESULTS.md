## 1. Exact communication cost for a fixed native Record law

The native encoding puts virtual vertices at `2v` and physical edge qubits at `2v+e_a`. This corrects the prior campaign's prospective three-site geometry: consecutive virtual edges are not consecutive physical nearest neighbors. The tested square has target sites `(1,0,0)`, `(2,1,0)`, `(1,2,0)`, `(0,1,0)`. All six physical neighbors of each target remain unchanged spectators in the stipulated native instrument.

In the supplied encoded vacuum, the four ideal edge measurements return one common fair sign. Every fixed ordering has only the all-plus and all-minus outcomes, each with probability `1/2`. After recording one target, another target's conditional probability changes by one despite its unchanged six-neighbor condition. This is an exact obstruction to identifying this entire stipulated history-conditioned instrument with a kernel of those unchanged conditions. It is not an obstruction to arbitrary quantum dynamics, different condition semantics, or the axioms themselves.

For two targets, the closest unrestricted product of two binary laws remains at total variation distance `sqrt(2)-1`. If the first marginal must remain fair, the distance is `1/2` for every second-site bias. These bounds concern the specified product competitor class.

There is also a positive construction. For an initially blank graph, fixed unchanging unrecorded background, and a chosen outcome-independent event order, assume every full-history conditional law depends only on earlier nearest-neighbor Records. The minimum extra sites needed to generate a common fair bit on a target set is its vertex-Steiner overhead: the smallest number of additional sites making a connected induced graph containing the targets. Component factorization proves the lower bound; a spanning-tree order and local copying rule attain it. A hidden shared seed, invisible quantum interaction, existing background Records, or an incompatible prescribed schedule changes this problem.

For the native square, the cost is exactly **one**: the center `(1,1,0)` relays the first fair sign to the four targets. Only `48/120` orders of these five sites work; the other 72 were retained as failures of that schedule choice. This reproduces the four target probabilities for this preparation. It adds a readable mediator Record and does not reproduce the full native quantum channel or arbitrary CAR states.

Evidence: [root proof](probes/native-instrument/ROOT_DERIVATION.md), [independent native review](probes/native-instrument-cold-review/), and [independent relay proof](probes/record-relay-review/). Finite checks cover all 384 ordered native histories, with separate exact signed-Pauli and relay computations. The general graph result rests on the displayed proof, not on a finite census.

## 2. Probability freedom survives removal of a shared spatial frame

`p_kappa(I|c) = 1/2 + kappa*q/12`, for `kappa=1/3` or `2/3`.

They are positive and normalized on the full declared condition domain, with translation and cubic rotation covariance and invariance under independent matrix conjugations. A single `I` neighbor gives respectively `19/36` and `5/9`. Thus a common frame is unnecessary for this particular conditional witness of freedom. The interpretation of matrix elements as physical possibilities remains an assumption; this is not a ratification of the framework's possibility semantics.

For the restricted three-symbol neighbor alphabet `{blank,0,I}`, the 24 proper cubic rotations have **57** orbits on the 729 conditions. Consequently the covariant binary scalar-output laws on that restricted domain form a 57-parameter cube. This count does not classify laws on arbitrary matrix-valued conditions. A separate finite-support argument requires independent local conjugation naturality and says only scalar matrices can belong to a finite invariant output set; it does not exclude continuous noncentral distributions.

## 3. Native parity and the missing physical boundary functional

The connected native square code represents the even global CAR sector. For the supplied commuting two-dimer Hamiltonian at `tau=log(2)`, the one-particle transfer eigenvalues are `(2,1/2,2,1/2)`. The exact traces are

`Z_even=41/4`, `D_plus=det(I+A)=81/4`, `D_minus=det(I-A)=1/4`,

with `Z_even=(D_plus+D_minus)/2`. Normalizing by their own zero-time dimensions gives `41/32` and `81/64`, so their difference is not removed by that normalization.

One additional inert leaf mode, encoded using five physical `M2` edge qubits and the even five-mode sector, restores `D_plus` under the full mathematical trace. No `M4` site or duplicate conjugate sector is needed for this finite construction. But weighting the occupied leaf sector by `q` instead gives

`Z(q) = Z_even + q*Z_odd = 41/4 + 10q`.

At `q=2`, normalization by the corresponding zero-time trace gives `121/96`, which differs from `81/64` by `-1/192`. The full trace has `q=1` by definition; interpreting it as the physical closure requires a supplier for that boundary preparation/functional. Zero leaf energy alone does not supply probabilities.

This diagnostic can become blind: after normalization, dependence on `q` disappears whenever `det(I-A)=0` throughout the tested matter family. In particular, an inert matter mode with transfer eigenvalue one can mask the boundary choice. The tested two-dimer matter example avoids that degeneracy. This is a conditional finite action/measure interface result, not a derived physical measure or flavor/mass prediction.
