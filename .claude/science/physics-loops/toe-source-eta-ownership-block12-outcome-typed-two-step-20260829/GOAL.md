# Goal

Source/Eta Block 12 tests the panel-ranked constructive escape from the
Block-11 quantum-program boundary.  One permanent predecessor Record carries
the realized outcome and the physical front in a single covariant `M2`
content, while five consumable neighboring conditions carry the continuous
Block-10 action/quadrupole packet.  The event writes one new permanent Record
and shifts those five live conditions forward along disjoint nearest-neighbor
edges.  The next target must then be selected and conditioned by the same
rule, with no supplied tape, role, epoch, site, scheduler, or global clock.

This is a joint Record/live/history test.  It is not another static carrier
or positivity campaign.  The exact decision is whether outcome typing,
causal packet transport, and two-event prefix consistency coexist in one
fixed covariant rule.

## Frozen outcome and Record code

Let the fourteen outcome labels be the six axis directions and eight normalized
corner directions

```text
B = {+/-e_x,+/-e_y,+/-e_z} union {c/sqrt(3): c in {+/-1}^3}.
```

For a front direction `f` in the six axis directions and outcome direction
`s_b` in `B`, freeze

```text
g = 9/16,
epsilon = 1/256,
r_(f,b) = -g f + epsilon s_b.                         (1)
```

The Record alternatives in this block are the encoded contents `r_(f,b)`.
The fourteen labels, their probabilities, and their spatial moment are frozen
from Block 09, but their post-formation `M2` contents are now the injective
mixed-state code (1), not the earlier pure direction representatives.  This
semantic change must remain explicit.  It is allowed only if Record content
alone recovers `(f,b)` and the same map is used at every event and frame.

The literal-pure comparator `r_(f,b)=s_b` is frozen as a hostile control.  A
failure of that comparator closes only that assignment inside the displayed
carrier; it is not a no-go for other Record codes or larger blocks.

## Frozen hybrid carrier

Let `F` be the full real `3x3` odd-shell matrix carrying
`A1+T1+E+T2`, with the Block-10 gains and decoder unchanged.  At a prospective
event site `x`, the predecessor Record lies at `x-f`.  For predecessor outcome
`a`, freeze the six neighboring Bloch contents

```text
v_(-f) = r_(f,a),
v_(+f) = 2 F f + r_(f,a),
v_n    = F n                     for n perpendicular to f. (2)
```

No literal `+y` is a runtime input.  `+y` is only the canonical proof chart;
every physical frame rotates `F`, `f`, `s_b`, and all contents together.
The front is recovered from the unique predecessor Record content and its
lattice displacement.

The runner must prove exactly that the odd-shell decoder of (2) is `F`, has
rank nine, preserves the Block-09 probability law and zero action leakage,
and remains strictly physical for H1, held-out H2, their proper-cubic images,
and every vertex of the registered nine-dimensional box.  No gain or support
may be changed after this registration.

## One event and the generated successor shell

Condition on Record formation at the unique unrecorded site selected by the
newest Record's decoded front.  The distribution over `b` is the unchanged
Block-09 fourteen-way law evaluated on (2).  After outcome `b`:

1. write the permanent Record content `r_(f,b)` at `x`;
2. shift each of the five non-Record condition contents at
   `x+f` and `x+/-e_1,x+/-e_2` to the corresponding site one step farther in
   direction `f` by five disjoint nearest-neighbor SWAPs;
3. leave every earlier Record unchanged.

The SWAP layer is triggered by the Record-formation instrument, once, after
the probability and outcome are fixed.  It is not a time-homogeneous static
Record trigger and may not be replaced by a persistent role/epoch bit.  The
five destinations are ordinary no-Record sites with arbitrary pre-event
contents; the SWAPs transport those prior contents backward, so no preferred
blank quantum state or preloaded future packet is assumed.

The forward source site becomes the next no-Record target.  Its neighboring
packet is the transported five-cell packet plus the new backward Record.  The
runner must derive, rather than insert, the successor matrix

```text
F' = F + (r_(f,a)-r_(f,b)) f^T / 2.                   (3)
```

and prove that the unchanged transported cells have exactly the form (2) with
`(F',b)`.  The same local rule must then execute the second event.

## Prefix and induction target

For a history `b_0,...,b_(N-1)`, define each conditional probability from the
actual transported shell and update `F` only by (3).  Require:

- strict positivity and normalization at every step;
- exact cylinder prefix marginals by summing the last outcome;
- one Record per visited site, permanence of the full trail, and no overwrite;
- exactly one forward no-Record site selected by the newest Record while every
  older Record points to an already recorded successor;
- no growth of the quantum packet beyond the same five transported `M2`
  contents;
- an induction proof for every finite prefix if the two-step identities close.

Formation occurrence is axiom content, but this block does not derive a
formation rate, physical time interval, or a microscopic realized-draw
mechanism.  The event-triggered post-formation SWAP layer is candidate dynamics
constructed here, not an imported axiom.

- `OUTCOME-TYPED-TWO-STEP`: the encoded predecessor outcome is permanent and

`OUTCOME-TYPED-TWO-STEP`, but it does not create a fourth terminal.
