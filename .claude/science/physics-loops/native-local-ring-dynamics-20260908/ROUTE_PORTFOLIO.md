# Routes and disposition

Order5 is a valid local bound but diverges as epsilon^-7 under its generic velocity at ringtime. A finite order13 construction gives a vanishing epsilon bound. Canonical order14 gives epsilon². The slow comparison L keeps all higher D-conserving terms, removing only zero/scalar-on-ice lower terms. Native auxiliary compression was a valid independent route but is unnecessary and not used. Optimal-prethermal normal forms remain separate context.
