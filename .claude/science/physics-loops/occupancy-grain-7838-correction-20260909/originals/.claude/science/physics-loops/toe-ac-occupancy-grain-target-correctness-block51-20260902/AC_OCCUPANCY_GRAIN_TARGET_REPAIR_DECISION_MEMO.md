# AC occupancy-grain target-repair decision memo

## Decision

Do not edit an axiom.  Do not mark the obligation closed.  When the obligation
owner next revises its formal wording, replace the bare determinant-power fork
with a sector-support-typed construction target:

> Derive the physical charged-lepton carrier, action, measure, and K/CPT
> action; prove a presentation-independent factorization
> `Z_phys=Z_s F_d^n Z_rest` in which the singlet factor and normalization are
> held fixed; and derive whether `n=1` (one K/CPT-orbit cell) or `n=2` (two
> independently physical channel cells).  Track any global conjugate copy
> separately: it changes the relative occupancy grain only when its support is
> anisotropic between the singlet and doublet sectors.  Do not insert the
> desired charged-lepton value or readout dictionary.

## Why

`det_C` versus `|det_C|^2` is decisive only when the symbol denotes a
sector-local doublet factor with the singlet held fixed.  Applied to the full
carrier, it scales both exponents together and cannot select their ratio.

Current axioms and approved primitives intentionally leave the physical
action, measure, K/CPT codec, and support-to-energy map downstream.  The
realized-state primitive additionally prevents a supplied state slot from
being mistaken for a forced charged-lepton weight.  The missing content is a
physics theorem or an explicit owner choice, not a wording trick.
