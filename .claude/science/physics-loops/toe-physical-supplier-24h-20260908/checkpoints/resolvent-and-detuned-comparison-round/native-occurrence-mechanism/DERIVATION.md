## What is and is not supplied

The new finite comb construction avoids the static-degree fresh-leaf obstruction without reusing any Record. It derives BOTH finite positive-click and no-positive-click operations from one detector Hamiltonian plus the explicitly supplied permanent readout/transport schedule. It does NOT derive when the two Z Records form: under the supplied schedule they both form on the first round even at g=0. Consequently a physical FIRST RECORD law is still not generated by this interaction. Turning a zero outcome into an unrecorded blank would change the primitive instrument; suppressing readout until a click presupposes the desired event selector. An autonomous transported bath/detector could be a next mechanism, but its clock, preparation and Record criterion are not present in this finite model.

The main weak-registration stack already assumes fresh registers and constructed registration per tick, with durable covariance transcription; this attempt adds actual native leaf deletion and moving-window capacity accounting, not a new proof of that stack's band law. Strong outcome: conditional finite-click instrument and a concrete permanence-compatible transport escape. Remaining hard supplier: autonomous physical occurrence/readout with bounded-strength transport, not another scalar hazard fit. No formal no-go package or audit verdict is proposed.
