## Checked primary prior art and applicability

Chandrasekharan and Wiese, Quantum Link Models, hep-lat/9609042v2, section3 equations3.5–3.17, explicitly construct U(1) link operators from spin raising/lowering operators and electric generators from spin-z divergences, including the two-dimensional link representation. This is the directly applicable standard algebraic precedent for a spin-half U1 quantum link. It does not prove the native embedding, supplied domain, fermionic phases, or a continuum phase. Their own discussion separates this finite link construction from continuum/dimensional-reduction arguments. Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-lat/9609042.

Zohar and Cirac, Eliminating fermionic matter fields in lattice gauge theories,1805.05347, sectionsII–IV, equations7–10 and the normal-Z2/representation qualification around sectionIII, supplies the relevant precedent that gauge constraints can transfer fermionic statistics into gauge variables. Its displayed U1 example uses a unitary link operator and [E,U]=U; its method requires an appropriate parity operator, not merely the name U1. The spin-half ladder is nonunitary, so I do not import the paper's whole theorem verbatim. The anticommutation of electric parity with a spin-half ladder can be checked directly, but that alone is not a native dictionary proof. Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05347.

Zohar and Cirac, Removing Staggered Fermionic Matter in U(N) and SU(N) Lattice Gauge Theories,1905.00652, sectionIII equations24–30 and37–58, is the closest premise comparison. It emphasizes unique determination of matter by charge; multiple charge-degenerate matter states obstruct direct removal. Finite matter spaces leave local electric constraints and projectors, and fermionic signs must survive in the transformed interactions. Its initial U1 gauge carrier is rotor-like; its matter example is staggered single-species, not this opposite-charge no-double three-state space. Thus the candidate resembles inverse constrained matter elimination, but its carrier and intertwiner still require their own proof. Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.00652.

These papers establish substantial standard methodology. No claim of historical priority follows from this limited search or from a failure to find the exact native formula.
