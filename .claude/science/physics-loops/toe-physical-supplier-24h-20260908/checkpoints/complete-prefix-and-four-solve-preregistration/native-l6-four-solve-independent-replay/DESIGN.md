## Chunk-order correction

Root identified a real floating-order defect in the original gather implementation, before any physical replay. The producer visits source chunks in increasing order, then coefficient rows in their original order. For a fixed aligned destination chunk of power-two width C, toggling a bit below log2(C) leaves its source chunk unchanged; toggling a higher stored bit sends the entire destination chunk to exactly one source chunk. The implicit top bit leaves the stored index unchanged. Therefore every destination's contribution chronology is recovered by sorting rows by `(source_chunk, original_row_index)`, where source_chunk is `(destination_start xor 2**j)//C` for stored j and destination_start//C for the top bit. The revised gather uses precisely that order, retaining independent source-index reconstruction.
