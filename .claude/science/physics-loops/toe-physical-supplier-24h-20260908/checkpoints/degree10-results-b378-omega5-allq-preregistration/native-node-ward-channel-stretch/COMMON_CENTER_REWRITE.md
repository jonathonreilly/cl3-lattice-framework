# An exact common-center rewrite, with no positivity conclusion

This is a further algebraic identity to investigate, not an evaluated scalar. Write g=gamma0, H=H0 with H Omega=0, and C=[H,g]=J_all/2. Native pair perturbations anticommute with g and J_A=[g,B_A]=2gB_A=-2B_Ag. Because R_A D_A=D_A R_A=-I, each single ordered soft-Ward word

 F_CA=R_C R_A+(1/2)R_C J_C R_C g R_A-(1/2)R_C g R_A J_A R_A

can be rewritten as

 F_CA=(1/2)(g R_C g R_A+R_C g R_A g)
 +(1/2)(R_C C R_C g R_A-R_C g R_A C R_A).                     (1)

Equation(1) is an operator identity, before taking any vacuum bracket. To check it, use either placement J_C=2gB_C or -2B_Cg and average the resulting expressions; do the same for J_A. The two resulting -R_C R_A pieces cancel the original direct term. The four remaining H terms combine into [H,g] and [g,H]. No commutation of distinct inverses is used.

Thus the pair-dependent local J_A source can be exchanged for the common free source C, at the cost of replacing the direct overlap by two center-conjugated overlaps. C is anti-Hermitian, has norm sqrt6 h, and C Omega=H g Omega is a one-particle vector. Its free energy moments are native center moments. This may help a moment-based bound but does not make either term positive, and does not remove the center boundary vectors. The exact original Ward and channel expressions remain the sign target.
