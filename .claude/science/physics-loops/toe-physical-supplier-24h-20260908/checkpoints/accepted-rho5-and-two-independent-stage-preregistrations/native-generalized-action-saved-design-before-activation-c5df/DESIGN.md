## Sign-selected integer multiplication and streaming extension

Whole d493 version is preserved separately. For intervals [x,y],[u,v], endpoint products are selected by nine sign classes. Nonnegative/nonnegative selects xu,yv; nonnegative/nonpositive selects yu,xv; nonnegative/straddling selects yu,yv. Nonpositive/nonnegative selects xv,yu; nonpositive/nonpositive selects yv,xu; nonpositive/straddling selects xv,xu. Straddling/nonnegative selects xv,yv; straddling/nonpositive selects yu,xu; both-straddling selects min(xv,yu),max(xu,yv). These follow monotonicity of multiplication on each orthant; zeros are covered by either closed adjacent class. Only the final class needs two comparisons.
