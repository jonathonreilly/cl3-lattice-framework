## Smallest informative next experiment

Start with L2, not L8. Use the actual864-state seed component, V=.95, free endpoints, and two fixed even path lengths n8 and32. This keeps a direct finite-G reference available and makes an algorithmic failure separable from an unknown thermodynamic result. No V0 endpoint comparison is needed to establish this baseline; add neither V nor size after inspecting outcomes. V0 could be a separately preregistered subsequent calibration, not a replacement for failed .95.

For each n use two initial path families and two burn lengths, giving eight arms. Keep16 independent chains per arm. Provisional fixed counts are burn16 and64 nominal spatial sweeps, followed by128 measured sweeps. A sweep is M=24 FACE BLOCKS, not one block, not one elementary plaquette-time update. These counts are a proposed calibration budget rather than justified equilibration times. Freeze numerical values only after a nonphysics cost forecast; do not shorten a completed run or choose a favorable count afterward. Suggested aggregate cap600s and180s/384MiB per independently restartable job, with prospective forecast<=480s and<=120s/job. If this does not fit, stop at the cost result and redesign before data; do not silently reduce chains or retain only successful arms.

Initial family A is the constant seed path. Family B is an exact stationary product-G path initialized using the full L2 oracle: normalized backward powers h_i=G^(n-i)1; draw x0 from h0 and subsequent states with G(x_i,y)h_(i+1)(y). This is computationally finite on864 states, not a scalable sampler or a thermal-state oracle for L8. Its purpose is to distinguish failure of the chain to relax from corruption of an already stationary law. Independent initialization randomness is essential. A deterministic legal seed-to-x0 witness is retained from component traversal. Both families are exactly in the same component, not merely in the zero-flux sector. FamilyB has no claim of exact machine randomness: the oracle uses explicitly bounded finite arithmetic, with L2 reference residuals checked before sampling.

This stationary-start diagnostic is more discriminating than two arbitrary nonstationary starts agreeing. In particular, both ordinary starts can remain in the same metastable region; agreement would not detect that. Length n32 is not claimed comparable to the previous L8 natural projector; its role is to expose growing temporal correlations at a known target.

## Frozen update and measurement law

Select every face independently and uniformly from all24 geometric labels using a dedicated selection RNG. Distinct geometric labels with equal L2 masks remain separate in this law and in G; do not deduplicate the physical transfer multiplicity. Use independent supplied uniforms for each whole-time conditional draw. Initializer, face selection and conditional-draw RNG streams have separate frozen namespaces, and no stream replacement is allowed. Record seeds and all configuration/runtime hashes before data.

Measure once per completed sweep, after its24 blocks, regardless of whether the state changed. State-independent thinning preserves stationarity in expectation. It does not make snapshots independent. Never trigger measurements only after a change, successful birth or face visit. All physical entries are measured at the same snapshots. Sixteen equal batches of eight snapshots are within-chain diagnostics, not additional replicas.

For each chain record the full per-face selection histogram, counts of unchanged blocks, changed time slices per block, and changes by selected face. Missing a face means missing coverage, even if other faces were repeatedly updated. A nominal sweep does not guarantee all-face coverage. These counters diagnose execution and opportunity, not equilibrium. Require every geometric face to have been selected at least once over the measured interval; report min/max/entire histogram. Do not demand equal counts or force corrective visits after seeing omissions. Such a coverage gate is necessary bookkeeping, not a sufficient mixing test.

## Physical observables and exact targets

Freeze a modest vector rather than scanning for the most significant channel. At each snapshot retain midpoint NF, endpoint mean h=(V-1)(NF_left+NF_right)/2, the pooled transverse S at q=pi using the validated literal complex source, S*h, and h_left*h_right. This permits direct finite-projector reference and residual reporting with joint covariance. Keep signed variance estimates, including negative ones; no clipping or automatic bound certificate.

Add three memory-sensitive diagonal entries: average NF over ALL n+1 slices; endpoint overlap C_end=(1/(3L³)) sum_e (2x_0e-1)(2x_ne-1); and temporal variation A_time=(1/(3L³ n)) sum_{i,e} 1[x_ie!=x_(i+1)e]. C_end and A_time detect long temporal plateaux which midpoint NF alone can miss. Freeze electric corner intensity sums at k=(pi,pi,pi), and face-plane anisotropy using the reviewed order module, as two spatial-order diagnostics. These are diagonal and invariant under arbitrary state amplitudes; no pointwise wavefunction ratio is imported.

Compute every target from the finite G oracle before sampling. Single-slice insertions use 1^T G^i X G^(n-i)1 / Z. Pair-slice observables use two insertions; endpoint overlap is a sum of diagonal edge-sign pairs. A_time uses a marked transition kernel G(x,y) times the actual Hamming difference, with free powers on both sides. These are finite matrix-power calculations, not enumeration of864^(n+1) paths. Store oracle source, exact/rational or residual-controlled values and all numerical error bounds before data. No claim that V=.95 mixed NF equals pure NF is used.

If implementation cost makes the proposed vector impractical, change the proposal before any samples. Do not discard a slow diagnostic after learning that it disagrees.

## Statistical units and prespecified disposition

Independent chains are the units. Retain128 raw snapshot vectors and16 batch vectors per chain. For each arm publish the16-chain mean vector, full chain covariance, covariance of the mean divided by16, and influence vectors for nonlinear quantities. Initializer families and burn arms use independent streams; differences use independent-arm covariance. The same chain's first-half versus second-half contrast is paired and uses its actual chain-level contrast covariance. Never treat128 snapshots or16 batches as independent chains.

Prospectively use nominal4SE exact-reference consistency for EVERY frozen primary entry and the D/residual derived entries. Require4SE<=15% of the absolute target for strictly positive scale observables NF,S,averageNF,A_time. For quantities whose target can vanish, freeze a nonzero absolute precision scale from their mathematical range before data (for example0.05 for C_end and normalized order intensities); do not divide by a noisy or zero estimated mean. Report all individual flags and acknowledge their multiplicity: nominal4SE is not familywise guaranteed coverage. No p-value shopping or largest-channel phase statement.

Fail the calibration if any integrity/resource/face-coverage gate fails; if any stationary-start exact-reference consistency check fails; if either long-burn seed-start arm fails exact consistency or its frozen precision gates; or if a long-burn seed/stationary comparison or paired half-window contrast exceeds4SE. Short-burn failures remain reported rather than replaced. If long burn passes while short burn fails, the conclusion is only a finite-budget sensitivity result, not a proof that64 sweeps suffice generally. No automatic burn doubling, replacement seeds or additional arms follows.

The finite-oracle arm provides an important diagnostic split: stationary-start disagreement points to implementation, oracle, arithmetic or uncertainty failure; seed-only disagreement points toward relaxation problems but does not by itself isolate a unique cause. A stationary-start passing mean can still have huge autocorrelation and poor precision. Passing all checks supports only this finite L2 calibration at these n values.

## Gate before any L8 scientific study

Even a clean L2 calibration is insufficient for L8. Next would be a separately frozen intermediate-size test (for example L4 at two short n values chosen prospectively), with two source-component-certified nonconstant starts: a propagated RK/Q path and a physically contrasting path generated by a recorded sequence of legal whole-time face flips or graph moves. A different flux is not a same-component control. A deterministic symmetry-related seed alone may share its trapping mechanism and is not a strong contrast. Actual overlap, temporal variation, order-vector distributions and independent-chain precision must be compared.

Only after measured per-block cost, whole-sweep coverage, initializer contrasts and increasing-n behavior exist should an L8 budget be proposed. The packed prototype's full validation and message-object overhead must be included. An interior algorithm can erase every old coordinate label while still preserving slow spatial order or path plateaux. No finite collection of these diagnostics certifies total-variation mixing, global ergodic times, a ground-state limit, phase continuity, confinement or photon poles. A rigorous rate would require additional spectral/conductance/coupling mathematics; this design is falsifiable evidence and explicit failure handling, not that theorem.
