# Prospective directed boundary-forgetting lemma

This is root research, not a canonical theorem or uniqueness result for a bilateral Gibbs specification. It uses the two-parent formation kernel beta(s|a,b)=phi(s,a)phi(s,b)/sum_t phi(t,a)phi(t,b), strictly positive symmetric six-menu orbit weights, and a supplied top-left directed formation order. An exact preliminary exhaustive local calculation gives one-parent TV coefficients c=30/143,65/434,910/5609 at(3,1,2),(5,2,4),(7,3,5). These are hypotheses until the replay below is independently checked. They have 2c<1 even where prior static Z3 one-site contraction criteria are silent; the objects differ.

Consider any finite rectangle with arbitrary fixed incoming top and left boundary values, and generate its interior with beta. Couple two such boundary problems by sequential maximal coupling at each site. Let d(i,j) be disagreement probability. Triangle inequality through an intermediate parent pair gives d(i,j)<=c[d(i-1,j)+d(i,j-1)], with d<=1 on incoming boundaries. Induction gives d(i,j)<=(2c)^min(i,j): if i=j, both preceding exponents are i-1; if i<j, one is i-1 and one i, whose weighted sum is at most (2c)^i because 2c<=1. The j<i case is symmetric. More sharply a killed north-west walk representation solves the recursion. For a finite observation set at directed depth at least r, joint TV between any boundary laws is at most |A|(2c)^r by the coupling union bound, capped by1.

Compare arbitrary boundary data with the correlated incoming row/column law inherited from the already projectively consistent plane law. Conditioning on those incoming values produces beta on the interior by the finite rectangle directed factorization. The same uniform bound then shows that arbitrary incoming boundaries approach that plane law as both distances recede. A stationary unilateral field compatible with this finite-DAG conditional factorization must agree with this limit. This is uniqueness within the explicitly directed compatible class, not a proof that every Gibbs measure of the expanded diagonal potential has this causal factorization.

This does not supply a physical infinite formation schedule, an event clock, perfect sampling, all-parameter ergodicity, a selected diagonal, static Z3 uniqueness, or a Gaussian/quantum action. Pairwise maximal couplings do not automatically give a simultaneous grand coupling or coupling-from-the-past sampler.

The strict threshold matters: beta(s|a,b)=(delta(s,a)+delta(s,b))/2 on a two-state menu has c=1/2. All-zero and all-one incoming boundaries remain distinct throughout the interior. It is a nonpositive boundary control, not a counterexample to the positive six-menu hypotheses. Positive noisy copies have 2c<1 and fit the argument.

Prior mathematical context: De Santis and Piccioni, Perfect simulation for unilateral fields (2004), https://arxiv.org/html/math/0402136 , introduction defines uniform loss of boundary dependence and separates stationary fields from easy Markovian-boundary simulation. Its more demanding simultaneous coupling/percolation results are not imported or claimed here. The displayed elementary pairwise contraction is rederived above.
