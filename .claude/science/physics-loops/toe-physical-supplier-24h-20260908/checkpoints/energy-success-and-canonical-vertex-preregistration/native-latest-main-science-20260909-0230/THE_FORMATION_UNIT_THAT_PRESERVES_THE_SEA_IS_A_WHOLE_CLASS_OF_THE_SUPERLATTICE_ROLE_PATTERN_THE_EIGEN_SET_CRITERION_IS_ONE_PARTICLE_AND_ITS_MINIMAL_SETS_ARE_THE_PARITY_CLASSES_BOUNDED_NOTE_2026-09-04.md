## Definitions and exact regular-set criterion

For a corner set S let `R=union_(v in S) star(v)` and
`S'={u: star(u) subset R}`. A set is regular when the Z strings supported in R that
commute with every face stabilizer are generated by the `B_v` for v in S'.
Their binary rank is `r_B=|S'|` for a proper closure and `r_B=|V|-1` for `S'=V`
on the connected graphs and parity sector used here. The rank definition is authoritative;
the empty complement at full closure is treated separately from a connected-complement description.
An eigen-set means every nonzero edge-pattern branch is an `H_R` eigenvector.

Let W have orthonormal columns spanning N occupied orbitals, `K=WW†`.
For odd V the numerical convention rounds V/2 up to an allowed even particle number;
this is a declared finite code choice, not exact half filling or a filling theorem.
For `S'=S1 disjoint-union S0`, put `Z=ker W[S1,:]` and
`U0=(W Z)[V minus S',:]`. The pattern is live **iff both**

1. `rank W[S1,:]=|S1|`, and
2. `rank U0=N-|S1|`.

The first condition is the nonzero occupied contraction; the second is nonzero survival
under the empty-site projection. Dimensions after restriction alone are insufficient:
`W=e_0`, `S1={1}`, `S0={0}` has remaining dimension zero but zero probability.
Small probabilities such as `2^-64` are representable in double precision; they merely
fall below a chosen `1e-13` threshold. Liveness uses orbital rank rather than that cutoff.

For a live pattern let U be an orthonormal basis of U0 and `h_R=h[V minus S',V minus S']`.
For a regular set, the exact finite formula is

`residual = ||(I-UU†)h_R U||_F`, `energy=Tr(U†h_R U)`,

`P(edge pattern w)=2^{-(|R|-r_B)} p(n_(S'))`,

where `p(n)=det(diag(n) K_(S'S') + diag(1-n)(I-K_(S'S')))`, including **occupied
and empty** sites of the specified pattern. In particular the full cube has gauge factor
`2^{-(12-7)}=1/32`, not 1/16. The full-pattern probability is different from the
occupied-only marginal `det K_(S1S1)`.

**Proof.** Expand the edge projector in Z strings. The subgroup commuting with the
face stabilizers has `2^r_B` elements. Its `2^{|R|-r_B}` cosets applied to the code sea
lie in mutually orthogonal syndrome sectors, commute with `H_R`, and have equal norms.
Thus they have the same residual and energy as the occupation projection, with the stated
uniform gauge factor. The occupation projection contracts the occupied rows of W and
then restricts the remaining orbitals, giving the two rank conditions. On their exterior
product, the quadratic Hamiltonian gives its trace times the state plus orthogonal
single-orbital replacements by `(I-UU†)h_R u_j`. Their squared norms sum to the
Frobenius residual squared. This proves the regular-set equivalence.
For an irregular set there are additional component-parity projections commuting with
`H_R`; the one-particle criterion is sufficient, and failure is not a many-body exclusion.
