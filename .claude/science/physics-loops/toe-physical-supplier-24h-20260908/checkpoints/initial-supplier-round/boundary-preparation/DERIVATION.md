In the physical edge-Z basis, every source A and B is real. Hence

    conjugate(T_e)=−T_e,
    conjugate(H)=−H

    conjugate(exp(−itH))=exp(+it conjugate(H))=exp(−itH).

Every pulse U and every Record projector is real. Therefore each unnormalized conditional word K rho K^dagger is real if rho is real; positive Born normalization preserves reality. Induction includes arbitrary finite outcome-adaptive schedules, mixtures and all deletion histories. No number-sector or Gaussian assumption is required. Real supplied ancillary inputs and the same controls on a larger graph do not break this invariant.

For a fixed nonzero real hopping H on a real invariant code P and beta>0, define only as a mathematical comparator

    rho_beta=P exp(−beta H)/Z_beta.

Conjugation maps it to rho_−beta. Spectral symmetry gives Z_beta=Z_−beta. Equality would force exp(−beta H)=exp(+beta H) on the code, hence H=0 there. Thus a nontrivial Gibbs state is outside the above reachable class.
