# Exact path-time energy balance for the proposed diagnostic

For the finite seed-reachable component, H=V Nf-A and Z(T)=1^T exp(-TH)1. A labeled path with n nonself flips, rescaled ordered times 0<s1<...<sn<1, has weight T^n exp(-VT integral_0^1 Nf(x(s))ds). Differentiating the absolutely convergent finite-matrix Dyson series gives

-E(T)=d log Z/dT=<n>/T-V<time_average_NF>.

Also H1=(V-1)Nf pointwise, so E(T)=<(h(left)+h(right))/2>, where h(x)=(V-1)Nf(x). Thus

<B>=0, B=V*time_average_NF-physical_event_count/T-endpoint_h.

This holds at any finite T and volume under the stationary free-endpoint CT target; it is not a ground-limit statement. All three terms are already recorded together in the L2 twelve-vector, and their covariance must be retained. The identity does not uniquely characterize the target and passing it does not establish mixing. At RK it reduces to <n>/T=<NF>, with endpoint h identically zero.
