In the simultaneous face-projector basis W_j conjugates the hit faces to their good projectors. Its off-block norm is bounded by the event on C minus F_j. The contour constant is valid. For precision, encode a connected m-edge set through the fixed vertex by a deterministic Euler tour of the multigraph obtained by doubling ALL its edges. The tour has exactly2m steps, at most6 choices each, and its used-edge set reconstructs the original. Thus36^m is an injective-encoding bound. A spanning-tree traversal alone would not encode extra cycle edges, so the source wording should be read in this all-edge sense. Combining r^ceil(m/8)<=r^(m/8) yields a=36(16U/kappa_*)^(1/4), and summing m>=ell gives a^ell/(1-a), optionally capped at1. No winding-only conclusion follows.

The counterexample is realizable on the stated even tori: uniform link signs have zero elementary flux; canonical staggered signs have pi flux. Cube and global-plane constraints hold (even transverse area). In the exact Z2 carrier representation, choose an even-parity matter vector and project each link-X representative with all Gauss projectors. The only gauge action fixing every link sign on the connected graph is the global action, which acts positively on this matter parity. Hence these projections are nonzero physical states in the required magnetic sectors. Their mixture has all joint nonempty defect probabilities p, regardless of set size. A coherent superposition has the same probabilities because flux sectors are orthogonal. It is expressly not an interacting ground-state counterexample; it refutes inference from marginal/mean information alone.

A usable weakening: for any target event C, LC is needed only on the finite removal-closed family generated from C by the sets F_j, with one uniform kappa_* there. For contour bounds one needs such a uniform constant for every connected target and its removal descendants. Restricting LC merely to connected sets remains insufficient because descendants disconnect. No unproved exterior-energy cancellation has entered this review.

Verdict: mathematical conditional theorem PASS. The sole material scientific obligation is LC for the actual interacting Hamiltonian; existing mean-density bounds do not discharge it. The all-edge traversal wording is a clarification, not a numerical or theorem correction.
