# Current all-u estimate status

The fixed-U ALL-ground-state theorem is not proved. The exact relative-energy variational reduction remains the target; the pointwise susceptibility difference bound is only one sufficient route and may be unnecessarily strong. Finite-volume level crossings can give distributional curvature of zero-temperature energies. The weaker sufficient statement is the integrated bound

 limsup_(beta->infinity) integral_0^U (U-u)[Chi_beta(u,s)-Chi_beta(u,0)]du
 <= C s(U/kappa)^2,

uniform in volume for fixed s=kappa/2 and0<U<=U*. It accommodates curvature measures without requiring pointwise differentiability of ground branches at nonzero U. No actual divergent native family is established.

New exact/provisional inputs obtained in this stretch:

* Symmetric ground-state local control from density plus an explicit diagonal-Clifford graph action; this is not every-ground-state control.
* Relative-impurity energy reduction and its local endpoint coefficient C(kappa/2)=71/(330kappa²), recovering19/(96kappa²) as s->0.
* Uniform leading coefficients of fixed joint-defect events, with minimal paths excluding zero-flux intermediate returns. This says nothing about a uniform remainder.
* Higher-odd nine-power soft spectral tail (independently reviewed by primary44c98f); stronger inverse moments than the generic cubic bound.
* Candidate higher-odd spatially summable singleton kernel via a one-sided inverse filter; pending independent review.
* Exact segmented-history thermal Holder bound, conditional on thermal partition stiffness. It controls wrong-flux durations but not connected spatial sums or the initial-flux normalization.

The remaining interacting expansion cannot replace Y with the full O on excited states. A bound on vacuum inverse vectors alone is not a bounded quasi-local operator inverse and does not control thermal matrix elements. Likewise a bounded higher-odd response kernel does not identify all sixth-order terms. The linear singleton return is naturally a resummation candidate, but its actual low-momentum form factor, other same-order terms and the interacting reference are not fixed by the current lemmas. These are the precise places where the relative impurity estimate still lacks a proof, rather than a reason to infer bulk instability.
