# Prospective smallest informative CT calibration

Design only; no stochastic launch or cost profile. Keep actual L2 component864, V=.95 and TOTAL T=.5/2. This is deliberately a short exact-reference calibration, not a continuation of old L8 tau36. The full-component integer oracle and rational truncation bounds are now frozen separately from any future timing data.

Use two initial families: constant seed, and the ideal CT auxiliary-count initializer conditioned on k<=K with the explicit TV bound. The latter must be called bounded-truncation stationary-start, not exact CT or machine-exact. Its actual random-bit, time-rounding and numerical conditional implementation needs independent review. It can distinguish corruption of a nearly stationary law from failure of a seed path to relax; agreement of two nonstationary starts alone would not do so.

Provisional coverage, NOT finalized before a separate cost forecast: two T values x two starts x two burns16/64 nominal spatial sweeps,16 independent chains per arm,128 measured sweeps. Each sweep means24 independently uniformly selected geometric FACE BLOCKS; one block is not a sweep, and a random sweep need not visit every face. Measure once after a completed sweep independently of whether anything changed. Retain128 raw physical vectors,16 within-chain batches, selected-face histogram, unchanged-block counts, selected-event counts and event-budget failures. Batches are not additional replicas.

The proposed physical vector is the ten raw oracle observables plus two bounded order signals: midpoint Nf,X,X²; endpoint average h and cross product; Xh; all-time average Nf; endpoint spin overlap; physical-event count and Hamming activity per time; electric corner intensity and plane anisotropy. D,C,R and signed VarH/VarX use joint chain covariance. Initial reptation tags have no meaning after an interior overwrite; do not use their disappearance as a mixing statistic.

Prospective decision menu: every fixed primary mean and D/R gets nominal4SE oracle consistency with the tiny deterministic target interval included. NF,X,time-average NF and activity get4SE<=15% of their positive target. Endpoint overlap and bounded order quantities get absolute4SE<=.05. Correction and signed variances remain resolution diagnostics; negative estimates are preserved and do not trigger clipping. Record all multiplicity and nominal-coverage limitations. Require every face selected at least once over each measured chain; missing coverage is a failure, not grounds for corrective extra visits.

Long-burn seed versus bounded-stationary comparisons use independent-chain covariance; within-chain halves use paired differences. A long-burn precision/consistency failure, stationary-start consistency failure, >4SE long-burn start/half contrast, integrity failure, event-budget failure or resource failure closes the fixed calibration. Short-burn failures remain reported. Unflagged comparisons do not prove stationarity. No replacement chains, extra seeds, increased burn or favorable-observable selection follows data.

Before freezing those counts, obtain a separately authorized bounded end-to-end COST fixture including actual count/path initialization, CT conditional messages and event recursion, all12 measurements, raw storage/checkpoints, startup and outer timing. Choose fixed fixture coverage before measuring; price variable event-count work and event-budget failures honestly. These are proposed gates, not an authorization or a current feasibility claim. If coverage cannot fit, stop at cost and redesign before any physical calibration, preserving this proposal.

No cost can yet be inferred from the exact-oracle runtime or the failed finite-G L8 throughput profile. A CT implementation might avoid long artificial self-slot scans but can still have slow spatial mixing and expensive event reconstruction. Its performance must be measured. Passing this L2 study would support only the chosen short-time finite component and would not authorize L4/L8, a ground-state extrapolation or a phase claim.
