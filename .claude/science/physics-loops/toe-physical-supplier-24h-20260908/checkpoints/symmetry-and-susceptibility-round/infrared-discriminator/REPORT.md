# Proposed new discriminator: inverse moment plus finite-size source scaling

This is a research protocol, not a photon/gap claim. The new useful measurement is the curvature of ground energy under a **linear** Cartesian source. It differs from the currently developed quadratic-source first derivative supplying the equal-time structure factor. No new stochastic production or component enumeration was run here.

## Exact finite-component target

Fix a connected component, H(V)=V Nf−A and its simple positive ground vector. For each declared transverse complex source O, use the two Hermitian diagonal sources F=Re O and Im O. Perturb H by ξF. Finite-dimensional perturbation theory gives −E_F''(0)=2 sum_{n>0}|<n|F|0>|²/(E_n−E_0). Pool all six orientations and both real components before normalizing. Let S_c=sum_F(<F²>−<F>²)>0. The existing kinetic sum rule supplies m1; the new curvature supplies m−1=sum_F[−E_F''(0)]/(2S_c). This is the connected spectral probability measure. A quadratic-source derivative alone supplies uncentered S; mixed origin means cannot remove its elastic contribution. Obtain pure means from linear first derivatives or prove the relevant component symmetry.

Set a=m1, b=1/m−1, C=a/b and ω*=sqrt(ab). Then Δ_supported≤b≤a. Neither bound is a lower bound. By Cauchy–Schwarz C≥1; equality characterizes one positive spectral energy. More quantitatively, for any r>1 the fraction outside [ω*/r,rω*] is at most 2(sqrt(C)−1)/(r+r^−1−2), capped at1. Proof: integrate x+x^−1−2 with x=ω/ω*, then Markov. This gives a source-weight concentration test rather than a spectral-gap certificate. The measures (1−ε³)δ_1+ε³δ_ε have C→1 but lowest support→0: no finite precision moment concentration excludes an arbitrarily weak soft tail.

Thus zero flux does not select an ensemble or prove ergodicity. Its supplied H=−A is also different from H(V)=VNf−A; its numerical gaps cannot be inserted into this protocol. The primary microscopic precedent is Hermele–Fisher–Balents, Physical Review B69,064404 (2004), https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0305401 and https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.69.064404 . It studies the related cubic-link model and identifies a U(1) phase with linear photons adjacent to the special RK point. This motivates, but does not validate, the present finite-component estimator or determine that V=.95 is inside the asymptotic phase. The continuum expectation that RK has vanishing electric stiffness is an imported effective-theory comparison; supplied dynamics, probability, carrier placement and source readout remain imports.

Highest-value next step is therefore the bounded linear-curvature calibration, not another937-component census or a fitted exponent from the existing two L4 moment values. It can either yield an accessible inverse moment or decisively show that its energy-noise amplification is too expensive under the current resource cap.
