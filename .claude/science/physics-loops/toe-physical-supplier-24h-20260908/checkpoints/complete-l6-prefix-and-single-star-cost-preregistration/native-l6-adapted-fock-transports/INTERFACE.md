# Unexecuted physical candidate transport

`transport.apply(vector, symmetry_index, parity)` accepts a finite float64 or complex128 array of length2^20 and returns a new array. No physical-length call has been executed. Entries are indexed by the lower20 occupation bits; bit20 is determined by total parity. Mode order is A,E1,E2,T1,T2,T3 in lambda12,24,36, then A,E1,E2 in lambda48. The vacuum phase is exactly the vacuum-fixed exterior representation.

`blocks(index)` returns exact normalized Eg entries as Fraction pairs(a,b) for a+b sqrt3, the eight-entry T-triplet signed exterior table, and determinant ±1. `BLOCKS.json` additionally retains unscaled rational Eg matrices. `FRAME.json` records frequencies and projected Gram norms from the frozen adapted geometry. Together these provide the normalized/unscaled conversion in Q(i,sqrt2,sqrt3); this package does not perform an unproved old-frame conversion.

Three ordinary Eg pairs use0-sector identity,1-sector R,2-sector detR. The final Eg pair spans bits19 and20; `_last_eg` partitions lower19-bit parity and explicitly reverses storage order for its one-particle pair. Three T triplets use local8-entry signed tables; no21-bit permutation table or dense Givens sequence is used. The parity mask is computed with19 bitwise passes on2^19 indices. Storage remains O(2^20); complex candidates may require multiple vector-sized temporary arrays. No cost or memory success for a physical call is asserted before its separate frozen pilot.

Controls execute exact Q(sqrt3)32-dimensional CAR intertwiners and all48x48 group products, vacuum fixing, exact integer8-dimensional triplet CAR checks, and tiny actual candidate kernel actions including both top-bit parities. The latter compare binary64 arithmetic to exact matrices converted for comparison at tolerance1e-12; they are not exact-floating claims. A missing determinant mutation changes the exterior action and is detected. Controls never call `apply` on the physical vector size. Total2690 primary plus192 supplemental predicates pass. The first control source/inputs were frozen before execution in PRE_FREEZE; supplementary controls are separately visible. Original geometry and original physical pilots are untouched.

Transport is a candidate-generation numerical operation. Certified residual arithmetic must still evaluate the resulting candidate independently; neither binary64 block rounding nor a symmetry transport alone certifies a physical solve. Stabilizer-induced ambiguity in approximate sources must be included in residual/error accounting. The analytic exact two-first/two-second reduction is established in the preceding adapted-transports proof.
