# Proper-prefix exact energy commutant in a connected CAR chain

Under the frozen assumptions, every supported energy-commuting operator is identity on matter. Precisely, on the full Fock space of a finite open chain with every nearest-neighbor hopping nonzero, let O belong to the even CAR algebra of a proper initial segment A={1,...,m}, tensored with an arbitrary finite battery. If [O,H_chain+H_B]=0 as a full operator identity, then O=I_matter tensor B and [B,H_B]=0.

## Proof on the frozen domain

Choose the fixed Jordan-Wigner representation ordered along the chain. An even operator on the initial segment acts only on its first m tensor factors (and battery); it is identity on the remaining factors. The real hopping becomes t_j(X_j X_(j+1)+Y_j Y_(j+1))/2, with a sign convention immaterial to the nonzero-coefficient argument.

Terms in H entirely outside the segment commute with O. The commutator therefore consists of an exterior-identity term [O,H_A+H_B] and the boundary terms

    (t_m/2)[O,X_m] tensor X_(m+1)
    +(t_m/2)[O,Y_m] tensor Y_(m+1).

All more distant exterior factors are identity in this equation. The exterior matrices I,X_(m+1),Y_(m+1) are linearly independent, so their coefficients vanish separately. As t_m is nonzero, O commutes with both X_m and Y_m. The full matrix algebra on site m is generated by those matrices; its commutant implies O=O' tensor I_m, with battery factors included in O'. This is an exact finite matrix-algebra statement, not a state expectation argument.

Now site m is exterior to O'. Isolating its X_m,Y_m coefficients using the live bond t_(m-1) gives identity on site m-1. Repeating removes every matter site. The remaining exterior-identity coefficient is [B,H_B]=0. This proves the theorem. It covers non-Hermitian O as well: real Hermitian and imaginary Hermitian parts separately commute with the Hermitian total energy and preserve the even/local domain.

The native reviewer independently derived the same result directly with CAR parity strings: the boundary coefficients are c_m† P_A and P_A c_m; even O commutes P_A, so exterior creation/annihilation independence forces commutation with c_m,c_m† and recursively factors off the last mode. This confirms that the chosen spin representation has not silently enlarged the frozen CAR support claim.

## Exact frozen tests and contrasts

The runner uses M=4, three unit hoppings and positive four-level battery1/2,3/2,5/2,7/2. It spans the entire even Hermitian prefix Pauli space tensor all battery Pauli words and solves the FULL sum-level commutator equations by sparse rational elimination. It substitutes every returned basis into the original equations, and emits all exact bases and canonical hashes. No fixed-N projection occurs.

The proper prefixes m=1,2,3 all have nullity4 and all basis words are identity on matter; their columns/ranks are respectively32/28,128/124 and512/508. This equals the diagonal commutant of the nondegenerate battery. Full support m=4 and each preregistered severed-boundary contrast permit nonidentity matter operators. These are scope checks, not substitute fixtures. The nonzero hopping on the m=2 segment fails global energy commutation and succeeds when its connecting bond is severed. For severed m=1, the nontrivial local number operator is available; there is no internal hopping to use as a control. All four battery spectral projectors pass.

## Quantifiers and limits

The theorem concerns strict support and exact global operator commutation in a fixed connected hopping Hamiltonian. It does not apply to a selected-input orbit that happens to conserve energy, to fuel-switched Hamiltonians, to a map between different matter Hamiltonians, or to disconnected regions. It is not an assertion that local energy exchange in general is impossible. An approximate energy lift can have small but nonzero commutator/error and is compatible with the earlier native quench bounds. A general graph requires an actual boundary/zero-forcing argument; a chain result alone does not provide it.

The proof also gives a separately identified analytic extension to arbitrary spin-tensor operators on an initial segment and onsite Z fields: the same boundary coefficient isolation and inward stripping work. This extension is not substituted for the frozen even-CAR numerical ansatz, and no native fuel or axiomatic interpretation is inferred from it. Arbitrary battery degeneracies merely enlarge the final battery-only commutant to operators commuting with H_B; they do not permit matter action under these hypotheses.
