# Goal and target contract

Execute the complete native reduced-cell sign/refusal isometry using a supplied finite autonomous Hamiltonian; quantify its completion window, energy, support and recurrence. Separately test exact global energy commutation for strictly supported matter/battery operators on a fixed connected chain. The frozen original contracts are autonomous-clock/PREREGISTRATION.md, autonomous-clock/NATIVE_PREREGISTRATION.md and boundary-commutant/PREREGISTRATION.md. This index summarizes them after completion and is not a new preregistration.
