# Boundaries and failed shortcuts

- Scalar corrections through five do not prove a nonzero sixth coefficient.
- One adjacent contribution or the internal bridge alone is incomplete; all six bridge supports are required.
- Same-color and distant bilinears cannot be omitted before proving their selection rules.
- A numerical residual without matrix/error bounds is not a certificate. The final rational replay does not rely on that shortcut.
- Dropping the unaffected vacuum offset, physical parity, closure sign or factor216 changes the answer.
- The finite L4 constant-square and signed-orbit conclusions do not automatically extend to larger tori.
- Leading excited multiplicities are not exact finite-u degeneracies, and an existential gap asymptotic is not a practical validity radius.

Failed synthetic decoder/control fixtures are preserved in originals; they were repaired before physical certificate execution and are not hidden as successful science.
