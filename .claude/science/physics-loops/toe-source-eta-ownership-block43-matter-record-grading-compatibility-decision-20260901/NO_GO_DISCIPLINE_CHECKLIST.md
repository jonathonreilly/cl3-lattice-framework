# No-go discipline checklist

## N1 — positive routes retained

1. global graded composition with one-site readout restricted to the fixed
   parity PVM and arbitrary-axis apparatus encoded on multiple modes;
2. matter-only graded composition with separate trivially graded Record
   carriers;
3. two-mode even logical encoding for arbitrary-axis measurements;
4. a rotation-covariant real Clifford carrier rather than complex `M_2(C)`
   site grading;
5. deferred composition, keeping ordinary and graded candidates conditional;
6. empirical physical identification of a framework carrier with known
   fermionic matter, followed by conditional calibration rather than
   deduction from the four axioms.

## N2 — independent walls

Local grading, cross-site product, carrier role, apparatus capacity,
readability, state, dynamics, matter functional, exchange-sign
identification, and Record probability calibration are separate inputs.

## N3 — hidden-premise scan

Same versus separate carrier, complex versus real algebra, inner versus
trivial grading, full versus code-space normalization, fixed versus
history-dependent roles, physical site count, parity of the writer,
cross-site product, local identification, and the meaning of readable must be
stated where used.

## N4 — residual matching

The six-projector pair changes readability while holding the local algebra
fixed. The ordinary/graded pair changes only cross-site composition while
holding local Record facts fixed. The commutant theorem targets capacity,
not statistics. The two-mode construction repairs readability, not the
selection of a matter law.

## N5 — rhetoric audit

No universal fermion no-go, impossibility of arbitrary measurement,
inconsistency of the four axioms, empirical exclusion, gravity conclusion,
or retained TOE closure is claimed.

## N6 — partial closure

Even if no clause is adopted, the cross-PR same-carrier architecture is
decided and the minimal two-mode/typed repair becomes an exact reusable
construction.

## N7 — steelman

A global graded law is viable if one accepts that one-site Record readout is
only the fixed parity PVM and implements arbitrary logical questions through
multi-mode even apparatus. A matter-only law is viable if role typing is
fundamental or dynamically supplied with a coherent history-indexed carrier
bundle. Either can be preferable to deferral once its migration cost and
physical identification are accepted.

## N8 — cross-cycle echo

PR #7827 supplies the arbitrary-axis writer conditionally; PR #7829 supplies
the two product candidates conditionally; PR #7830 supplies the even-algebra
classification conditionally. Block 43 intersects them without promoting
any open PR or declared hypothesis to governing status.
