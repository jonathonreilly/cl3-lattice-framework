# Five-role physics panel return

## Role 1 — foundations

The collision is carrier typing, not another probability formula. A fixed
nontrivial grading and arbitrary one-carrier rank-one readability cannot both
hold because the readable projectors span the algebra. Record permanence does
not choose which role receives the grading.

## Role 2 — quantum information

A single two-dimensional carrier has no commuting full-matrix complement.
The smallest natural arbitrary-axis parity-even logical qubit uses two
physical modes, with `span{|00>,|11>}` as an even code. The complete CP
instrument and complement behavior must be checked rather than inferred from
code-space projectors.

## Role 3 — lattice/QFT

Matter-only graded composition is algebraically cleaner than globally grading
Record carriers. It preserves CAR for matter while allowing full Record
matrices to remain even, but the distinction between matter and apparatus is
new physical typing and must be declared.

## Role 4 — phenomenology/experiment

Exchange interferometry can distinguish already identified bosons and
fermions, including with parity-even final readout. It cannot by itself
identify the framework's abstract matter carrier with the fermionic branch;
that remains a physical-identification premise.

## Role 5 — constructive mathematical physics

Run the exact span, commutant, code, Choi, and typed-product checks. Preserve
an ordinary-product twin with identical local Record facts. If it survives,
the strongest honest outcome is a minimal architectural decision, not a
derived statistics law.
