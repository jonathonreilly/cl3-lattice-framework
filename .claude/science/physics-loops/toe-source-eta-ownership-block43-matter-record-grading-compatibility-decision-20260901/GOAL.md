# Block 43 goal — matter/Record grading compatibility decision

## Highest-value question

Can one local `M_2(C)` carrier simultaneously support the arbitrary-axis
rank-one quantum Record events used by Block 38 and the fixed nontrivial
parity grading proposed for fermionic matter in PR #7829?

This is a cross-PR compatibility campaign, not another attempt to display a
conditional fermionic exchange sign. It must decide whether PR #7827's
one-mode random-axis writer, PR #7829's candidate graded matter product, and
PR #7830's parity-even readable algebra can inhabit the same carrier. If they
cannot, it must execute the smallest exact repair rather than merely name the
conflict.

## Proposed attack

1. Use six rank-one projectors on the Pauli axes to prove that arbitrary
   rank-one readable events span `M_2(C)`. Any grading fixing every such event
   is therefore the identity.
2. Execute the conflict for the literal `Ad(Z)` grading: the `X`- and
   `Y`-axis projectors used by a random-axis writer are not parity-even
   readable events.
3. Prove that one two-dimensional carrier cannot contain independent
   commuting full `M_2(C)` matter and Record algebras, because the commutant
   of an irreducible `M_2(C)` copy is scalar.
4. Build the positive repair: encode one logical qubit in the even two-mode
   code `span{|00>,|11>}`. Its three logical Pauli operators and every
   logical rank-one projector commute with total parity. Construct the full
   two-outcome effect family and an even normalized CP writer.
5. Execute a typed alternative in which matter factors carry the nontrivial
   grading while separate Record factors are trivially graded. Show that it
   is algebraically consistent but imports carrier roles and cross-site
   composition as physical law data.
6. Preserve ordinary-versus-graded current-axiom twins. Neither the repair
   nor a Record-visible exchange experiment is allowed to manufacture the
   missing matter-statistics selection premise.

## Hard impact gate

Block 43 earns a science package only if it proves all of the following:

1. a basis-level theorem rules out nontrivial fixed grading together with
   arbitrary rank-one one-carrier Record readability;
2. the conflict is reproduced on the literal Block-38 random-axis carrier,
   not inferred from terminology;
3. carrier separation inside one qubit is ruled out by the exact commutant;
4. a two-mode parity-even arbitrary-axis logical qubit, its effects, and a
   normalized CP Record writer are constructed and mutation-tested;
5. the typed matter/Record alternative is executed with honest dimension,
   locality, parity, and commutation accounting;
6. an explicit ordinary/graded twin prevents false derivation of CAR or the
   exchange sign from Record content alone;
7. the collision with PRs #7827, #7829, and #7830 is reduced to a concrete
   owner decision among global grading, matter-only grading with typed Record
   apparatus, and deferral; and
8. no axiom amendment, obligation retirement, or TOE percentage movement is
   claimed without explicit owner adoption and independent audit.
