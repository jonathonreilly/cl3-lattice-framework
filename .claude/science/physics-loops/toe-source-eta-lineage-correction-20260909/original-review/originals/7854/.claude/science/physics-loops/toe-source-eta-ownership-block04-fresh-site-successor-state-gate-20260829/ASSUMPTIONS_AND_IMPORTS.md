# Assumptions and Imports

## Load-bearing inputs

- The minimal-axiom lattice is `Z^3` with symmetric nearest-neighbor
  adjacency; Records are permanent and a state is a Record configuration.
- Block 03 supplies a one-event writer conditional on a target site and the
  six neighboring Record values.  It does not supply site, rate, or history.
- The active H1 orbit contains 24 of the 64 masks.

## Recomputed in this block

- the arbitrary-domain ready-set identity;
- pairwise nonadjacency and order independence of ready sites;
- invariance of every remaining ready eta;
- all 768 direct-target rows;
- 32 outer-shell completions per tuple and the 9,216 active-start census;
- the `3456/5760` active/inactive nominal-mask split;
- the five-neighbor propagating counterexample;
- independent arbitrary-graph exhaustion through six vertices.

## Explicitly not imported

- a formation site, selector, rate, clock, no-event channel, or event order;
- non-Record neighbor states or an initial-state distribution;
- completion of Block231, staged Block232, or any gravity branch;
- an audit verdict, axiom amendment, obligation retirement, or TOE movement.
