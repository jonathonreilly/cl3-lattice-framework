# Mutation Plan

The primary runner rejects 23 planted faults:

- six authority/registration drifts;
- asymmetric adjacency and five-neighbor scope changes;
- overwrite, wrong deletion, new-ready, and outcome-coupling errors;
- an occupied target called fresh;
- collapsed completion data or compatibility called reachability;
- hidden selector or synchronous front claims;
- erased live routes; and
- history, axiom, obligation, TOE, or retention overclaims.

The independent runner rejects 20 faults through a separately structured
arbitrary-graph and affine-mask implementation.  Its semantic mutants include
directed adjacency, degree-minus-one readiness, erased census, adjacent-ready
and new-ready claims, a one-completion shell, and the same accounting fences.

Every mutation must make at least one top-level check fail.  A survivor blocks
packaging.
