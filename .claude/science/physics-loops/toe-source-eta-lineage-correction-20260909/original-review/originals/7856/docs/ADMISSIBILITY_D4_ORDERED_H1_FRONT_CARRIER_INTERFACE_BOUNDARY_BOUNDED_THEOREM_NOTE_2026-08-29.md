## Why one M2 cannot carry the full instrument

Suppose the dual of a trace-preserving qubit compression were a unital CP map
`Phi*:M2 -> M32` and sent a Pauli triple `tau_i` to `D_i`.  Since both sides
are involutions,

```text
Phi*(tau_i^2) = I = Phi*(tau_i)^2.                     (6)
```

Equality in the CP Schwarz inequality places every `tau_i` in the
multiplicative domain.  Thus `Phi*` is a star-homomorphism on the M2 algebra
generated by the triple.  A Pauli triple in one M2 has scalar central volume,

