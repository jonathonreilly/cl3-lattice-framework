# Literature Bridges

No external literature is load-bearing. Standard finite-group
representations, Stinespring/Kraus dilation, QND instruments, Boolean Mobius
inversion, and reversible Boolean circuits are general machinery only.

The framework-specific burden is the exact join of the pinned affine eta,
native H1 source, Schur probability germ, and Record semantics. If an external
result becomes load-bearing, freeze its exact statement and source before use.
