and let `r(eta)` be either binary branch-odds convention for its exact local
probability germ.  Proper-cubic transport makes the active value one common

constant-field joint law does exist; ordered histories, enlarged carriers,
projective processes, and formation hazards also remain live.  Therefore this
theorem is neither a universal history no-go nor an axiom defect.

## The two-site compatibility equation

Take adjacent sites `x` and `y=x+d`.  Their other five neighbors are disjoint
on the cubic lattice and can be varied independently.  Let `a` and `b` be
their five-bit exterior environments.  Denote by `eta_x^v(a)` the mask at
`x` with its `d` bit set to `v`, and by `eta_y^v(b)` the mask at `y` with its
`bar(d)` bit set to `v`.

Starting from the same `(x,y)=(0,0)` endpoint, a positive joint weight can be
reconstructed by flipping `x` then `y`, or `y` then `x`.  Path independence
requires

```text
r(eta_x^0(a)) r(eta_y^1(b))
  = r(eta_y^0(b)) r(eta_x^1(a))                       (5)
```

for every `d,a,b`.  This is a necessary finite four-state identity.  It does
not assume translation-invariant boundary weights, thermodynamic limits, a
Hamiltonian, time, or a formation process.
