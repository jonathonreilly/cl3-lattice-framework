The trace fails if the block merely specializes `lambda=kappa=1`, reruns a
known controlled-copy dilation, or assumes that a pointer label is a Record.

law, the Record-only neighbor map and adaptive calibration are exact, and the
remaining constitutional/physical input is reduced to a clearly named clause.
