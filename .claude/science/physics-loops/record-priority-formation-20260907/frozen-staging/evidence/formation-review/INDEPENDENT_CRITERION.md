# Independent formation support criterion, before author derivation

Read primary formation-extension/PREREGISTRATION.md and finite checker, plus current MINIMAL_AXIOMS and monotone-formation parent in toe-dimension-campaign. The current Record wording locks one admissible possibility when present and requires permanence. It does not explicitly state that an old record must remain in the support of a newly evaluated distribution after neighbors form. Treat that ongoing-support reading as a separate stronger condition, not an automatic paraphrase of the axiom.

Let K_x(eta) be the supplied records-only nearest-neighbor distribution. Call eta ongoing-valid if every recorded site's content lies in K_x(eta)'s current support. A one-step append eta→eta+x:a preserves this property for every valid eta and every supported a at blank x iff, for every such triple and every already recorded neighbor y ofx,

eta_y ∈ supp K_y(eta+x:a).

The new site's support is checked before formation and its neighbor contents do not change during its own append. Nonneighbors' distributions do not change. Thus the displayed condition is necessary and sufficient. Its evaluation uses radius2 aroundx; the quantifier is over globally valid configurations, not arbitrary locally consistent patches which might lack a global extension. For a fixed initial state it may instead be restricted to reachable configurations. Imposing it on all supported outcomes prevents hidden selective filtering or renormalization.

Concrete six-menu counterexample: K is the common neighbor content's point mass when the recorded neighbor list is nonempty and unanimous, and is uniform on the six labels when empty or mixed. It is total and covariant under label permutations/cubic rotations. Put opposite labels0,1 at endpoints of a length-two path, with blank center and all other sites blank. Both endpoint records are initially supported by the empty-neighbor uniform rule. The center sees mixed labels and all six outcomes are supported. Any appended center labela forces each endpoint's conditional support to{a}; both old labels cannot remain supported. Thus there is no supported append at the only blank center preserving both old supports. This is an exact obstruction to universal liveness under the stronger reading, not an obstruction to at-formation recording and not a counterexample to the full-domain positive product rule.

For positive product weights the support is the full six-menu for every neighbor condition, so the stronger condition is automatic, including arbitrary pre-existing menu records. No old content is changed or sampled again.

Independent clock checks: iidcontinuous activation times have no ties a.s. Every decreasing ancestor path is self-avoiding. There are at most6·5^(n−1) lengthn paths from a site, and each specified path has decreasing priority probability1/(n+1)!. Union bounds imply finite ancestor radius a.s.; local finiteness/Konig's lemma then gives a finite dependency set. Pre-existing records terminate dependency branches and do not need activation marks. Outcome marks at a site are independent of its earlier-neighbor configuration because no earlier site's dependency can include the later site's marks. This justifies implementing the actual K without rejection filtering. The construction supplies a new clock/formation law; it is not derived from Admissibility, and the decreasing-path method is known probability machinery.

Every site forms at finite time almost surely, but there is no finite global completion time on an infinite blank lattice. A fixed noninvariant pre-existing background prevents translation-invariance of its conditional output law; covariance means transforming that background together with lattice and labels. Finite open-square order mixtures are separately defined laws, not automatic restrictions of the infinite-clock process.
