# A well-defined real scalar node target

Provisional independent derivation, before numerical evaluation. All hypotheses are those of the frozen infinite creator theorem and the actual90-term native star vertex. The observations below do not determine whether the scalar is zero.

## 1. Removing vacuum-creator ambiguity at the node

The creator Y_v is not unique, and its complex Majorana coefficients c_vj are not unique away from a node after acting on the Gaussian vacuum. Nevertheless the value at the Dirac node of a smooth coefficient-symbol matrix is unique. Indeed two representations of the same one-particle transition differ by a smooth matrix D(k) with P_+(k)D(k)=0 almost everywhere (transpose conventions merely exchange row and column). In the doubled 2x2x2 magnetic cell the canonical one-particle symbol, in a smooth gauge equal to identity at k=0, is

 h(k)=4t sum_a sin(k_a/2) Gamma_a,
 Gamma_a=(product_{b<a} Z_b) X_a.

The three Hermitian real Gamma matrices anticommute and square to identity. Along opposite rays k=r n and k=-r n, their positive-frequency limiting projectors are complementary. Smoothness of D therefore requires both complementary projections to annihilate D(0), hence D(0)=0. This argument does not assign a value to the discontinuous projector at zero. It only uses its directional limits and the smooth unprojected coefficient symbol supplied by the parent.

Consequently a node value extracted from a symmetry-covariant smooth creator is a property of the physical one-particle vacuum transition, not an artifact of the chosen inverse filter or null creation coefficients.

## 2. Magnetic translations and site-centered reflections

In the infinite canonical gauge eta_a(r)=(-1)^(sum_{b<a}r_b), a unit lattice translation in direction a is restored by the vertex sign

 g_a(r)=(-1)^(sum_{b>a}r_b).

Its action at reduced momentum zero on the eight cell sites s in {0,1}^3 is M_a=X_a product_{b>a}Z_b. A reflection r_a->-r_a is restored by g(r)=(-1)^r_a: reversing the oriented a-bond supplies the minus which this vertex gauge cancels. At k=0 the reflection leaves the cell parity s fixed, so its matrix is Z_a. Off-node cell phases do not change this zero-momentum statement.

These are physical signed graph symmetries of H and of its local pair-defect family, not merely symmetries of a scalar dispersion. They send gamma_v to its signed image and permute the disjoint-pair sum defining O_v. The canonical vacuum is invariant as a state. Therefore the node coefficient matrix C(0) intertwines the same representation on its input vertex and output Majorana indices.

Commutation with all three Z_a forces C(0) diagonal in the cell-site basis. Commutation with every M_a then equates diagonal entries differing in coordinate a. The cube is connected, so

 C(0)=alpha I_8.

No assumption that C(k) is a function of h(k), or of h(k)^2, has been made. This finite little-group argument fixes only the value at the node. It does not constrain the full momentum dependence to an onsite vertex.

## 3. Reality

The bipartite anti-linear CAR automorphism sends i->-i and gamma_r->epsilon_r gamma_r, epsilon_r=(-1)^(r_1+r_2+r_3). It preserves every real nearest-neighbor active Hamiltonian H_A and the canonical vacuum state. It sends the90-term O_v to epsilon_v O_v. The filtered creator may be chosen with the same covariance: conjugating its real filter reverses both real times, and changing integration variables restores its definition. Thus c_vj^*=epsilon_v epsilon_j c_vj. The diagonal cell entries are real, proving alpha is real.

Equivalently, combine anti-linear covariance with uniqueness of the smooth node value, avoiding dependence on the particular covariant creator construction.

## 4. Exact residual target

The scalar is the absolutely convergent sum alpha=sum_{r in 2Z^3} c_{0r}, in the cell convention just defined. Off-diagonal cell-parity sums vanish by the reflection constraints. The convergence follows from the parent's weighted l1 theorem. Computing a finite-volume total one-particle norm does not determine this signed sum, and nonzero higher-particle weight does not settle it.

Symmetry permits either alpha nonzero (the example O_v=gamma_v) or zero (the symmetry-covariant double commutator [H,[H,gamma_v]], whose one-particle symbol has an omega^2 factor). These examples are not substitutions for the native vertex. The unresolved task is to evaluate or bound the scalar for its actual defect-resolvent definition. A proof of alpha !=0 would make the directional projected form factor nonzero; alpha=0 would be a genuine native nodal cancellation, not merely a band-gauge convention.
