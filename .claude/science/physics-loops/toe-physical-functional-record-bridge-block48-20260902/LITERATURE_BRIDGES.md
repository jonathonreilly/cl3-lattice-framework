# Literature and theorem bridges

The mathematical ingredients are standard finite-dimensional facts:

- a normalized Gibbs density defines a positive state on `M_2(C)`;
- a normalized positive functional evaluated on a POVM gives a probability
  distribution;
- the density-matrix barycenter of a classical ensemble fixes all affine
  effect statistics but not the ensemble decomposition;
- an instrument contains post-outcome information not fixed by its POVM
  effects alone.

This block does not claim novelty for those facts individually. Its possible
repo value is the exact composition/independence map against the present
axioms and the physical `I-4`/Record obligations. Primary-source citation work
is required only if the final note makes a literature priority claim; none is
preregistered.
