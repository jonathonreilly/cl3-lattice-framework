# Preregistration

Frozen before theorem implementation or runner construction.

## Question

Does a fully specified finite physical action, together with the four current
axioms, determine the probabilities and physical contents of Records? If not,
what is the smallest exact missing identification?

## Primary hypotheses

1. A finite Hermitian `H`, inverse temperature `beta > 0`, and normalization
   convention uniquely determine `rho = exp(-beta H)/Tr exp(-beta H)` and the
   normalized positive functional `omega(E) = Tr(rho E)`.
2. This mathematical fact does not by itself select which effect partition is
   the registered physical question. One state can assign different outcome
   distributions to different PVMs.
3. A classical probability measure over candidate state/possibility objects is
   not the same typed object as a positive functional on quantum effects. A
   barycenter maps the first to the second, non-injectively.
4. Even after a functional and POVM are fixed, matching Record content requires
   an instrument/outcome identification; conditional content probabilities do
   not determine formation site, hazard/rate, cadence, or a permanence
   implementation.
5. The current axioms assert an Admissibility distribution and that Records
   lock supported possibilities, but do not literally identify that
   distribution with `omega(E)` for a registered effect partition.

## Falsifiers

- Kill hypothesis 1 if two normalized positive functionals arise from the same
  completely specified finite Gibbs package.
- Kill hypothesis 2 if the current axioms or supplied structure uniquely select
  a nontrivial PVM/POVM for every allowed state.
- Kill hypothesis 3 if probability measures over states and positive effect
  functionals are canonically bijective without an added quotient/registration.
- Kill hypothesis 4 if equal effects and outcome probabilities force matching
  Record contents, formation allocation, and persistence under the current
  axioms alone.
- Kill hypothesis 5 if exact current axiom text entails the event map and the
  equality of distributions in every model, rather than merely permitting it.

## Outcome classes

- `CLOSED_CURRENT_AXIOMS`: every identification is forced; eligible for a
  direct obligation-retirement assessment.
- `FINITE_TRIANGLE_PLUS_ONE_TERMINAL_IDENTIFICATION`: the mathematical chain is
  exact and only one physical equality/typing clause remains; produce an owner
  decision surface.
- `MULTIPLE_INDEPENDENT_WALLS`: explicit countermodels keep two or more
  physical choices independent; do not inflate progress.
- `DUPLICATE`: an exact current-main/open-PR source already owns the joined
  result; stop and redirect.

## Scope firewall

No claim that unobserved alternatives are directly measured. Only Record
frequencies are observable; probabilities and possibility support may be
inferred under stated repeatability, stationarity, and sampling assumptions.
No gravity work, axiom edit, audit verdict, PR landing, or review-loop use.
