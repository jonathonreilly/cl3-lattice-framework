# Axiom owner decision surface

No canonical text is changed by this packet.

## Decision exposed

The present wording uses one word, “possibility,” across objects that need not
have the same type. The owner must decide whether Admissibility is fundamentally
a measure over alternative local quantum states, a positive functional on
quantum effects, or a measure whose physically registered event partitions
push forward to outcome probabilities.

The user's stated ontology—possibilities are alternatives of the quantum state,
only Records are measured, and probability/support are inferred from repeated
Records—is most directly represented by the partition/pushforward route below.
It keeps state alternatives distinct from event effects and does not insert the
Born trace formula as an axiom.

## Recommended minimal package

### Qubit typing clarification

> Each site carries a one-site quantum system whose observable algebra is
> `M_2(C)`. Its local possibilities are alternative physical states of that
> quantum system; `M_2(C)` is the algebraic carrier, not an identification of
> arbitrary matrix elements with physical states, effects, or Records.

Before adoption, the owner should choose whether “alternative physical states”
means all normalized positive functionals/density matrices, pure rays only, or
the branch alternatives generated by a physical process. Those choices are not
mathematically equivalent.

### Admissibility event interface

> For each neighboring condition `eta`, Admissibility supplies a probability
> measure `mu_eta` on the local possibility space. Each physically registered
> local experiment `M` supplies a measurable partition
> `{A_eta(i|M)}` of those possibilities. Its outcome probabilities are the
> pushforward `p(i|eta,M)=mu_eta(A_eta(i|M))`. Physically equivalent
> registrations of the same event have the same probability, and splitting an
> event into disjoint registered subevents and recombining it preserves that
> probability by addition.

This says what “same event,” “refinement,” and “probability” mean. It does not
say two different events or two different experiments have the same
probability. It does not select which experiment is physically installed.

### Record/outcome link

> Conditional on a Record forming at the site under registered experiment `M`,
> its content determines exactly one outcome `i`, and the conditional
> distribution of that Record outcome is `p(i|eta,M)`.

This makes precise the existing non-governing reading note. It supplies no
formation site, opportunity, hazard/rate, clock, update law, typicality rule,
or physical persistence mechanism.

## Optional effect-functional route

If the owner intends Admissibility itself to be the quantum state functional,
the stronger alternative is:

> For each neighboring condition `eta`, Admissibility supplies a normalized
> positive functional `omega_eta` on `M_2(C)`. A physically registered finite
> effect resolution `{E_eta,i}` has probabilities
> `p(i|eta,M)=omega_eta(E_eta,i)`. Conditional on formation for that
> registration, Record content determines the matching outcome `i`.

This immediately yields a unique density matrix in finite dimension. It adds
more structure than the recommended package and changes “distribution over
possibilities” into a functional on events.

## Conservative no-edit route

Keep the four axioms unchanged and require every candidate physical completion
to derive:

1. the typed possibility space or positive functional;
2. the physical experiment/event registration;
3. the equality of the action-induced event law and the Admissibility law;
4. the matching Record instrument; and
5. a separate formation-history kernel and persistence implementation.

This is formally safest but leaves the current probability/Record lane
conditional. The Block 48 countermodels show that those links cannot be
treated as literal consequences of the current governing sentences.

## Recommendation

Adopt no edit until the owner chooses the meaning of “alternative physical
state.” If the intended meaning is confirmed, prefer the partition/pushforward
package plus the separate Record link. It matches the stated records-first
ontology, preserves the existing Admissibility measure, and leaves the Born
trace form available as a theorem after physical effect registration and
equivalence/refinement consistency are established.
