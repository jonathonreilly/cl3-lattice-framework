## Hostile objection and boundary

The strongest objection is that six target configurations in a Stinespring
table may be bookkeeping after an atomic radius-three writer, blank-sector
projector, selected center, Record sectors, and desired packet are supplied.
The panel accepts that objection as the scope boundary.  Success removes the
supplied oriented cap state but replaces it with a supplied unoriented
covariant block channel.  It does not provide a nearest-neighbor compilation,
overlap rule, occurrence event, rate, or clock.

Include a narrow model pair as a scope certificate: a correlated law uniform
over the six complete branches and a differently coupled law with the same
one-site marginals but a different probability of a valid joint cap/packet.
This shows only that the tested one-site conditionals do not determine the
joint writer.  It is not a general formation or dynamics no-go.

The axiom-update vote is `0--5`.  A positive is one compatible downstream
channel; nonuniqueness is not inconsistency, and other blocks, sequential
compilations, boundary routes, and orthogonal pointers remain live.
