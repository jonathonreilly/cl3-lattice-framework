# Preflight Witnesses

No Block-13 target runner, independent checker, result cache, or target
mutation was executed before this freeze.  These are design identities and
falsifiers that keep the registered target live; post-registration code must
rederive all of them.

## Portfolio decision

The Block-12 five-physicist panel unanimously ranked an oracle-free,
collision-safe local controller above hazard/clock and above another gravity
or static-carrier rung.  The majority retained the Block-12 straight-prefix
algebra conditionally; the minority refused microscopic promotion.  Both
positions agree that the occupied-destination test is the earliest decisive
next discriminator.

## Geometry-only front witness

For a straight Record trail

```text
..., x-3f, x-2f, x-f
```

the candidate `x` sees the unique predecessor at displacement `-f` and the
grand-predecessor at `-2f`.  A lateral candidate `x-f+e` would infer direction
`e`, but its required grand-predecessor `x-f-e` is absent in the isolated
trail.  Thus two collinear Record flags select the continuation without
inverting either nonorthogonal Record content.

This witness deliberately requires a two-Record seed.  It does not explain
how the seed forms or arbitrate a second active tip.

## Clear-branch witness

When all five destination flags vanish, the all-or-none controller reduces
exactly to the Block-12 five-SWAP layer.  For predecessor outcome `a` and
realized outcome `b`, the unchanged transported states have successor matrix

```text
F' = F + (r_(f,a)-r_(f,b)) f^T/2,
```

and satisfy

```text
2 F' f + r_(f,b) = 2 F f + r_(f,a),
F' n = F n for n perpendicular to f.
```

The runtime controller need not know `F` or `a`; these identities are proof
coordinates for the actual states it transports.

## Blocked-branch witness

If any destination flag is one, identity on all ten source/destination sites
leaves every pre-existing Record at its original site and leaves every live
source state uncopied and unchanged.  After writing the new Record at `x`, the
nominal next site `x+f` has:

- the new Record at its backward neighbor; and
- at least one additional Record among its forward/lateral five neighbors.

It therefore fails the unique-predecessor/five-live-neighbor eligibility test.
No terminal bit is needed, and retry cannot move the obstacle because the
nominal continuation is no longer eligible.

All 31 nonempty five-bit destination patterns have this same invariant.  The
single all-zero pattern is the clear branch.

## Normalization witness

The guard uses pre-event Record flags and not outcome `b`.  Conditional on
formation, the unchanged fourteen probabilities sum to one.  For a clear
pattern, all mass enters the transported successor histories.  For a blocked
pattern, all mass enters terminal histories `(b,STOP)`.  Hence adding `STOP`
does not renormalize or discard any branch.

## Effective-channel witness and named wall

For a fixed classical Record-flag sector, the clear map is a product of five
disjoint SWAP unitaries and the blocked map is identity.  Both are CPTP and
neither clones nor resets a quantum state.  The direct sum over classical
Record sectors is therefore a well-defined finite-radius effective channel.

This does not construct a microscopic circuit that coherently senses five
Record flags, computes the guard, controls all five gates once, and uncomputes
its ancilla.  That implementation remains named rather than silently imported.

## Principal risks frozen before execution

1. Treating Record presence as an effective classical sector may be acceptable
   law-level state typing but is not a derived quantum measurement channel.
2. The collinear eligibility predicate has radius two and belongs to the open
   formation-site rule, not the nearest-neighbor Admissibility distribution.
3. A single-active-tip event sequence avoids simultaneous overlap; a global
   multi-tip process still needs occurrence arbitration and rate/clock.
4. Safe termination closes permanence under static obstacles but does not
   guarantee indefinite continuation in every state.
5. The two-Record seed is supplied.

Any one may bound the physical interpretation.  None permits a same-cycle
change to the frozen controller, outcome law, Record code, or axioms.
