## Selected route

native `Z_e` projector as the permanent Record value. BKSF original-graph words
give the matter dictionary. After an event, exactly the corresponding hopping
term is deleted while the edge qubit remains fixed and continues to supply its
sign to neighboring original words.

This route has four linked proof layers:

1. remaining-cycle checks and old-Z signs give code dimension `2^(M-c)` and a
   faithful componentwise-even CAR representation with fixed component parity;
2. nonbridge events are fair surviving-state isometries, while bridge events
   are signed component-parity measurements;
3. total number is exact and uniform independent live-edge selection gives the
   stated ensemble energy moments;
4. one retained positive continuous battery lifts the history with exact total
   energy, while full initial nonbattery stationarity, initial battery independence
   and safe-cap support give an exact comparison for procedural-history and
   conditional energy distributions.

The finite physical witness uses even open cubic boxes and the maximum-degree
bound. Abstract finite 6-regular bipartite graphs remain an algebraic special
case and are not identified with finite open `Z^3` subgraphs.
