- one bounded theorem note under `docs/`;
- one exact primary runner under `scripts/`;
- one cached stdout under `logs/runner-cache/`;
- source-binding, mutation, panel, novelty, no-go, and trace checkpoints in
  this loop pack.

publication tables, lane registry, status board, README, minimal axioms, or
primitive registry. Do not open a PR unless the written postexecution value
