# Goal

Test the smallest strict-free action-to-Record bridge that can decide whether
one-site nearest-neighbor Record probabilities see the `K0/K1` flux bit.

The preregistered target has two joined surfaces:

1. derive the exact occupation full-conditionals of the zero-chemical-potential
   Gibbs state of a strict quadratic six-link fermion star, and determine
   whether they depend on link phases; and
2. apply one common preparation, action channel, two-outcome writer, and
   readout to the full eight-corner cube, where the `K0` and staggered `K1`
   link phases carry different plaquette flux.

The block is valuable only if it establishes a new structural distinction and
locates the exact bridge choice:

1. a strict-free incident-star exponential gives a varying local Record law
   that is phase blind, but those translated full conditionals are not thereby
   one global equilibrium specification;
2. an edge-factor diagonal functional gives a globally consistent positive
   nearest-neighbor Record law, but is not the exponential of the summed
   noncommuting quantum action; and
3. the honest loop-bearing global free quantum action is flux sensitive, with
   one coherent cell-scale Record transcript sharply separating the branches.

It does not select a physical action, derive the Gibbs/clock/writer bridge from
the four axioms, retire `P-KIN`, amend an axiom, or move a TOE percentage.
