- The graded finite Fock composition and the two candidate strict-free link
  families `K0` and `K1` are supplied mathematical completions; the four
  minimal axioms do not select them.
- Zero chemical potential, a positive inverse temperature, the finite
  incident-star Gibbs preparation, occupation preparation/conditioning, the
  cube cadence, and the target/complement writer are supplied protocol choices.
- Treating the incident-star result as one translated formation/update rule is
  supplied. It is not assumed to be the full conditional of one global quantum
  or classical equilibrium state.
- The identification of these conditional weights with the physical
  Admissibility distribution is tested as a compatible bridge, not derived.
- Record formation occurrence and later pointer decoupling or
  formation-triggered gating are supplied. Record supplies permanence as
  ontology, not its Hamiltonian implementation.
