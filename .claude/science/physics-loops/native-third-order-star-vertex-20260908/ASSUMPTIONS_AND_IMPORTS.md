# Assumptions And Imports

The full native CAR/Z2 dictionary, supplied uniform H0+uD, canonical L4 flux isolation, active vacuum, parity convention and finite-dimensional Feshbach algebra are inputs. No low-charge projector, physical parameter selection, all-active SW gap or bulk phase is imported. t=1 is the explicit control unit. Original source/proof hashes and raw vectors are in evidence and ORIGINAL_HASHES.json. The complete sixth coefficient cited in the historical proof is a comparator only, not an input to the singleton calculation.
