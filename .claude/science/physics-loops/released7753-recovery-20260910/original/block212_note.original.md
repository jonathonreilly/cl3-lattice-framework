## N4h — THE DERIVATION PATH, WITH ITS DEAD ENDS

**Dead end one — expecting the two-pin residual to be a pile-up of two pins.**
The block was written expecting the compounding to be uniform: two records, two
disturbances, a residual an order of magnitude above either. It is an order of
magnitude above **Block 202's landed** residual and above the slot-`2` one, but
it is **not** above the slot-`4` one — `200 ×` the excess is still below that
single pin's own norm, and one forward component is strictly smaller. The later
pin dominates. `break_compounding` exists as a mutation precisely because the
block is written as if the other answer had come back.

**Dead end two — reading `SOLVABLE` at `(4, 4)` as a result.** The single-readout
sixteen-weight system has sixteen unknowns and five equations. It **cannot** be
inconsistent for rank reasons alone, so its solvability establishes nothing
about weights and everything about the instrument's weakness at one readout. The
fix was to report it as a weakness and name the multi-context stack as the next
instrument rather than to quietly drop the measurement.

**Dead end three — expecting the contradiction direction to be
refinement-invariant.** This was the conjecture the block set out to confirm: one
canonical contradiction direction shared by every rung, a geometric object
underneath the whole exclusion series. The independent check refuted it, and the
refutation reproduces here exactly, down to the `7 mod 101` certificate. Two
weaker statements survive and are carried, and neither of them is what was
conjectured.

**Dead end four — a naive projector for the canonical residual.** The first
attempt formed `A^T A` and inverted it. `A` is rank-deficient at **every** rung
— ranks `4`, `8`, `12` against `4`, `12`, `20` columns — so the normal matrix is
singular and the fallback pseudo-inverse blew up on multi-thousand-digit
rationals. The fix is a **column basis**: the exact RREF pivot columns span
`col(A)` and have full column rank, so their normal matrix is invertible and the
projector is exact. That is disclosed as a dead end rather than smoothed.

**Dead end five — printing the residuals.** The two-pin residual components have
numerators past `1400` digits. Carrying eight of them in full would have made the
note unreadable without adding a single verdict. They are fingerprinted by exact
sign, exact zero sum, exact nonzeroness and digit count, and the one exact
rational small enough to be useful — the `292`-digit pin-order maximum — is
carried in full instead.

**What actually worked.** Running the parent's *named* instrument rather than an
adjacent one; asking the order question that a sequential construction forces;
sharing **one** environment cache across all three families so that Block 202's
landed residual and Block 210's landed rung are **re-measured** rather than
cited; measuring the joint-weight normalisation **before** reading a single
residual; measuring the compounding against **three** denominators instead of
the one that flattered it; and taking every rank through `DomainMatrix` over
`QQ_I`, which is what keeps a forty-five-environment baseline near half a
