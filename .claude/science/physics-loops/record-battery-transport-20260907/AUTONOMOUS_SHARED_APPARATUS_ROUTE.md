## Generator and resources

Use the candidate head/fuel placement in NEXT_FORMATION_ROUTE.md. Include a
live/dead latch at each edge, one head excitation, and a degenerate history
register. In a definite head/live-mask sector define the system Hamiltonian
H_R + Delta*(number of live fuels), with Delta=1 supplied initially. The
native nonbridge Record map moves the head, spends one fuel, deletes one
hopping term and appends its sharp native Record sign. For the first four
events on the cube every head trail is nonbridge. Its normalized CAR action
is identity in the common gauge. The generator on later sectors uses the
parent's FULL bridge/parity instrument; it does not continue fair isometries
past the nonbridge domain. The four-event cutoff is an analysis horizon,
not a stopping counter in the autonomous law.

Lift EACH branch map with the same continuous energy battery, now using
the Hamiltonians including fuel. The shift is a-b+Delta, where a,b are
the input and output matter energies. Let L_(v,w,z)=sqrt(gamma) times
this lifted branch Kraus map (its Record amplitude is 1/sqrt(2)). The
sum over signs has L*L=gamma P_v n_e on the reachable safe sector.
Thus a fixed GKSL generator has rate gamma times the live head degree;
edge choice is uniform and the waiting time is exponential, without a
port table or predetermined dwell durations. gamma and the GKSL law are
supplied, not derived from Admissibility.

Because the lifts intertwine the full system-plus-battery Hamiltonian,
[L,H_total]=0 on the direct sum of the correct sectors. Consequently
the adjoint GKSL generator annihilates every spectral function of
H_total on the reachable domain. This is stronger than the earlier
local dissipative fuel-budget identity: it directly preserves total
energy through an explicit shared apparatus. It is not yet a local
reservoir dilation or a closed finite autonomous unitary model.

For off-safe-sector completion, cap the combined two-sign instrument S
and add ONE refusal complement F*F=I-S*S. Completing each amplitude-weighted
sign map separately would double the desired edge rate and is incorrect.
Refusal retains the input Hamiltonian. All safe trajectories considered
here have zero refusal amplitude.

The actual event lift remains spectrally global. The head/edge proposal
has a local star description; that fact alone cannot make its energy
implementation local. The time-homogeneous dissipator also carries an
irreversibility/entropy sink assumption. Neither can be omitted from the
premise account or counted as retired merely because the event schedule
has been removed.

Independent review confirmed the algebra under these qualifications. A
