# Dwell convention and preregistered free-battery comparison

The primary baseline is frozen to the parent lift of the ideal dwell:
D_j tensor I_B. It is a supplied energy-conserving operation. This is
an apparatus phase/control premise, not uncontrolled laboratory free motion.
Root and the independent checker derived the distinction independently
before the free-battery comparison was executed.

For the separately specified laboratory comparison use
exp[-i d_j(H_(j-1)+E_B)] at each dwell and keep the same fixed event
lifts, width-one packet, phase pulse, edges, and dwell times. No parameter
is selected from an output. Energy intertwining implies

Wtilde_j exp[-i d_j Htotal_(j-1)] ... Wtilde_1 exp[-i d_1 Htotal_0]
= exp[-i t_j Htotal_j] Wtilde_j ... Wtilde_1,

where t_j is the sum of the supplied dwells. In the common CAR gauge
the normalized ideal Record maps are identity, so the product of lifts
is the lift of identity from H0 to Hj. For all matter observables and
battery-energy moments, tracing out the final free battery unitary gives
the same endpoint formula as the baseline with U_s=exp(-i t_j H_s).
This replacement is a derived expression for this separately defined
protocol, not a correction to the frozen baseline implementation.

At a j-minus surface use H_s=H_(j-1); at j-plus use H_s=H_j. The first
minus surface again agrees with the ideal first dwell. In the broad-packet
limit the free-battery matter state tends to exp(-i t_j H_s) rho0
exp(i t_j H_s), generally different from the ordered product of matter
dwells. Consequently widening alone does not imply recovery of the
original ordered-dwell target in this convention. No universal failure
claim is made: its own finite transport must be computed.

Comparison outputs will retain both protocol labels and all frozen gates.
No phase controller, clock, or free battery Hamiltonian is derived from
the framework axioms by either comparison.
