# Literature and attribution

BKSF encoding and energy-translation batteries are established techniques,
explicitly attributed in parent7983. They are not new contributions of this
campaign. Before relying on a literature theorem, read its primary source and
state its role. The first route rederives the finite operator specialization.

Primary-source attribution checked2026-09-07: Aberg, Catalytic Coherence,
arXiv1304.1060; Chiribella/Yang/Renner, Fundamental energy requirement of
reversible quantum operations, arXiv1908.10884. Their abstracts establish
the prior resource-theory context; no quantitative bound from either paper
is used as a premise here. This campaign must not claim the energy-translation
construction or a generic coherence-resource tradeoff as its discovery.
