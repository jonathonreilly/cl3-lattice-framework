## Result Up Front

The [current minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) define physical
states as Record configurations. The
[Block-64 theorem](ADMISSIBILITY_STRICT_NEAREST_NEIGHBOR_STATE_DEPENDENT_RECORD_BORN_HISTORY_SINGLE_FRONT_POSITIVE_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-08-12.md)
starts from a head Record whose Hermitian part is the complete density operator
`rho`. That is mathematically legitimate on its supplied front domain. It is
not yet a physical compiler from one live unknown quantum state: a quantum
channel is affine in its input density, while the deterministic classical map

```text
rho -> the exact Record label H(rho,R,m,p)
```

is not affine. For two distinct states `rho_0,rho_1`, it sends their equal
mixture to the single label `H((rho_0+rho_1)/2,...)`, whereas the equal mixture
of the outputs has two distinct labels. This narrow obstruction disappears if
`rho` is separately supplied as an ontic classical field, if tomography on a
many-copy ensemble is supplied, or if the carrier semantics are changed. None
of those extra inputs is in the current four axioms.

There is a more economical route. Measure the live coherent pointer/matter
system first. A realized branch writes only:

1. the first pure outcome Record `O(E_(m,h),h)`; and
2. the successor head `H(tau_(m,h),R,1-m,1-p)`.

Here `tau_(m,h)=E_(m,h)/Tr(E_(m,h))` is fixed by the selected effect and outcome,
not copied from the unknown input. The two sites are exactly `O_0` and `H_1`
of the Block-64 strip. Every formation branch therefore enters the unchanged
Block-64 local rule at its next forced relay. The runner continues each of the
three first branches through 63 additional events and checks the full
64-outcome cylinder weight exactly.

This closes a construction question, not the selection question. Menu 0 and
menu 1 give different exact matter-dependent distributions even though their
shared `E_0` produces the same literal Record. Formation hazards `f=1` and
`f=3/4` give different occurrence probabilities with the same conditional
content law. Both choices require extensional law data. The present axioms do
not choose either one.

## 1. Exact Live Input And Instrument

Let the local live Hilbert input be pointer qubit `P` tensor matter qubit `M`,
with arbitrary density operator `Omega` on `P tensor M`. This includes product,
coherent-pointer, classically correlated, entangled, and maximally mixed
inputs. It does not assume a clean pointer bit.

Write

```text
P_0=|0><0|,  P_1=|1><1|,
sum_h E_(m,h)=I_M,
0 <= f <= 1.
```

For a proper-cubic frame `R`, menu `m`, and formation hazard `f`, define the
four effects

```text
F_empty = P_0 tensor I_M + (1-f) P_1 tensor I_M,
F_h     = f P_1 tensor R(E_(m,h)),  h=0,1,2.
```

They are positive and sum exactly to `I_(P tensor M)`. Thus

```text
Pr(empty|Omega) = Tr(Omega F_empty),
Pr(h|Omega)     = Tr(Omega F_h)
```

is normalized for every joint density. The formation branch map is the
measure-and-prepare map

```text
Phi_h(Omega)=Tr(Omega F_h) R(tau_(m,h)).
```

Its Choi matrix is `F_h^T tensor R(tau_(m,h)) >= 0`. For the two exact hazards
used by the runner, `f=1` and `f=3/4`, the no-formation branch has the one-Kraus
operator

```text
K_empty=P_0 tensor I_M + sqrt(1-f) P_1 tensor I_M,
```

with `sqrt(1-f)=0` or `1/2`. Consequently the complete branch instrument is
CP and trace preserving. Formally, the no-Record and three formation outputs
occupy orthogonal classical output sectors: the former retains a joint live
system, while each latter sector prepares its fixed `tau_(m,h)` and carries
the classical branch label. Positivity of each branch plus
`F_empty+sum_h F_h=I` is therefore the direct-sum instrument criterion, even
though the live and formed sectors have different quantum dimensions. A
coherent pointer does not have to be collapsed to a supplied admission bit:
for pointer `|+>` and `f=3/4`, total formation weight is exactly `3/8`.
